Published by West End Games in 1996 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*, *Tales of the Jedi Companion* shifts the D6 campaign setting four millennia before the Battle of Yavin. Written by George R. Strayton and Eric S. Trautmann, the book adapts Dark Horse Comics' *Tales of the Jedi* storyline—covering the Beast Wars of Onderon, the Freedon Nadd Uprising, and the lead-up to the Great Sith War—into a playable Old Republic era.

Because the Jedi Order of 4,000 BBY operates openly across the galaxy and technology is noticeably more archaic than in the Galactic Civil War, the companion serves both as a historical setting guide and a rules expansion for Jedi-centered campaigns. Gamemasters receive era-specific guidelines for Force traditions, ancient starships, and political factions alongside ready-to-run material.

## Key Contents

- **Historical Overview**: Background on early hyperspace exploration, the Old Republic, the Beast Wars of Onderon, the saga of Nomi Sunrider, and the Freedon Nadd Uprising.
- **Character Profiles**: Full D6 statistics and biographies for prominent Jedi and era figures, including Ulic Qel-Droma, Nomi Sunrider, Arca Jeth, Cay Qel-Droma, Thon, Ood Bnar, and Tott Doneeta.
- **Expanded Force Rules**: Detailed coverage of Control, Sense, and Alter skills, sustaining and proximity modifiers, and ancient Jedi and dark side Sith Force powers.
- **Old Republic Technology & Gear**: Era-appropriate weapons, archaic lightsabers, armor, starships, and equipment reflecting the technological state of 4,000 BBY.
- **Adventures & Campaign Hooks**: Playable scenario material, including the solitaire adventure *Ruins of Kabus-Dabeh* and adventure seeds such as *The Muza Disappearances*.
