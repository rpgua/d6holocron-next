*Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope* (Second Edition), published by West End Games in 1995 (WEG 40124), is a revised and expanded update of Grant S. Boucher's landmark 1989 sourcebook, reworked for the *Second Edition* D6 ruleset by Michael Stern and Paul Sudlow. Retaining the in-universe framing of Rebel historian Voren Na'al's intelligence dossier on the events leading up to the Battle of Yavin, this edition refreshes all character, creature, and droid statistics to match 2E mechanics, including updated skill lists, scale rules, and Wild Die play.

In addition to mechanical updates, the 1995 release replaced the original edition's black-and-white illustrations with film stills from *Episode IV*, expanded coverage of equipment, vehicles, and starships, and introduced a brand-new introductory adventure chapter. It serves as an authoritative Second Edition reference for Gamemasters setting campaigns on Tatooine, aboard Imperial installations, or within the early Rebel Alliance.

## Key Contents

- **Tatooine Wilderness & Mos Eisley:** Updated 2E profiles for desert inhabitants, Tusken Raiders, Jawas, wildlife, and Mos Eisley cantina patrons, with expanded lore and species notes.
- **The Death Star & Yavin:** Revised statistics for Imperial commanders, stormtroopers, Rebel Alliance leadership, starfighter pilots, and key personnel from both sides of the Battle of Yavin.
- **The Heroes of Yavin:** Full Second Edition character dossiers for Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa, Han Solo, Chewbacca, C-3PO, R2-D2, and Obi-Wan Kenobi.
- **Expanded Hardware & Gear:** Added 2E statistics for notable vehicles, starships, droids, and personal equipment featured in *A New Hope*.
- **Chapter Six: *The Battle of Wayfar*:** A Second Edition-exclusive introductory adventure set on Tatooine, designed to launch starting player characters directly into the Galactic Civil War.
