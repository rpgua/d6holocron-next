*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 8* was published by West End Games in November 1995, edited by Peter M. Schweighofer for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Spanning both the Galactic Civil War and New Republic eras, the issue combines four Expanded Universe short stories—several later reprinted in the *Tales from the Empire* and *Tales from the New Republic* anthologies—with a multi-part espionage adventure and deep-lore alien species profiles.

The issue is particularly valuable to gamemasters running fringe, mercenary, or intelligence-focused campaigns. Alongside a complete three-part scenario pitting player characters against an Imperial advisor and rival mercenaries, it expands the galaxy's outer reaches with an in-depth look at the enigmatic Gree species and an illicit corporate arms syndicate.

## Key Contents
- **Short Fiction:** Includes Charlene Newcomb's *"A Certain Point of View"* (aboard the luxury liner *Kuari Princess* following Alderaan's destruction), Tony Russo's *"Blaze of Glory"*, Patricia A. Jackson's *"Uhl Eharl Khoehng"*, and Carolyn Golledge's *"Firepower"*.
- **Black Curs Blues:** A complete three-part roleplaying adventure by Peter M. Schweighofer involving New Republic Intelligence operatives, the Black Curs mercenary unit, and Imperial Advisor Bregius Golthan.
- **The Gree Enclave:** A detailed source article by Timothy S. O'Brien profiling the isolationist Gree species, their ancient technology, enclave space, and D6 character statistics.
- **The Yard of Opportunity:** An underworld source feature by Christopher M. Olson detailing the black-market arms network Syndicate One and its corporate front, Executive Results.
- **Recurring Departments:** Features *"Wanted by Cracken"* bounty dossiers, *"Galaxywide NewsNets"* by Paul Sudlow, and *"Scouts' Dispatch"* by Peter Woodworth.
