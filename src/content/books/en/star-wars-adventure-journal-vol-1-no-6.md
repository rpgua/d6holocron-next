*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 6* was published by West End Games in May 1995 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Continuing the quarterly anthology format, this issue blends Expanded Universe short fiction set across the Rebellion and New Republic eras with playable campaign locations, underworld lore, and recurring game columns designed for immediate table use.

Gamemasters gain ready-to-use criminal profiles, frontier scouting reports, and in-universe news feeds alongside mechanical expansions for swoop racing gangs and fringe hideouts. Several short stories in this issue continue serialized Expanded Universe arcs, including the ongoing adventures of Alexandra Winger on Garos IV and Tinian I'att's fugitives on Silver Station.

## Key Contents
- **Short Fiction:** Includes Kathy Tyers's *"To Fight Another Day"*, Charlene Newcomb's *"Rendezvous with Destiny"* (featuring Alexandra Winger and Luke Skywalker), and Laurie Burns's *"Ringers"*.
- **Swoop Gangs:** A source feature by Wayne Humfleet and John Beyer detailing swoop-bike culture, rival biker gangs, tactics, and D6 game statistics for fringe campaigns.
- **The Greel Wood Haven:** A location and adventure guide by C. Robert Carey providing maps, local factions, and scenario hooks for gamemasters.
- **Additional Features:** *"Finder's Fee"* by Peter Schweighofer and *"Kella Rand, Reporting..."*, continuing the exploits of the Galactic News Service journalist.
- **Recurring Departments:** Includes *"Wanted by Cracken"* (criminal dossiers for Rebel and New Republic Intelligence), *"Scouts' Dispatch"* by Peter Woodworth, and *"Galaxywide NewsNets"* by Paul Sudlow.
