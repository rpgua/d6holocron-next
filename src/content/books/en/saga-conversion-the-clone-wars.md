*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: The Clone Wars Campaign Guide* is a fan-compiled conversion supplement by The Rancor Pit community adapting Wizards of the Coast's *The Clone Wars Campaign Guide* (2009) from *Star Wars Saga Edition* to West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Because West End Games lost the *Star Wars* license prior to the prequel films, this conversion fills a major gap for D6 groups by translating Clone Wars species, Jedi abilities, military hardware, and faction rosters into classic D6 statistics.

Designed for use alongside the original *Saga Edition* sourcebook for setting lore and planetary gazetteers, it enables campaigns set throughout the galactic conflict between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

## Key Contents

- **Era Species**: D6 attribute blocks and special abilities for prominent Clone Wars species, including Dugs, Gen'Dai, Iktotchi, Kaleesh, Kaminoans, Kerkoids, Nautolans, Nelvaanians, and Vurks.
- **The Force & Jedi**: Converted Force powers, combat techniques, and non-Jedi traditions active during the war, such as the Bando Gora, the Believers, and the Korunnai, alongside stats for Jedi Order figures and craft.
- **Military Gear & Droids**: D6 statistics for clone trooper armor, Republic and Separatist weaponry, personal equipment, and battle droid models deployed across the conflict.
- **Vehicles & Starships**: Full D6 profiles for Republic, Separatist, and Jedi starfighters, gunships, bombers (including the *Scurrg* H-6 Prototype Bomber), walkers, and capital warships.
- **Faction Rosters & Mass Combat**: Converted NPC stat blocks for Republic High Command, clone units, and Separatist leadership, plus D6-compatible guidance for squad-level and large-scale military engagements.
