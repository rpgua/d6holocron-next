*Supernova* (1993, `WEG40066`) is a Second Edition adventure anthology and mini-campaign supplement published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the book centers on a looming astronomical catastrophe in the Demophon system, where the local sun is rapidly expanding into a red giant on the verge of going supernova.

While the Galactic Empire evacuates only select personnel and abandons populations it deems expendable, Rebel operatives must enter the destabilizing system to rescue endangered civilians, unravel local conspiracies, and extract vital Alliance assets before the star detonates. Opening with a comprehensive campaign overview of the Demophon system and its domed capital of Byrne City, the supplement unfolds across five linked adventures that can be played sequentially as a ticking-clock mini-campaign or adapted as standalone scenarios.

## Key Contents
- **Demophon System Gazetteer:** Background sourcebook section detailing the doomed star, planetary environments, Imperial presence, and the domed metropolis of Byrne City.
- **Five Linked Adventures:** Complete Second Edition scenarios—*"Infiltration"*, *"The Mynock Conspiracy"*, *"Triple Cross"*, *"The Evacuation of Jatee"*, and the climactic finale *"The Beginning of the End"*.
- **Regional Threats & Mysteries:** Encounters involving an Imperial Security prison break, a deadly viral outbreak codenamed "Mynock," double-crossing factions, and desperate mass evacuations.
- **New Alien Species & Creatures:** Second Edition D6 profiles for local inhabitants and hazards, including the Ssithers and the monstrous Iagoin.
- **Campaign Timeline & Aids:** Escalating stellar-disaster effects, NPC dossiers, and hooks for integrating the Demophon crisis into an existing Rebel campaign.
