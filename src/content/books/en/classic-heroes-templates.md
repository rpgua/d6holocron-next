*Classic Heroes Templates* is a fan-created character compendium for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* designed in the classic West End Games layout and used widely alongside the community-compiled *Revised, Expanded, and Updated* (REUP) edition. Serving as a companion to the *Classic Heroes* optional rules supplement, it streamlines character creation by offering ready-to-play archetypes drawn from the Original Trilogy, Expanded Universe lore, and broader galactic society.

Built for players and gamemasters who want fast table setup without sacrificing variety, the collection goes far beyond standard core-book templates. Each sheet follows the traditional WEG D6 format—complete with attribute allocations, starting equipment, background hooks, and personality notes—making it equally useful for quick player onboarding or instant NPC generation.

## Key Contents
- **Force-Users & Mystics:** Ready-to-play templates covering unconventional Force traditions and Jedi archetypes, such as Alien Student of the Force, Failed Jedi, Jedi Consular, and Jedi Guardian.
- **Underworld & Fringe:** Expanded scoundrel and criminal concepts including specialized Bounty Hunters, Classy Smugglers, Devaronian Grifters, Spice Runners, and Arms Merchants.
- **Military & Imperial Archetypes:** Combat and espionage templates spanning Ex-Imperial Commandos, Alliance Agitators, CompForce Assault Troopers, House Guard Officers, and ISB Agents.
- **Distinctive Alien & Cultural Backgrounds:** Species-focused templates featuring Ewok warriors and shamans, Barabel shockboxers, Ithorian storytellers, Jawa traders, and Squib scavengers.
- **REUP & Classic Compatibility:** Formatted to integrate smoothly with standard 2E/REUP attribute rules as well as the optional Boons and Banes customization system from *Classic Heroes*.
