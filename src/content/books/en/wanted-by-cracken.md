*Wanted by Cracken* (1993, `WEG40062`) is a 96-page NPC and villain sourcebook written by Louis J. Prosperi and published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Framed as a classified intelligence briefing compiled around 7 ABY by General Airen Cracken, Supreme Commander of New Republic Intelligence, the book serves as the New Republic's galactic "Most Wanted" list for operatives, bounty hunters, and Gamemasters.

Profiling more than fifty distinct adversaries operating during the turbulent post-Endor era, the supplement was so well received that West End Games later spun off a recurring "Wanted by Cracken" feature in the *Star Wars Adventure Journal*. Each dossier is designed to drop directly into a campaign as a recurring nemesis, one-shot bounty target, or faction leader.

## Key Contents
- **Structured Intelligence Dossiers:** Every entry pairs an in-universe New Republic Intelligence report (including official credit bounties, known aliases, and sometimes flawed intelligence) with a separate Gamemaster section revealing the target's true background and motives.
- **Imperial Remnant & Warlords:** Profiles of rogue Imperial officers, governors, and die-hard loyalists waging guerrilla campaigns or carving out petty fiefdoms against the fledgling New Republic.
- **Underworld Kingpins & Pirates:** Smugglers, spice cartels, slavers, and pirate captains exploiting the post-Endor power vacuum across the Outer Rim and Mid Rim.
- **Assassins, Spies & Bounty Hunters:** Mercenaries, saboteurs, double agents, and lethal independent hunters available as rivals or hired threats.
- **Rogue Droids & Support Assets:** Complete Second Edition D6 statistics for malfunctioning or modified criminal droids, along with custom starships, gear, and minion profiles tied to key targets.
