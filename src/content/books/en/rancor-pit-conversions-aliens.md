*Rancor Pit Conversions: Aliens* (`rp_aliens`) is a community-produced fan supplement compiled by contributors on The Rancor Pit forums for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Following the end of West End Games' license in 1998, The Rancor Pit emerged as a central hub for keeping the classic D6 system current with the expanding *Star Wars* galaxy, translating material from the Prequel Trilogy, *The Clone Wars*, Expanded Universe fiction, and Wizards of the Coast's d20 and Saga Edition sourcebooks.

This volume focuses specifically on sentient alien species, giving D6 players and gamemasters standardized racial templates that were never covered in official West End Games releases or that required mechanical updates for 2E-RE campaigns across any galactic era.

## Key Contents

- **Playable Species Templates:** Standardized D6 attribute minimums and maximums (Dexterity, Knowledge, Mechanical, Perception, Strength, Technical) for dozens of converted alien races.
- **Special Abilities & Story Factors:** Mechanical translations of species traits—such as natural armor, low-light vision, aquatic adaptation, pheromones, or cultural reputations—calibrated for D6 balance.
- **Lore & Biology Summaries:** Concise entries covering homeworlds, physical characteristics, average lifespans, and typical galactic roles for each species.
- **Cross-Era Compatibility:** Species spanning the Old Republic, Rise of the Empire, Galactic Civil War, and New Republic eras, formatted for immediate drop-in use with WEG 2E-RE rules.
