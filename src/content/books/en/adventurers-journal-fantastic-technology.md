*Adventurer's Journal: Fantastic Technology* (subtitled *Professional Gadgets & Personal Gear*, product code `AJ-FT-1-FALL19`) is a fan-produced equipment supplement published in October 2019 by the *Adventurer's Journal* community project for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP). Modeled on West End Games' classic *Galladinium's Fantastic Technology* and *Gundark's Fantastic Technology* sourcebooks, this special issue focuses on everyday civilian devices, specialized professional tools, and personal field gear suitable for campaigns across any era of the galaxy.

Rather than focusing exclusively on military hardware, the supplement broadens character loadouts with practical hardware—ranging from single-task droids, domestic automatons, and security bypass tools to customized personal equipment—paired with short adventures, location hooks, and technical commentary written in the traditional in-universe holodoc style.

## Key Contents
- **Personal Gear & Tech Specs:** Extensive catalog entries with full D6 / REUP game statistics for civilian gadgets, security gear, and specialized personal equipment, including *Personal Gear STAT!* entries such as *Two Five* and *Acid Rain*.
- **Featured Articles & Mechanics:** Rules and setting essays including *Adventuring in the Docking Bay* by Jason Dray, *Stock From The Factory* by Charles McNeill, *On-Board Diagnostics*, and Ray Napoli's *Of Vice and Men* and *Curious Coverts: Persephone*.
- **Solo Adventure:** *And No Questions*, a solitaire scenario by Mark Dowson featuring the character IF-121 "Izi".
- **Galactic Gazetteer & Player Aids:** Setting notes on Paladez alongside a ready-to-use blank character sheet formatted for D6 / REUP play.
