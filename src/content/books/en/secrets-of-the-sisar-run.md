*Shadows of the Empire: Secrets of the Sisar Run* (1997, `WEG40136`) is a campaign sourcebook published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Authored by Craig Robert Carey, Shane Lacy Hensley, and Pablo Hidalgo as a tie-in to the *Shadows of the Empire* multimedia project, the book is set in the Outer Rim between *The Empire Strikes Back* and *Return of the Jedi*.

Its primary focus is the Sisar Run, a hazardous hyperspace corridor linking the Perlemian Trade Route to the borderlands of Hutt Space. Controlled largely by the Black Sun crime syndicate under Prince Xizor and his regional vigo Sprax, the route serves as a prime arena for smugglers, spice runners, pirates, and undercover operatives navigating a volatile web of syndicate politics and Imperial corruption.

## Key Contents
- **Into the Shadows & The Sisar Run:** Regional history, astrogation data, and detailed planetary profiles for key stops along the hyperlane, including shadowports, spice-processing worlds, and frontier outposts.
- **Running the Sisar:** Practical mechanics and operational guidance for smuggling campaigns, covering contraband trade, customs evasion, hyperspace hazards, and underworld contacts.
- **A Black Sun Campaign:** Gamemaster advice for structuring criminal or undercover campaigns tied to Black Sun's hierarchy, rival syndicates, and the power struggles within Xizor's organization.
- **The Baroni Conspiracy:** A full three-part campaign adventure (*Hunting the Hunter*, *Sedri Intrigue*, and *Codebreaker*) plunging player characters into a multi-system conspiracy involving Black Sun agents, bounty hunters, and rival factions.
- **NPCs, Vessels & Gear:** Complete D6 game statistics for syndicate enforcers, local bosses, custom freighters, and specialized smuggling equipment.
