*Otherspace* (1989, `WEG40018`) is an official First Edition adventure module for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, published by West End Games during the Galactic Civil War era. Blending space opera with survival horror, the scenario removes Rebel player characters from familiar Imperial entanglements and strands them in an eerie pocket dimension existing outside normal realspace and standard hyperspace lanes.

The adventure begins when a Rebel prisoner transport, the *Celestial*, suffers a catastrophic hyperdrive malfunction and slips into Otherspace—a starless, lightless void where vessels lost across the galaxy accumulate in a derelict graveyard. Trapped alongside vanished Imperial and Rebel ships, the heroes must navigate zero-visibility hazards and uncover a way back to realspace before falling prey to the dimension's native inhabitants: the Charon, an aggressive, arachnid-like species driven by a nihilistic death cult that views all other life as an abomination. At the heart of the void drifts *The Desolate*, a massive, biologically engineered Charon starship spanning more than 450 meters. *Otherspace* later spawned a direct sequel, *Otherspace II: Invasion* (1989).

## Key Contents
- **Pocket Dimension Setting:** Rules, environmental hazards, and navigation challenges for running encounters inside the lightless void of Otherspace.
- **The Charon Species:** Background lore, cultural overview, and complete First Edition D6 statistics for the four-armed, four-legged Charon warriors and cultists.
- **Starship Profiles:** Detailed game stats and deck notes for the Rebel transport *Celestial* and the bio-organic Charon vessel *The Desolate*.
- **Survival-Horror Structure:** Multi-episode scenario progression emphasizing investigation, uneasy alliances with stranded Imperials, and escape under time pressure.
- **Ready-to-Use Aids:** Gamemaster maps, non-player character dossiers, and pull-out handouts designed to support tabletop play.
