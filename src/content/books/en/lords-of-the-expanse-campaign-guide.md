The *Campaign Guide* is the third softcover booklet included in West End Games' 1997 *Lords of the Expanse* boxed set, authored by Paul Sudlow and Eric S. Trautmann for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (2E-RE). Designed specifically for gamemasters, it bridges the setting lore of the *Sector Guide* and the behind-the-scenes conspiracies of the *Gamemaster Guide* into an actionable sandbox campaign in the Tapani Sector during the Galactic Civil War.

The book focuses on how to structure ongoing storylines in a sector where open blaster fire often carries severe legal and political consequences. It guides gamemasters through pacing courtly intrigue, balancing noble house rivalries with Rebel and Imperial agendas, and building recurring locations that react to the player characters' growing reputation.

## Key Contents

- **Campaign Frameworks:** Practical advice for launching and sustaining campaigns centered on noble house retainers, independent Freeworlds traders, corporate troubleshooters, or covert Rebel cells.
- **Adventure Locations:** Detailed regional sites, estates, spaceports, and neutral meeting grounds ready to drop into ongoing Tapani Sector play.
- **Introductory Adventure (*"Hunting Grounds"*):** A ready-to-run scenario that introduces players to Tapani politics, aristocratic rivalries, and the dangers lurking beneath the Expanse's polished exterior.
- **Character & Scenario Support:** Guidance on integrating Tapani-specific character templates, patron relationships, and linked episode hooks across multiple sessions.
