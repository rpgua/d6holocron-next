*Ahsoka: The Novel Sourcebook* is a fan-made supplement released in February 2021 for the *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* D6 system, formatted for compatibility with Second Edition and the community-compiled *Revised, Expanded and Updated* (REUP) ruleset. It adapts E.K. Johnston's 2016 novel *Ahsoka* into playable tabletop material, focusing on the early Imperial era roughly one year after Order 66 and the fall of the Jedi Order.

Set during the period when former Jedi Padawan Ahsoka Tano lives in hiding under the alias "Ashla," the sourcebook bridges the gap between the end of the Clone Wars and the rise of organized rebellion under Bail Organa. Gamemasters running Dark Times or early Rebellion campaigns can use the supplement to introduce local resistance cells, Imperial occupation forces, and Inquisitorius threats operating on Outer Rim worlds.

## Key Contents

- **Characters and Adversaries:** D6 REUP game statistics for Ahsoka Tano in exile, Bail Organa, local allies on Thabeska and Raada, Imperial officers, and the Sixth Brother of the Inquisitorius.
- **Worlds and Locations:** Setting profiles for key locations from the novel, including the trade world of Thabeska, the agrarian moon of Raada, and Imperial garrisons enforcing early New Order rule.
- **Equipment and Lightsabers:** Technical details and rules for specialized gear, salvaged tools, Imperial occupation hardware, and Ahsoka's white-bladed lightsabers constructed from purified kyber crystals.
- **Early Rebellion Campaign Context:** Narrative background covering fugitive Jedi survival, underground resistance cells, and the origins of the Fulcrum intelligence network.
