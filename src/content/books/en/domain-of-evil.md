*Domain of Evil* (1991, `WEG40034`) is a First Edition adventure module published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Billed as an advanced scenario for four to six experienced Rebel characters and a Gamemaster, it blends survival adventure with psychological horror during the Galactic Civil War era.

Pursued by the bounty hunter Crutag and his mercenaries, the player characters crash-land on the isolated swamp world of Trinta. Beyond repairing their ship and evading their pursuers, the Rebels discover that the planet conceals a potent Dark Side nexus—an anomaly comparable to the cave on Dagobah—inhabited by Halagad Ventor, a former Jedi Knight broken by Darth Vader's interrogation and driven into madness by the nexus's influence.

## Key Contents

- **Trinta Swamp Setting:** Environmental hazards, wilderness encounters, and regional details for a remote frontier world shrouded in Dark Side energy.
- **Psychological Trials and Illusions:** Encounters that blur reality and hallucination, confronting each character with personal fears, moral tests, and Dark Side temptations.
- **Halagad Ventor and Crutag:** Full First Edition D6 statistics, backgrounds, and tactical notes for the fallen Jedi hermit, the pursuing bounty hunter, and his henchmen.
- **Climax at the Dark Nexus:** A multi-stage finale guiding the group through the heart of the nexus to confront Ventor and escape the planet.
