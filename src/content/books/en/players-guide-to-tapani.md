Published by West End Games in July 1997 as a 64-page softcover companion to the *Lords of the Expanse* boxed set, *Player's Guide to Tapani* (WEG40155) was written by Paul Sudlow and Eric S. Trautmann for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (2E-RE). Designed to sit on the players' side of the table without spoiling gamemaster secrets, it consolidates and expands the cultural background, character creation options, and regional rules needed to portray natives of the Tapani Sector during the Galactic Civil War.

The book provides structured guidance on navigating the aristocratic Expanse and the independent Freeworlds Region, helping players build characters deeply rooted in the sector's social fabric—whether as scheming heirs, house guards, lightfoil duelists, or sharp-witted merchants.

## Key Contents

- **Tapani Sector & The Expanse:** Accessible overviews of sector history, geography, Imperial relations, and daily life in the civilized core provinces.
- **Tapani Noble Houses:** Detailed player-safe breakdowns of the Seven Great Houses (Barnaba, Cadriaan, Calipsa, Mecetti, Melantha, Pelagia, and Reena), including noble titles, status, and courtly etiquette.
- **The Freeworlds Region & Gazetteer:** Profiles of non-noble systems, trade ports, and travel destinations suited for smugglers, scouts, and independent spacers.
- **Character Templates & Mechanics:** New Tapani-specific character templates, rules for social standing and saber rake dueling traditions, and equipment entries including lightfoils.
- **Tapani Campaign Guidance:** Player and GM advice for weaving character backgrounds into political and corporate intrigue.
