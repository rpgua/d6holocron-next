*Shadows of the Empire: Planets Guide* (1996, `WEG40134`) is a 96-page planetary sourcebook published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Developed alongside the *Shadows of the Empire* multimedia project (with contributions from Sterling Hershey, John Beyer, Chris Doyle, and Timothy S. O'Brien), the supplement expands four pivotal locations connected to the struggle between the Rebel Alliance, the Galactic Empire, and Black Sun in 3.5 ABY.

Each chapter follows the classic WEG gazetteer format, combining planetary statistics, climate and geography, local politics, cultural customs, native wildlife, notable NPCs, and multiple ready-to-run adventure outlines designed to drop directly into ongoing campaigns.

## Key Contents
- **Rodia:** Explores the sweltering jungle homeworld of the Rodians, its clan-based hunting culture, guild politics, local predators, and four adventure outlines including *Jungle Hunt* and *A Trip to the Country*.
- **Vergesso Asteroids (Vergesso Base):** Details the concealed asteroid shadowport and Rebel-sympathetic underworld haven, its black-market economy, criminal syndicates, and scenarios such as *Slave Raid* and *"Will the Last Person Leaving Vergesso Turn Off the Lights?"*
- **Bothawui:** Profiles the ancestral Bothan homeworld, the intricate espionage networks of the Bothan Spynet, the political maneuvers of the "Great Game," and adventure hooks including *A Case of Mistaken Identities* and *An Invitation to Defect*.
- **Kothlis:** Covers the strategic industrial and research colony within Bothan Space—site of the pivotal Death Star II plans intelligence coup—alongside four espionage and sabotage outlines such as *Operation: Recovery* and *The Ganjuko Graveyard*.
