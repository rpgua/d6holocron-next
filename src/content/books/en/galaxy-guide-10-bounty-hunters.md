*Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters*, written by Rick D. Stuart and published by West End Games in 1994 (WEG 40073), is a Second Edition sourcebook dedicated to the legalities, tactics, organizations, and infamous figures of bounty hunting during the Galactic Civil War. Rather than treating bounty hunters solely as recurring antagonists, the book provides a complete framework for running entire hunter-focused campaigns or integrating Imperial bounty contracts into standard Rebel and Fringe games.

Combining in-universe commentary with practical Gamemaster tools, the guide explores how the Empire regulates licensed hunters through Imperial Peace-Keeping Certificates (IPKCs), how contracts are posted and fulfilled, and how independent operators navigate the overlapping jurisdictions of the Empire, local sectors, and the criminal underworld.

## Key Contents

- **The Profession & The Business:** Detailed overview of bounty hunting under the New Order, Imperial Bounty Registration, IPKC regulations, contract payouts, and sample *Wanted* dossiers.
- **Character Creation & Templates:** Guidelines for building bounty hunter player characters, complete with new 2E character templates and skill advice.
- **The Hunt & Modes of Operation:** Practical rules and narrative techniques for investigation, tracking fugitives across star systems, surveillance, and capture tactics.
- **Hunter Notables & Guilds:** Profiles and 2E statistics for infamous hunters—including Boba Fett, Bossk, and IG-88—alongside coverage of the Bounty Hunters' Guild and rival agencies.
- **Specialized Equipment:** Catalog of restraint systems, tracking fobs, armor, non-lethal weapons, and starship modifications tailored for live-capture missions.
- **Chapter Nine: *Two for the Price of One*:** A ready-to-play introductory adventure placing player characters directly into a competitive bounty hunt.
