*Force Power Cards* is a community-produced printable play aid created by the Google+ Star Wars D6 community for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (REUP and Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). In the classic West End Games D6 system, Force-sensitive characters often juggle numerous individual powers across the Control, Sense, and Alter skills—many of which combine two or three disciplines and carry layered prerequisite chains, target difficulties, and duration rules.

This reference deck solves the problem of constant rulebook page-flipping at the tabletop by condensing individual Force powers onto uniform, print-and-play cards. Players running Jedi, Padawans, or Force-attuned fringe characters can assemble a personal hand containing only the powers their character knows, while Gamemasters can pull cards to track the abilities of Inquisitors, Dark Jedi, or allied Force users during encounters.

## Key Contents

- **Control, Sense, and Alter Coverage:** Organizes core and expanded Star Wars D6 Force powers by their required Force skills, including multi-skill combination powers.
- **At-a-Glance Game Mechanics:** Summarizes skill difficulties, required time to use, duration, "kept up" status, and prerequisite powers directly on each card.
- **Concise Effect Summaries:** Presents clear, tabletop-ready summaries of each power's mechanical effects, modifiers, and Dark Side Point triggers.
- **Print-and-Play Layout:** Formatted in uniform card grids intended for home printing, cutting, and sleeving for repeated campaign use.
