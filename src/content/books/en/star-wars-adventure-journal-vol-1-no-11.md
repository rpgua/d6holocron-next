*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 11* (WEG41011), published by West End Games in November 1996 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer alongside Bill Smith, marks the series' shift to full compatibility with *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. Combining landmark Expanded Universe fiction with tournament-tested D6 adventure material, this issue spans the Imperial era and the prelude to the Thrawn campaign.

Its centerpiece fiction piece by Timothy Zahn expands the backstory of Grand Admiral Thrawn in the Unknown Regions, while the issue's main RPG module provides a full-length social and intrigue scenario suitable for any fringe or Rebel crew.

## Key Contents
- **Headline Short Fiction:** Features Timothy Zahn's *"Command Decision"*—chronicling Admiral Thrawn, Commander Voss Parck, and Captain Dagon Niriz confronting Ebruchi pirates in the Unknown Regions—and Patricia A. Jackson's *"The Longest Fall"* (later collected in *Tales from the New Republic*).
- **Full-Length Adventure:** *"The Farrimmer Cafe"* by Jean Rabe and J. Allan Fawcett is a 31-page RPGA Network tournament scenario centered on intrigue, rival patrons, and escalating danger inside a bustling spaceport cafe.
- **Smuggler's Log:** Continues Platt Okeefe's framing commentary with new underworld lore introducing the Klatooinian Trade Guild and guild boss Pok Nar-Ten.
- **Comic Strip:** Includes Jim Anderson's humorous comic strip *"Bungo n' Rusti Get Carry-Out"*, set on Sullust.
- **D6 Rules & Recurring Columns:** Contains *Galaxywide NewsNets* (including subtle hints of Thrawn's return toward the Core), *Alien Encounters* species profiles, and *Revised & Expanded* rules updates.
