*Cracken's Threat Dossier* (1997, `WEG40139`) is a 144-page sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), written by Drew Campbell and published by West End Games. Framed as a collection of classified New Republic Intelligence security files assembled under General Airen Cracken, the book adapts major post-Endor Expanded Universe novels into playable D6 campaign material.

Covering three major regional crises of the New Republic era, the dossier equips gamemasters with political background, military assessments, alien species templates, starship specifications, and character profiles drawn directly from Bantam-era Star Wars fiction. It serves as both a lore reference for the evolving New Republic timeline and a source of high-stakes adversaries beyond the traditional Imperial Remnant.

## Key Contents

- **Chapter One — The Hapan Cluster Incident:** Adapts Dave Wolverton's *The Courtship of Princess Leia*, detailing the Hapes Consortium, Hapan Battle Dragons and military forces, noble politics, Dathomir, and the Force-wielding Witches of Dathomir and Nightsisters.
- **Chapter Two — The Black Fleet Crisis:** Draws from Michael P. Kube-McDowell's *Black Fleet Crisis* trilogy (*Before the Storm*, *Shield of Lies*, *Tyrant's Test*), profiling the xenophobic Yevetha, the Duskhan League, the Koornacht Cluster, and Yevethan thrustships and stolen Imperial warships.
- **Chapter Three — The Corellian Incident:** Covers Roger MacBride Allen's *Corellian Trilogy* (*Ambush at Corellia*, *Assault at Selonia*, *Showdown at Centerpoint*), examining Corellian system politics, separatist factions, Centerpoint Station, and native species including the Selonians and Drall.
- **Complete 2E-RE Game Data:** Extensive D6 statistics for major characters, alien species, Force traditions, planetary locations, and capital ships across all three crisis zones.
