*Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two* (1992, `WEG40057`) is an official West End Games planetary sourcebook for the First Edition of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by John Terra. Set roughly two years after the Battle of Endor during the early New Republic era, this 80-page anthology builds upon the format established in Volume One by delivering eight original worlds ready for immediate campaign use.

Each world is presented with geographical data, planetary statistics, local political conditions, and ongoing Imperial or criminal plots, making the volume a versatile resource for Gamemasters running exploration, diplomatic, or Rebel-versus-Imperial campaigns. The book was later revised for Second Edition and reprinted in *The Star Wars Planets Collection* (1994).

## Key Contents

- **Eight Original Worlds:** Detailed planetary gazetteer entries for Algara II, Atraken, Carosi XII, Ergeshui, Fyodos, Gacerian, Korbin, and Zelos II.
- **New Alien Species and Wildlife:** Game statistics and cultural backgrounds for native sentient species alongside indigenous creatures and ecological hazards.
- **Local Factions and Imperial Plots:** Profiles of regional governments, orbital stations, criminal elements, and post-Endor Imperial operations.
- **Adventure Hooks and Locations:** Points of interest, starport descriptions, and scenario seeds integrated into every planetary chapter to help Gamemasters launch sessions quickly.
