*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 2* (WEG41002), published by West End Games in May 1994 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer, is the second quarterly anthology supporting *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Set primarily during the Galactic Civil War, the issue blends Expanded Universe short fiction with ready-to-use D6 campaign resources, planetary gazetteers, and gamemaster aids designed to plug directly into ongoing Rebel and fringe-sector campaigns.

A major thread in this issue is the Imperial-occupied world of Garos IV, introduced through both fiction and adventure material, alongside expanded guidance for privateering, smuggling, and navigating Imperial Customs checkpoints.

## Key Contents
- **Short Fiction:** Includes Charlene Newcomb's *"Whispers in the Dark"* (introducing Alexandra "Alex" Winger's resistance efforts on Garos IV), Patricia A. Jackson's *"Out of the Cradle"*, and Peter M. Schweighofer's *"Big Quince"*.
- **Adventures & Campaign Frameworks:** Features *"A World to Conquer"* by Dustin Browder (a Garos IV adventure framework) and *"Rebel Privateers!"* by Timothy S. O'Brien with rules and guidance for running Alliance privateer operations.
- **Planetary & Travel Guides:** *"The Free-Trader's Guide to Sevarcos"* by Anthony P. Russo details the spice-producing desert world, while *"Recon & Report: The Journey to Coruscant"* covers travel to the Imperial capital.
- **NPC Profiles & Smuggler Lore:** *"Smuggler's Log: Imperial Customs"* profiles dangerous customs officials, and *"Wanted by Cracken"* provides full D6 stats and background for the bounty hunter Boddu Bocck.
- **Behind-the-Scenes Feature:** *"Making Star Wars Comics Come Alive"* by Ilene Rosenberg interviews Dark Horse Comics creators Tom Veitch, Chris Gossett, and Dan Thorsland.
