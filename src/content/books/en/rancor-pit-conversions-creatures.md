*Rancor Pit Conversions: Creatures* (`rp_creatures`) is a fan-compiled bestiary produced by The Rancor Pit community for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Part of the site's long-running conversion library, it bridges the gap left after West End Games ceased publishing in 1998 by adapting beasts, apex predators, mounts, and exotic fauna introduced in later *Star Wars* films, novels, comics, and Wizards of the Coast's d20 and Saga Edition sourcebooks into classic D6 mechanics.

Designed as a practical encounter reference for gamemasters, the book supplies ready-to-use wildlife profiles for wilderness survival scenes, planetary exploration, arena battles, and outer-rim expeditions across multiple eras of the *Star Wars* timeline.

## Key Contents

- **Converted Bestiary Entries:** Complete D6 stat blocks including Dexterity, Perception, Strength, Move rates, scale, and specialized creature skills such as *Tracking*, *Sneak*, and *Intimidation*.
- **Natural Attacks & Special Traits:** Game mechanics for claws, venom, natural armor, flight, camouflage, Force-sensing instincts, and pack hunting tactics.
- **Habitat & Ecological Notes:** Brief descriptions of native worlds, preferred biomes, temperament, and behavioral patterns to help gamemasters stage believable encounters.
- **Mounts, Beasts & Monsters:** Coverage ranging from domesticated riding animals and minor planetary pests to dangerous megafauna and legendary predators suited for high-stakes D6 combat.
