*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Scum and Villainy* is a fan-made conversion guide compiled by The Rancor Pit community to bring Wizards of the Coast's *Scum and Villainy* (2008) from *Star Wars Saga Edition* into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Acting as a rules and statistics companion to the original sourcebook, it translates fringe species, black-market hardware, criminal archetypes, and underworld figures into D6 dice codes and mechanics.

Focused on smugglers, bounty hunters, slicers, pirates, and syndicates operating outside galactic law, the conversion supports gritty Outer Rim and underworld campaigns across any era of *Star Wars* play, supplementing classic WEG books such as *Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters* and *Platt's Smugglers Guide*.

## Key Contents

- **Fringe Species**: Complete D6 attribute ranges and special abilities for eight underworld-oriented species: Blood Carvers, Clawdites, Falleen, Gand, Jawas, Ryn, Toydarians, and Ubese.
- **Outlaw Tech & Gear**: Converted statistics and modification rules for black-market weapons, customized armor, concealed mounts, miniaturized gadgets, and illegal starship upgrades favored by smugglers and bounty hunters.
- **Criminal Organizations & NPCs**: D6 profiles for generic underworld archetypes—fixers, slicers, swoop gangers, enforcers, and assassins—alongside major syndicates including Black Sun, the Hutt Kajidics, the GenoHaradan, the Tenloss Syndicate, and Thalassian Slavers, plus notable figures such as Prince Xizor and Booster Terrik.
- **Shadowports & Fringe Campaigns**: Mechanical support for underworld locales like Point Nadir, Nar Shaddaa, and the *Errant Venture*, as well as job-generation and heist encounter elements.
