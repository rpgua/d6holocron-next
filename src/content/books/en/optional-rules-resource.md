*Optional Rules Resource* is a fan-compiled reference document preserved by the Google+ Star Wars D6 community and the D6 Holocron archive for use with *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (REUP and Second Edition). Over more than a decade of publishing, West End Games introduced dozens of specialized subsystems, variant mechanics, and situational rules across sourcebooks, adventures, and *Star Wars Adventure Journal* issues. *Optional Rules Resource* gathers these scattered mechanics—alongside widely adopted community refinements—into a single indexed handbook.

Rather than forcing Gamemasters to search through out-of-print supplements mid-campaign, the compilation organizes optional subsystems by topic so groups can selectively enable the level of mechanical detail that fits their table. It is particularly useful for long-running sandbox, smuggler, scout, and naval campaigns that benefit from deeper procedural rules.

## Key Contents

- **Environment & Survival Rules:** Covers encumbrance, overland foot travel, contaminated water, extreme atmospheric hazards, and environmental dangers.
- **Trade, Underworld & Economy:** Compiles mechanics for speculative cargo trading, tonnage and cargo space, black market sourcing, loan sharks, and spice dealings.
- **Equipment, Maintenance & Tech:** Includes rules for gear upkeep, jury-rigging across editions, starship and vehicle repairs, computer languages, and quick-draw actions.
- **Starship Maneuvers & Space Operations:** Details specialized space combat tactics (such as the Ackbar Slash and A-Wing Slash), gravity well projectors, and hyperspace interdiction.
- **Campaign & Exploration Generators:** Collects procedural tools for generating star systems and planets, scouting expeditions, and ancient research.
