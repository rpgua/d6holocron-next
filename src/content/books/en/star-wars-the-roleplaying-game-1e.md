Published by West End Games in October 1987 and designed by Greg Costikyan, *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (WEG40001) is the 144-page hardcover core rulebook that inaugurated the D6 System and helped revive the Star Wars Expanded Universe during the late 1980s. Set primarily during the Galactic Civil War between *A New Hope* and *Return of the Jedi*, the game was built to replicate the fast, cinematic action of the films using only standard six-sided dice.

Costikyan's design emphasized accessibility and rapid setup. Rather than complex point-buy tables, players selected from ready-to-play archetypes, allocated a small pool of starting skill dice, and jumped directly into play. The book won the 1987 Origins Award for Best Roleplaying Rules and established the foundational terminology, Imperial and Rebel lore, and mechanical framework that shaped West End Games' decade-long Star Wars line and influenced later Star Wars fiction.

## Key Contents

- **The D6 System Basics:** Introduces the six core attributes (*Dexterity, Knowledge, Mechanical, Perception, Strength,* and *Technical*), dice codes, difficulty numbers, and Force Points.
- **24 Character Templates:** Ready-to-play archetypes including Smuggler, Bounty Hunter, Brash Pilot, Failed Jedi, Quixotic Jedi, Ewok, Mercenary, Pirate, Rebel SpecForce, and Gambler, each with background hooks and starting gear.
- **Combat & Starship Rules:** Fast-paced personal combat, vehicle chases, hyperdrive astrogation, and starfighter dogfights structured around cinematic action rather than grid-heavy simulation.
- **The Force:** Foundational rules for the three Force skills (*Control, Sense,* and *Alter*), classic Force powers, and the temptation of Dark Side Points.
- **Introductory Adventures:** Includes a solo mini-adventure to teach the rules alongside the classic group scenario *Rebel Breakout*, pitting new characters against Imperial forces.
