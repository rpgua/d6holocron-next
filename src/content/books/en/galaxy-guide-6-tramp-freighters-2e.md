*Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters (2nd Edition)* updates Mark Rein-Hagen and Stewart Wieck's 1990 free-trader sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*, revised by Eric S. Trautmann and published in this printing in 1996. Designed for Rebellion-era campaigns focusing on independent spacers, smugglers, and debt-ridden freighter crews, the book converts all trade systems, ship modification rules, and setting statistics to Second Edition mechanics.

Structured across thirteen chapters, the sourcebook serves both as a standalone sandbox toolkit for fringe campaigns and as a modular rules expansion that integrates independent crews into the wider Galactic Civil War.

## Key Contents

- **Spacer Campaign Setup:** Guidance for launching a tramp freighter campaign, spacer character templates, and rules for integrating independent crews with Rebel Alliance operations.
- **Trade, Contraband & Debt:** Complete 2E chapters covering drop-point delivery contracts, speculative commodity markets, black-market dealing, Imperial Customs infractions, and loan sharks.
- **Ship Modifications & Repairs:** Updated Second Edition rules for upgrading engines, shields, weapons, sensor suites, and smuggling holds, alongside repair difficulties and maintenance costs.
- **The Minos Cluster:** Full sector gazetteer detailing Imperial oversight (anchored by the *Victory I*-class Star Destroyer *The Chariot*), the Minos-Mestra Corporation, and key worlds including Adarlon, Mestra, Gesaril, and Haven.
- **The Minos Campaign & Reference Tables:** A ready-to-play five-adventure mini-campaign, 2E profiles for local contacts and regional starships, and quick-reference charts for trade and ship economics.
