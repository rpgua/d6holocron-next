*The Abduction of Crying Dawn Singer* (1992, `WEG40053`) is an official West End Games rescue and intrigue adventure for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, later reprinted in the 1995 *Classic Adventures* anthology. Set in the Rayter sector during the Galactic Civil War, the scenario centers on delicate diplomatic negotiations between the Rebel Alliance and the avian Shashay species of Crytal Nest to secure a vital new Rebel base.

The talks collapse into crisis when **Crying Dawn Singer**, a celebrated Shashay performer and cultural icon, is abducted by pirates led by Yearo Seville. Falsified HoloNet broadcasts frame the Rebel Alliance for the kidnapping—a conspiracy orchestrated by TransGalMeg Industries administrator Braig Farool and his brother Nak, an aide to Imperial Moff Nile Owen, to discredit both the Rebels and the Moff simultaneously. Dispatched by Rebel commander Zeke Rondel, the player characters must follow the trail across Imperial-controlled worlds, uncover the corporate-Imperial plot, and rescue the singer from a high-security stronghold before the alliance with the Shashay is permanently ruined.

## Key Contents

- **Six-Episode Investigative Arc:** Progresses through *Najarka*, *Leaving Najarka*, *Laim*, *Narg*, *Into the Bird Cage*, and *Escape*, combining clue-gathering, travel across the Rayter sector, and stealth infiltration.
- **Political & Corporate Conspiracy:** Detailed background on the Farool brothers' scheme, TransGalMeg Industries' local influence, and Moff Nile Owen's precarious position in the sector.
- **The Shashay & Rayter Sector Lore:** Setting details for Crytal Nest, Najarka, Laim, and Narg, along with cultural notes on the Shashay species and their diplomatic leverage.
- **Stronghold Infiltration ("The Bird Cage"):** Tactical layout, security measures, and pirate opposition guarding Crying Dawn Singer's compressed desiccation chamber.
- **Pre-Generated Character Templates:** Ready-to-play Rebel templates and NPC stat blocks for immediate integration into an ongoing campaign or standalone session.
