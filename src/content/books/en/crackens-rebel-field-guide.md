*Cracken's Rebel Field Guide* (1991, `WEG40046`) is an 80-page First Edition supplement for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Christopher Kubasik and published by West End Games. Framed as an in-universe Rebel Alliance Intelligence technical manual compiled by General Airen Cracken during the Galactic Civil War, the book addresses the practical realities of asymmetric warfare against the better-funded Galactic Empire.

Instead of functioning as a standard shopping catalog, the guide emphasizes resourcefulness, improvisation, and tactical adaptation. Entries are accompanied by in-character annotations, field reports, and technical diagrams explaining how Rebel technicians repurpose civilian machinery, reverse-engineer captured Imperial hardware, and push standard-issue equipment beyond factory tolerances.

## Key Contents

- **Jury-Rigging Rules:** Dedicated D6 mechanics allowing technically skilled characters to temporarily modify gear, boost performance, or assemble improvised devices—such as converting power packs into explosives—under field conditions.
- **Weapons and Combat Gear:** Specialized armaments, heavy weapons, modified blasters, and tactical accessories tailored for guerrilla operations and covert strikes.
- **Surveillance and Detection:** Field-tested sensors, reconnaissance monitors, countermeasures, and tracking hardware used to evade or observe Imperial patrols.
- **Computer Data and Slicing Tech:** Guides to computer frameworks, data security protocols, decryption utilities, and slicing tools for infiltrating Imperial networks.
- **Cybernetics and Reverse-Engineered Imperial Tech:** Overviews of field cybernetics and prosthetics alongside intelligence breakdowns of Imperial hardware, including communication arrays and tracking systems such as the XX-23 S-thread tracker.
