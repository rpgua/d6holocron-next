*Adventurer's Journal Issue 2* (product code `AJ080318`) is the second issue of the community-produced *Adventurer's Journal* anthology for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP), released on August 3, 2018. Built around a unifying focus on alien species, non-human cultures, and xenobiology, the issue expands player and gamemaster options for campaigns that venture beyond standard human-centric worlds.

Combining updated D6 species statistics, hazardous environment rules, specialized alien gear, and random generation tools, the issue serves as both a mini-sourcebook on galactic diversity and a traditional anthology of fiction and playable scenarios. It also continues recurring columns introduced in Issue 1, including *Location Scout*, *Beyond BoSS*, and *General Quarters*.

## Key Contents
- **Alien Species & Environments:** *Alien Species Update* by Oliver Queen and Daniel Sperelli, *New Alien Species: The Sen-dro* by Daniel Sturman, *Alien Species... STAT!* by Chris Shaylor, and *Alien Environments* by Charles McNeill.
- **Gear & Random Generators:** *Alien Technology and Gear* by Johannes Verri, a *Random Alien Appearance Generator* for rapid NPC creation, and a *3D Tabletop Miniature: Alien Gunslinger* feature.
- **Group & Solo Adventures:** *What Was Lost*, a group adventure by Eric Rodriguez, and *Just For One Day, You Are The Hero!*, a solitaire adventure by Mark Dowson featuring recurring scout Aurek Jenth, plus the short story *Never Tear Us Apart* by Jason Arana Sr.
- **Templates & Locations:** The new *Adventurer* character template, *Location Scout: Much Hollow*, *Beyond BoSS*, *General Quarters*, and a blank character sheet.
