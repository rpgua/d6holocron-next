*Instant Adventures* (1997, `WEG40137`) is a Second Edition Revised & Expanded anthology published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Unlike full-length campaign modules, the 72-page softcover presents nine concise, self-contained scenario frameworks intended to be run with minimal preparation as one-shot sessions or interludes between longer campaign arcs.

Spanning Rebel strikes, intelligence recoveries, smuggling jobs, and fringe-world complications during the Galactic Civil War, each scenario provides a structured plot outline, key scenes, and adaptable hooks so Gamemasters can drop them into existing groups with little adjustment.

## Key Contents

- **Nine Ready-to-Run Scenarios:** Includes *"The Argovia Strike"*, *"Agent Nallok Is Missing"*, *"Heavy Lifting"*, *"Into the Heat of Battle"*, *"Family Problems"*, *"Meltdown"*, *"New Recruits and Rebel Guns"*, *"The Treasure of Celis Mott"*, and *"Operation: Shadowstrike"*.
- **32 Full-Color Adventure Cards:** Visual reference cards depicting ships, vehicles, aliens, and major non-player characters featured across the anthology.
- **Modular Structure:** Each adventure is organized into compact episodes with difficulty targets, pacing tips, and optional complications for fast tabletop reference.
- **2E Revised & Expanded Statistics:** Ready-to-use game stats for Imperial troops, fringe rivals, droids, starships, and supporting characters.
