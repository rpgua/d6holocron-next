*Rancor Pit Conversions: The Force* is a fan-produced rules compendium compiled by The Rancor Pit community for West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Because West End Games ceased publishing before the Prequel Trilogy, *Knights of the Old Republic*, and later Expanded Universe eras introduced dozens of new Force abilities and mystical orders, community contributors developed this volume to translate post-WEG Force material from d20, *Saga Edition*, and fiction into the classic *Control*, *Sense*, and *Alter* framework.

Rather than relying on rigid mathematical conversion formulas, the supplement adapts modern Force powers, lightsaber forms, and esoteric traditions to fit D6 difficulty numbers, multi-action penalties, Force Point expenditures, and Dark Side Point mechanics. It serves as an expanded companion to the core rulebook and *Tales of the Jedi Companion* for campaigns featuring Jedi, Sith, or independent Force-sensitives.

## Key Contents

- **Expanded Force Powers:** New *Control*, *Sense*, and *Alter* powers—along with combination powers—complete with difficulty ratings, time to use, prerequisites, and mechanical effects.
- **Alternative Force Traditions:** Game rules and lore adaptations for non-Jedi mystical groups across the galaxy, translating their distinct philosophies and signature abilities into D6 terms.
- **Lightsaber Combat Forms & Techniques:** D6 mechanical interpretations of the classic seven forms of lightsaber combat and specialized Force-assisted martial maneuvers.
- **Dark Side Powers & Sith Alchemy:** Corrupted techniques, Sith sorcery, and Dark Side abilities balanced with Dark Side Point triggers and resistance rules.
- **Force Artifacts & Holocrons:** Guidelines and statistics for interacting with Jedi and Sith holocrons, Force-imbued relics, and rare lightsaber crystals.
