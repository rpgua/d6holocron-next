Published by West End Games in October 1997 (WEG 40089) with Bill Smith serving as lead author alongside a large team of WEG contributors, the *Star Wars Trilogy Sourcebook: Special Edition* is an updated, comprehensive reference covering *A New Hope*, *The Empire Strikes Back*, and *Return of the Jedi*. Built for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* (2E-RE), it consolidates and revises material from earlier classic volumes—particularly *The Star Wars Sourcebook* and *The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook*.

Created to coincide with the 1997 theatrical Special Edition releases, the book incorporates newly added scenes, creatures, droids, and background elements—such as the expanded Mos Eisley sequences, Jabba the Hutt's Docking Bay 94 confrontation, and enhanced Cloud City and galactic celebration scenes—into a single all-in-one encyclopedia for original trilogy campaigns.

## Key Contents

- **Heroes, Scoundrels, and Villains:** Updated 2E-RE game statistics and backgrounds for major and minor characters across all three films, from Rebel heroes and Imperial commanders to cantina denizens and Jabba's court.
- **Special Edition Additions:** New profiles and statistics for creatures, droids, vehicles, and background characters introduced or expanded in the 1997 Special Edition releases, including rontos, scurriers, and ASP labor droids.
- **Planets and Iconic Locations:** Campaign guides for Tatooine, Yavin 4, Hoth, Dagobah, Bespin, Endor, and both Death Stars.
- **Starships and Vehicles:** Revised technical profiles and game stats for X-wings, TIE fighters, Star Destroyers, the *Millennium Falcon*, AT-AT walkers, snowspeeders, and speeder bikes.
- **Aliens, Creatures, and Equipment:** Reference entries for sentient species, beasts such as rancors and wampas, droids, weapons, and classic trilogy gear.
