*Galaxy Guide 13: The Phantom Menace* is an unofficial, fan-produced sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Because West End Games lost the *Star Wars* license in 1998—one year before the release of *Episode I*—the D6 community created this supplement to continue the *Galaxy Guide* numbering beyond *Galaxy Guide 12* and adapt the Prequel Trilogy era to classic D6 mechanics.

Structured in the tradition of official WEG movie guides, the book provides Gamemasters with the political backdrop, factions, and game statistics needed to run campaigns set during the twilight of the Galactic Republic and the Trade Federation's blockade of Naboo.

## Key Contents

- **Episode I Character Profiles:** Full D6 stat blocks and background notes for Qui-Gon Jinn, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Queen Padmé Amidala, young Anakin Skywalker, Jar Jar Binks, Darth Maul, Senator Palpatine, and Trade Federation leaders.
- **Species & Fauna:** Playable species templates and creature statistics for Gungans, Neimoidians, Toydarians, Dugs, and native Naboo and Tatooine wildlife such as kaadus and fambaas.
- **Planetary Gazetteers:** Setting profiles and adventure hooks for Naboo (Theed and Otoh Gunga), Coruscant's Senate and Jedi Temple, and Mos Espa on Tatooine.
- **Starships, Vehicles & Droids:** D6 combat and movement statistics for N-1 starfighters, Trade Federation Droid Control Ships, battle droids, droidekas, AATs, STAPs, the Sith Infiltrator, and podracers.
