*Corellian Engineering Corporation Compendium* is a fan-created reference book for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 System), compiled by the Womp Rat Press community for use with the *Revised, Expanded, and Updated* (REUP) ruleset. Dedicated entirely to the galaxy's most prolific civilian and paramilitary shipwright, the compendium consolidates decades of canon, Legends, and fan-adapted Corellian Engineering Corporation (CEC) spacecraft into a single game-ready catalog.

Designed as an exhaustive resource for Gamemasters and players running campaigns across the Rebellion, New Republic, and Fringe eras, the volume moves beyond iconic light freighters to examine the full breadth of CEC's modular engineering philosophy. Entries follow standard West End Games D6 stat-block conventions, pairing technical specifications with operational histories and modification notes so crews can customize, refit, or salvage Corellian vessels during play.

## Key Contents

- **Corporate Lore & Engineering Philosophy:** Background on the Corellian Engineering Corporation, its decentralized shipyard network, and its hallmark modular hull design.
- **Light & Medium Freighters:** Consolidated D6 game statistics for the famous YT-series, YU-series, and YV-series transports alongside lesser-known courier and cargo hulls.
- **Heavy Transports & Bulk Cruisers:** Specifications for large-scale commercial haulers, container ships, and modular freighter convoys used along major hyperlanes.
- **Corvettes, Gunships & Capital Ships:** Combat-rated CEC designs, including blockade runners, patrol corvettes, and heavy escort vessels operated by planetary defense forces and the Rebel Alliance.
- **Customization & Variant Notes:** Guidance on stock upgrades, aftermarket modifications, and common smuggler refits compatible with REUP starship rules.
