*Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin* is an 80-page First Edition planetary sourcebook written by Jonatha Ariadne Caspian and published by West End Games in 1989 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Shifting away from the character-focused format of *Galaxy Guide 1*, this volume serves as an in-depth gazetteer for two iconic gas-giant systems from the original trilogy during the era between *A New Hope* and *The Empire Strikes Back*.

The book is split evenly between the Yavin system—exploring the evacuated Rebel base and ancient ruins of Yavin 4—and the Bespin system, detailing the floating metropolis of Cloud City and its lucrative tibanna gas mining operations.

## Key Contents

- **The Yavin System & Yavin 4:** Planetary data for Yavin and its habitable moons, jungle ecosystems, native fauna, ancient Massassi temples, and the aftermath of the Battle of Yavin.
- **Cloud City & Bespin:** Comprehensive breakdown of Cloud City's districts, tourism, casino plazas, tibanna gas refineries, Ugnaught labor communities, and Lando Calrissian's administration.
- **First Edition Game Stats & NPCs:** 1E D6 statistics for local personalities, Imperial and Rebel agents, Bespin Wing Guard personnel, Lobot, and native creatures.
- **Maps & Adventure Outlines:** Location layouts, security notes, and scenario hooks enabling Gamemasters to set espionage, exploration, or underworld adventures on both worlds.
