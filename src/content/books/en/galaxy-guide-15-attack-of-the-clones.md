*Galaxy Guide 15: Attack of the Clones* is a 126-page fan-produced sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), developed primarily by Joseph McWhorter with design and production by Thiago S. Aranha. Modeling its layout and structure on West End Games' official *Galaxy Guide* series, the supplement adapts the characters, factions, locations, and military hardware of *Episode II* into classic D6 rules.

Set at the outbreak of the Clone Wars, the book outlines the political crisis gripping the Galactic Senate, the rise of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, and the deployment of the Grand Army of the Republic, giving Gamemasters a complete toolkit for Prequel-era campaigns.

## Key Contents

- **The Galactic Republic & Senate:** Profiles and D6 stats for Supreme Chancellor Palpatine, Padmé Amidala, Bail Organa, Mas Amedda, Captain Typho, Republic senators, royal guards, and Naboo vessels such as the Royal Cruiser and Yacht.
- **The Jedi Order:** Stats for Yoda, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, Mace Windu, and Jedi Council members, alongside Delta-7 *Aethersprite* Jedi starfighters, training lightsabers, and analysis droids.
- **The Separatists & Bounty Hunters:** Write-ups for Count Dooku, Jango Fett, young Boba Fett, and Confederacy military and corporate leaders.
- **Kamino & Geonosis:** Setting details for Kaminoan cloning facilities, Clone Trooper profiles and equipment, Geonosian arena beasts, and prequel-era species and weapons.
