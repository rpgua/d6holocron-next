*REUP Lost Stock Stats Archive* is a companion reference supplement compiled by Womp Rat Press for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game — Revised, Expanded, and Updated* (REUP). As the core REUP rulebook consolidated decades of West End Games material into a single volume, numerous ready-to-use statistics from older books, adventures, and defunct community sites were either omitted for space or scattered across separate documents. This archive gathers those missing stock profiles into one accessible Gamemaster resource.

Structured for quick reference during session prep or live play, the book provides standardized REUP-compatible stat blocks for generic characters, fauna, droids, and hardware. It allows Gamemasters to populate cantinas, Imperial garrisons, Rebel bases, and wilderness encounters without having to build stat blocks from scratch or convert legacy First Edition figures on the fly.

## Key Contents

- **Stock NPCs & Archetypes:** Ready-to-run profiles for Imperial troopers, officers, pirates, smugglers, bounty hunters, Rebel operatives, and planetary security personnel.
- **Creatures & Wildlife:** Standardized D6 statistics for beasts of burden, predators, and exotic fauna drawn from classic WEG supplements and fan archives.
- **Vehicles & Starships:** Recovered and converted stat blocks for common speeders, walkers, freighters, starfighters, and support craft omitted from the core compilation.
- **Droids & Equipment Blocks:** Quick-reference profiles for utility, protocol, astromech, and security droids alongside standard gear loadouts.
- **REUP Consistency:** Unified dice codes, scale indicators, and skill listings aligned with the 2015 REUP core rulebook.
