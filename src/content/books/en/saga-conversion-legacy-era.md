*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Legacy Era Campaign Guide* is a fan-compiled conversion supplement by The Rancor Pit community that translates Wizards of the Coast's *Star Wars Roleplaying Game: Saga Edition — Legacy Era Campaign Guide* (2009) into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Intended as a rules companion to the original *Saga Edition* sourcebook, it converts the setting's species, Force traditions, advanced gear, starships, and major faction NPCs into D6 System game statistics.

Set more than 130 years after the Battle of Yavin and inspired by Dark Horse Comics' *Star Wars: Legacy* series, the setting depicts a fractured galaxy caught in a three-way struggle between Darth Krayt's One Sith Empire, Emperor Roan Fel's loyalist Imperial Remnant and Imperial Knights, and Admiral Gar Stazi's Galactic Alliance Remnant. This conversion enables D6 Gamemasters to run gritty, late-timeline campaigns with ready-to-use mechanics.

## Key Contents

- **Legacy Era Species:** D6 attribute ranges and special abilities for playable species featured in the era, including Chiss, Chagrians, Codru-Ji, Klatooinians, Nagai, Weequay, and Caamasi.
- **Force Traditions & Imperial Knights:** D6 adaptations of Force powers, the light-aligned Imperial Knights serving Roan Fel, the One Sith order, and fringe traditions such as the Disciples of Twilight and Ember of Vahl.
- **130+ ABY Equipment & Heirlooms:** Converted D6 stats for faction-specific weapons, Imperial Knight armor, Sith gear, advanced droids, and rules for ancestral heirloom items.
- **Modernized Starships & Vehicles:** D6 stat blocks for Legacy-era starfighters (such as the *Predator*-class), transports, and capital ships deployed by the Alliance Remnant, Fel Empire, and Sith fleets.
- **Major Faction NPCs & Fringe Figures:** Converted D6 profiles for key leaders, military commanders, Sith Lords, Jedi survivors, and underworld operators from the Galactic Alliance, Empire, One Sith, and Fringe chapters.
