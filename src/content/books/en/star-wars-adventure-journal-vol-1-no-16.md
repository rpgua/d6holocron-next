*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 16* (WEG41016) was scheduled for release by West End Games in 1998 as the next quarterly installment for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. When West End Games declared bankruptcy and lost the *Star Wars* license following Issue 15, Issue 16 became the first cancelled volume of the series and was never officially printed.

Over the following years, surviving manuscripts, author drafts, and planned articles were shared online or repurposed in other publications, and the *Star Wars* D6 fan community compiled and preserved this archival edition so gamemasters and historians could access the lost material.

## Key Contents
- **Planned Short Fiction:** Slated to feature *"Hutt and Seek"* by Chris Cassidy and Tish Pahl and *"Gathering Shadows"* by Kathy Burdette (both later associated with *Tales from the New Republic* and online releases).
- **Adventure & Underworld Scenarios:** Included planned gaming pieces such as *"Credit Denied"* by George R. Strayton and *"A Servant of the Empire"* by James L. Cambias.
- **Setting & Planetary Guides:** Planned source articles covered *"The Garos System"* by Charlene Newcomb and Steve Miller (expanding on the Garos IV storyline from earlier issues) and *"Corellian Trans-lines"* by Chris Grind.
- **Recurring Features:** Designed to continue regular *Adventure Journal* columns such as *"Smuggler's Log"* with *Revised & Expanded* D6 statistics and campaign hooks.
