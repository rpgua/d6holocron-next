*Black Ice* (1990, `WEG40030`) is a First Edition military-scale adventure module published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the scenario moves beyond small-scale skirmishes to place Rebel Alliance player characters at the center of a sector-wide logistical heist with strategic consequences for the Rebellion.

The mission begins with an infiltration of an Imperial research and logistics outpost to acquire intelligence on Replenishment Fleet DK-209. The stolen data reveals the *Black Ice*, a colossal 7,800-meter field-secured container ship composed of nine linked force-field spheres hauling nearly a billion tons of starship-grade fuel cells. Desperate for fuel, the Alliance launches a fleet engagement to board and capture the vessel, only to face an Imperial counter-offensive involving zero-gee stormtroopers and an Imperial Torpedo Sphere.

## Key Contents

- **Multi-Act Military Campaign**: Structured episodes covering outpost infiltration, starfighter and boarding combat, shipboard counter-insurgency, and a final defense of an Alliance Sector HQ.
- **The *Black Ice* Vessel**: Full deck plans, internal security layouts, and D6 statistics for the massive container ship and its escort elements.
- **Imperial Counter-Strike Forces**: Game profiles and tactical notes for zero-gee stormtroopers, Imperial naval officers, and the siege-class Imperial Torpedo Sphere.
- **Gamemaster Pull-Out Section**: Includes a scripted opening scene, player handouts, pre-generated character templates, and tactical maps.
- ***Star Warriors* Integration**: Contains cardboard counter strips and conversion guidelines allowing groups to resolve the space battle using West End Games' *Star Warriors* board game.
