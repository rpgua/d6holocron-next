*Scoundrel's Luck* (1990, `WEG40102`) is a single-player solitaire adventure gamebook published by West End Games as part of the *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* product line. Released alongside its companion volume *Jedi's Honor* (which stars Luke Skywalker), the book was designed to introduce newcomers to *Star Wars* roleplaying and offer existing fans a portable, GM-less experience.

Unlike standard First Edition D6 adventure modules, *Scoundrel's Luck* requires no Gamemaster, dice, or character sheets. Instead, it uses a branching numbered-section format similar to classic interactive fiction gamebooks, relying on reader judgment, intuition, and resourcefulness to survive encounters. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the narrative casts the reader as Han Solo, piloting the *Millennium Falcon* alongside his Wookiee co-pilot Chewbacca. Racing across the galaxy while dodging Imperial TIE fighters and underworld hazards, Solo must track down a mysterious kidnapper and rescue Princess Leia Organa before time runs out.

## Key Contents
- **Branching Solitaire Format:** Self-contained numbered paragraphs allowing a single reader to play through multiple paths, dead ends, and triumphant conclusions without dice or a Gamemaster.
- **Classic Trilogy Cast:** First-person perspective as Han Solo, featuring Chewbacca, Princess Leia, and the *Millennium Falcon* in an original Expanded Universe mission.
- **Fast-Paced Set Pieces:** Interactive scenes covering starship dogfights, smugglers' bluffs, cantina investigations, and daring rescues.
- **Original Artwork:** Interior black-and-white illustrations depicting key characters, starships, and dramatic moments from the adventure.
- **Companion Series Entry:** Forms one half of West End Games' two-book *Star Wars* Solitaire Adventure series alongside *Jedi's Honor*.
