The *Gamemaster Screen for Second Edition* (WEG40064) was published by West End Games in December 1992 as one of the earliest table accessories supporting *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Written by Bill Olmesdahl and developed by Bill Smith, the package pairs a sturdy four-panel cardstock reference screen with a 48-page companion booklet built to speed up play and minimize preparation time.

Designed for active use during Rebellion-era and fringe campaigns, the screen consolidates the updated Second Edition mechanics—such as the Wild Die, scale modifiers, and revised combat charts—into an at-a-glance format. Its accompanying booklet focuses on practical rapid-play support, giving gamemasters modular adventure hooks that can be dropped into a session with little advance prep.

## Key Contents
- **Four-Panel GM Screen:** Durable fold-out cardstock barrier concealing notes and dice rolls while organizing essential Second Edition difficulty, combat, damage, movement, and starship charts.
- **Quick-Start Adventure Hooks:** A 48-page companion booklet packed with structured, ready-to-run scenario outlines and plot seeds designed for instant gaming sessions.
- **Thematic Scenario Categories:** Adventures and encounters organized across classic *Star Wars* genres, including exploration, quests, alien encounters, mysteries, Force-related plots, and smuggling runs.
- **Fast-Play Reference Material:** Condensed rules summaries, encounter setups, and NPC guidelines tailored to reduce mid-session page-flipping through the 2E core rulebook.
