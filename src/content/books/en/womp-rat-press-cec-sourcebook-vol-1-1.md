*Corellian Engineering Corporation Sourcebook Vol. 1.1* is a community-produced *Star Wars* D6 supplement published by Womp Rat Press and formatted for the *Revised, Expanded, and Updated* (REUP) edition. Released as a revised first volume in the collective's Corellian vessel series, the book focuses on the history, industrial infrastructure, and signature starship lines of the Corellian Engineering Corporation (CEC).

Written in the style of classic West End Games corporate and hardware sourcebooks, this volume bridges setting lore with practical tabletop utility. It gives Gamemasters detailed background on how CEC operates within the Corellian Sector and across the wider galaxy, while equipping players with reliable D6 statistics for classic freighters, support craft, and patrol vessels frequently encountered by smugglers, independent traders, and Rebel cells.

## Key Contents

- **History & Shipyards:** Overview of CEC's origins in the Corellian system, its orbital construction yards, and its commercial rivalry with competing galactic shipbuilders.
- **Classic Freighter Lines:** Full D6 stat blocks, cargo capacities, and performance profiles for foundational CEC light freighters and utility transports.
- **Patrol & Escort Craft:** Game statistics and operational roles for Corellian picket ships, corvettes, and civilian security vessels.
- **Modular Systems & Refits:** Notes on CEC's standardized components, ease of repair, and typical aftermarket weapon, shield, and hyperdrive upgrades.
- **Campaign Integration:** Adventure hooks and corporate context for using CEC brokers, salvage yards, and shipwrights in Rebellion-era or Fringe campaigns.
