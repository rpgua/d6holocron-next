*Hideouts & Strongholds* (`WEG40111`) is an official West End Games sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Sterling Hershey. Designed to be adaptable across any era of play—from the Old Republic and Rebellion to the New Republic—the supplement provides Gamemasters and players with ready-to-use base layouts as well as rules for constructing, upgrading, and maintaining custom headquarters.

Whether a campaign centers on Rebel cells operating from hidden outposts, smugglers defending a secret shadowport, or players infiltrating an Imperial garrison, the book offers architectural context, tactical floor plans, and game statistics to ground base-centered encounters and long-term campaign operations.

## Key Contents

- **Base Engineering Rules:** Introduces the `(A) engineering` advanced skill along with step-by-step rules for designing, building, modifying, repairing, and jury-rigging structures and settlements.
- **Facility Upgrades and Defenses:** Catalogs of base customization systems including weapons emplacements, defensive shielding, power generators, computer networks, medical bays, and cargo storage.
- **Twenty Ready-to-Use Locations:** Detailed profiles with maps, floor plans, game statistics, and customization tips covering criminal dens, Rebel safehouses, Imperial military posts, corporate complexes, mansions, and starports such as Estaria Central Starport.
- **Scenario Hooks:** Built-in adventure seeds tailored to each base archetype, allowing Gamemasters to drop locations directly into ongoing campaigns.
