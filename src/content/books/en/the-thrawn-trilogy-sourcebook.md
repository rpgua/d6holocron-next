Published by West End Games in 1996 as a 248-page hardcover omnibus, *The Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook* compiles, streamlines, and updates the three earlier standalone volumes—*Heir to the Empire Sourcebook*, *Dark Force Rising Sourcebook*, and *The Last Command Sourcebook*—for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. Written by Bill Slavicsek and Eric S. Trautmann and featuring a foreword by novel author Timothy Zahn, it serves as the definitive D6 reference for the 9 ABY Thrawn campaign.

Rather than simply reprinting the three books back-to-back, the omnibus reorganizes their material into ten cohesive thematic chapters, eliminating duplicate character entries and standardizing all game statistics under *2E-RE* rules. It provides gamemasters with a unified campaign guide to the New Republic's struggle against Grand Admiral Thrawn and the dark Jedi clone Joruus C'baoth.

## Key Contents

- **Thrawn's Campaign & Factions**: Dedicated chapters covering Grand Admiral Thrawn's military strategy, the New Republic government and military, the Imperial Remnant, and fringe organizations such as Talon Karrde's smuggling network.
- **The Force**: Era-specific Force lore and mechanics addressing dark side adepts, cloned Force-users, and Joruus C'baoth.
- **Planets & Mount Tantiss**: Location profiles and maps for major worlds—including Myrkr, Wayland, Honoghr, Nkllon, Berchest, and Pantolomin—plus a full chapter detailing Emperor Palpatine's cloning facility inside Mount Tantiss.
- **Aliens, Creatures & The Noghri**: Statistics and background for trilogy species, indigenous fauna such as Force-blocking ysalamiri and vornskrs, and an in-depth chapter on Noghri society and history.
- **Unified Character & Hardware Stats**: Consolidated *2E-RE* profiles for major characters (Thrawn, Mara Jade, Captain Pellaeon, Talon Karrde, Luke, Han, Leia, and Lando), starships, vehicles, and droids.
