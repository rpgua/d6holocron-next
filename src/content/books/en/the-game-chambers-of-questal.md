*The Game Chambers of Questal* (1990, `WEG40033`) is a First Edition adventure module for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* published by West End Games. Set on the remote Outer Rim world of **Questal** during the Galactic Civil War, the scenario begins when a fellow Rebel agent named Tiree disappears while pursuing an unsanctioned lead on a rumored Imperial superweapon. Because Alliance High Command refuses to commit resources to an unauthorized mission, the player characters travel to Questal undercover and without official support to rescue their friend.

Under the tyrannical rule of **Moff Bandor**, Questal has degenerated into a fearful, crime-ridden colony. The heroes soon uncover Bandor's secret psychological terror weapon—the **Hurlothrumbic Generator**, designed to break the will of his enemies—alongside the subterranean **Game Chambers** beneath his palace, a series of lethal labyrinthine arenas where the Moff hunts captives for sport. Surviving Bandor's deadly gauntlet is the only way to free Tiree, neutralize the weapon, and spark Questal's liberation.

## Key Contents

- **Six-Episode Adventure Structure:** Moves from planetary arrival and underworld investigation (*Welcome to Questal*, *Tiree's Trail*, *Tiree's Tale*) to palace infiltration, arena survival, and rebellion (*Bandor's Palace*, *The Game Chambers*, *Freeing of Questal*).
- **The Game Chambers Arenas:** Detailed trap-filled gauntlet featuring distinct deadly environments—including a space simulator, laser maze, pit of winds, and quarry—complete with tactical maps and running notes.
- **Questal Planetary Setting:** Background on the capital city of Gralleenya, local cantinas such as *Shilley's*, oppressed citizens, and criminal elements operating under Moff Bandor's regime.
- **Moff Bandor & the Hurlothrumbic Generator:** Full game statistics for Moff Bandor, his guards, and rules for the fear-inducing psychological weapon threatening the Alliance.
- **Pullout Adventure Script & Maps:** Center-section visual aids, floorplans for Bandor's palace and sublevels, and gamemaster guidance for running an unsupported covert rescue.
