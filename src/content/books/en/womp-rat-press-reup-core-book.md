*Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game — Revised, Expanded, and Updated* (commonly known as **REUP**) is a landmark 2015 fan-compiled core rulebook produced by the REUP Team under the Womp Rat Press imprint. Built upon West End Games' *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* (1996), REUP was created by the online D6 community to preserve, modernize, and unify the classic tabletop system after the official WEG line ended.

Spanning over 500 pages, the book consolidates official errata, clarifies ambiguous mechanics, and integrates material from across the *Star Wars* timeline—incorporating Prequel Trilogy, Clone Wars, and Expanded Universe elements alongside the original Galactic Civil War setting. It serves as a complete, self-contained core manual for both new players and longtime D6 Gamemasters, preserving the fast-paced Wild Die and attribute-plus-skill mechanics that defined the West End Games era.

## Key Contents

- **Character Creation & Templates:** Full rules for attributes, skills, specializations, advantages, disadvantages, and an expanded roster of playable templates and alien species.
- **Core Mechanics & Combat:** Streamlined D6 task resolution, Wild Die rules, movement, ranged and melee combat, vehicle chases, and damage recovery.
- **The Force:** Consolidated rules for *Control*, *Sense*, and *Alter* skills, Force Points, Dark Side Points, lightsaber combat, and a broad library of Force powers.
- **Equipment, Droids & Starships:** Extensive catalogs of weapons, armor, gear, droids, ground vehicles, starfighters, freighters, and capital ships with updated space combat rules.
- **Gamemastering & Setting Guide:** Advice on adventure design, campaign pacing, NPC management, and running games across multiple *Star Wars* eras.
- **Unified 2E-RE Core Ruleset:** Combines the 1996 hardcover rules with popular optional mechanics, updated skill specializations, and expanded character creation options in a single indexed volume.
- **MK III Errata & Rules Fixes:** Corrects legacy editing errors from earlier REUP printings, including restoring standard multiple-action blaster fire rules to match WEG 2E-RE and fixing stat anomalies such as Verpine attribute caps and YT-2400 hull codes (u/EngineeredToDeath). See: [r/StarWarsD6](https://www.reddit.com/r/StarWarsD6/comments/xd8xcx/reup_typo_and_errata_correction_unofficial_reup/)
- **Expanded Force & Era Support:** Integrates dozens of Force powers, Force traditions, lightsaber combat rules, and templates covering the Galactic Civil War, New Republic, and Prequel eras.
- **Consolidated Gear, Species & Starships:** Gathers playable alien species, droids, weapons, vehicles, and iconic starships from across the WEG catalog into updated stat blocks.
- **Layout & Reference Polish:** Cleans up broken page references, overlapping text blocks, and missing index entries reported in the community errata threads.
