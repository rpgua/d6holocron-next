Published by West End Games in 1998 near the close of its Star Wars license, *Alien Encounters* is a comprehensive species compendium for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. Compiled by Paul Sudlow and edited by Jen Seiden, the book gathers 135 sentient alien species that had previously been scattered across earlier sourcebooks, adventure modules, and issues of the *Star Wars Adventure Journal*, updating and standardizing them into a single reference volume.

Designed to work alongside *Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races* and *Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies*, *Alien Encounters* eliminates the need to hunt through dozens of out-of-print books for playable alien templates. In addition to its encyclopedic species catalog, it provides structured guidelines for gamemasters designing original sentient species for their own sectors.

## Key Contents

- **Alien Generation Guidelines**: Chapter One offers practical rules, balance advice, and biological frameworks for creating custom playable alien species in the D6 system.
- **135 Sentient Species Profiles**: Alphabetical entries detailing each species' homeworld, physiology, culture, language, attribute dice ranges, movement, and special abilities.
- **Standardized 2E-RE Statistics**: Revised and harmonized game mechanics bringing older 1E, 2E, and *Adventure Journal* species up to *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* standards.
- **Master Alien Indices**: Closing cross-reference indices—both alphabetical and organized by biological/physiological category—covering *Alien Encounters*, *Galaxy Guide 4*, and *Galaxy Guide 12*.
