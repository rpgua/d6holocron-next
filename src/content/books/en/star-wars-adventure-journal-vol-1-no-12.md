*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 12* (WEG41012), published by West End Games in February 1997 under editors Peter M. Schweighofer and George R. Strayton, is the twelfth issue of the *Revised and Expanded* era anthology. Best remembered for launching the four-part crossover novella *"Side Trip"* by Timothy Zahn and Michael A. Stackpole, this issue also packs an unusually large slate of playable D6 scenarios, tactical accessories, and Imperial military source material.

Blending Corellian intrigue, alien biology, and Imperial planetary defense doctrine, Issue 12 serves equally well as an Expanded Universe story collection and a practical gamemaster toolkit.

## Key Contents
- **Headline Crossover Fiction:** Opens *"Side Trip"* (Parts One and Two), written jointly by Timothy Zahn and Michael A. Stackpole, bringing together characters from the *Thrawn* and *X-Wing* series in a high-stakes Corellian adventure.
- **Playable Adventures & Scenarios:** Includes *"Betrayal by Knight"* by Patricia A. Jackson and Charlene Newcomb, *"Festival of High Winds"*, *"Heroes Need Not Apply"*, *"Small Favors"*, and *"Idol Intentions"*.
- **Imperial Military Source Material:** *"Imperial Garrisons"* outlines the organization, fortifications, and D6 operational details of standard Imperial military outposts.
- **Alien Species Guide:** *"Shape-shifters"* by Pablo Hidalgo explores shapeshifting species across the *Star Wars* galaxy with background lore and roleplaying statistics.
- **Tactical Aids & News:** Features punch-out/reference *"Vehicle Counters"* for tabletop vehicle combat alongside the recurring *"Galaxywide NewsNets"* column.
