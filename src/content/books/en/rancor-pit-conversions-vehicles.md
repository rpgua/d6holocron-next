*Rancor Pit Conversions: Vehicles* is a community-compiled planetary transport and ground combat supplement created by The Rancor Pit for West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Drawing from Wizards of the Coast's d20 and *Saga Edition* sourcebooks, visual guides, video games, and Expanded Universe media, the compendium converts atmospheric and surface vehicles into standard D6 System statistics.

Covering eras from the Old Republic and Clone Wars through the Galactic Civil War and beyond, the book fills out ground and atmospheric rosters for planetary campaigns, chase scenes, and large-scale military engagements. Each vehicle uses the established D6 stat block layout—specifying Scale (Speeder or Walker), Skill, Crew, Move, Maneuverability, Body Strength, and mounted weapons—ensuring full compatibility with *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* vehicle combat rules.

## Key Contents

- **Speeder Bikes, Swoops & Landspeeders:** Civilian and military repulsorlift craft for reconnaissance, urban pursuit, and high-speed swoop racing.
- **Airspeeders & Atmospheric Craft:** Low-altitude gunships, cloud cars, planetary patrol craft, and atmospheric fighters operating within a world's gravity well.
- **Combat Walkers & Armored Repulsortanks:** Heavy assault walkers, scout walkers, hovertanks, and mobile artillery pieces statted at Walker scale with armor codes and fire arcs.
- **Aquatic & Submersible Vehicles:** Surface skimmers, hydrofoils, and deep-sea submarines for ocean worlds and amphibious operations.
- **Utility & Industrial Transports:** Cargo skiffs, sandcrawlers, troop carriers, and heavy construction vehicles for frontier logistics and heist scenarios.
