*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: The Force Unleashed Campaign Guide* is a community-built conversion supplement from The Rancor Pit translating Wizards of the Coast's *The Force Unleashed Campaign Guide* (2008) from *Star Wars Saga Edition* into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Paired with the original sourcebook for narrative background, it supplies full D6 game statistics for campaigns set during the "Dark Times"—the nineteen-year span between *Revenge of the Sith* and *A New Hope*.

Drawing on both the *Force Unleashed* multimedia project and broader Dark Times Legends lore, the conversion equips D6 Gamemasters to run stories about surviving Jedi, nascent Rebel factions, Imperial purges, and clandestine Force users operating under Darth Vader's shadow.

## Key Contents

- **Dark Times Species**: Complete D6 species templates for ten alien races prominent during the early Empire: Aleena, Caamasi, Felucians, Gran, Nosaurians, Talz, Togorians, Togruta, Whiphids, and Yarkora.
- **The Force & Alternative Traditions**: D6 conversions of high-powered Force abilities, dark side techniques, and non-Jedi Force traditions surviving the Purge.
- **Equipment & Droids**: Converted statistics for early Imperial armor, specialized weapons, infiltration gear, and notable droids such as holodroids and combat automata.
- **Vehicles & Starships**: D6 profiles for transports, starfighters, and Imperial craft active during the rise of the New Order.
- **Notable Characters & Factions**: Converted NPC stats for key figures of the era—including Starkiller (Galen Marek), Darth Vader, General Rahm Kota, and PROXY—alongside organizational templates for early rebel cells and Imperial agencies.
