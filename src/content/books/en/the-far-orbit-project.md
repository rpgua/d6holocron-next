*The Far Orbit Project* (1998, `WEG40029`) is a campaign supplement published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Authored by Abel G. Peña and Hudson Hodgson as a direct companion to *Pirates & Privateers*, it was one of the final Star Wars sourcebooks released by West End Games before the license ended.

The book centers on the *Far Orbit*, a newly commissioned Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate whose crew mutinied under Commander Dhas Vedij and defected to the Rebel Alliance. Operating under a Rebel letter of marque in the wealthy Ringali Shell near the Core Worlds, the *Far Orbit* conducts daring commerce-raiding operations against Imperial shipping while evading relentless pursuit. Players can take on command-level roles or rotate between bridge officers, starfighter pilots, and boarding teams using the book's troupe-style campaign rules.

## Key Contents
- **The *Far Orbit*:** Complete D6 starship statistics, operational history, crew roster, internal politics, and detailed multi-deck floorplans for the modified Nebulon-B frigate.
- **The Ringali Shell:** Regional gazetteer detailing the Bormea and Darpa sectors, major trade lanes, wealthy Core-adjacent targets, and safe anchorages for privateer operations.
- **Enemies of the *Far Orbit*:** Profiles and statistics for recurring adversaries hunting the frigate, including Imperial Navy commander Captain Vocis Kenit, Sector Rangers, corporate security forces, and system patrol units.
- **Linked Campaign Arc:** A complete sequence of interconnected privateering adventures—including *Preparing for War*, *The Hijacking of Shipment 1037*, *Hunter/Hunted*, *Raid on Brentaal*, *The Grand Prize*, and *The Trap*.
- **Privateer Campaign Mechanics:** Guidelines for managing ship supplies, prize crews, morale, repairs, and multi-character play aboard a capital warship.
