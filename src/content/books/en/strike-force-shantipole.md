*Strike Force: Shantipole* (1988, `WEG40009`) is a 32-page First Edition adventure module published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set during the Galactic Civil War prior to the Battle of Endor, the supplement occupies a landmark place in *Star Wars* Expanded Universe lore by introducing the origins of the Rebel Alliance's B-wing heavy assault starfighter and the insectoid Verpine species.

The player characters are dispatched to the treacherous Roche Asteroid Field to deliver urgent intelligence to Commander Gial Ackbar, who is secretly overseeing Project Shantipole—a joint Alliance-Verpine engineering effort hidden inside Research Station Shantipole. Shortly after the heroes arrive, Imperial agents compromise the station's location and launch an assault to seize or destroy the prototype starfighters. Caught in the middle of the attack, the characters must help delay the Imperial strike force, coordinate the evacuation of Ackbar and the Verpine engineers, and escort the prototype B-wings back to Alliance High Command. The module was later reprinted for Second Edition in *Classic Adventures: Volume Five* (1998).

## Key Contents
- **Project Shantipole Lore:** Background history on the development of the B-wing starfighter, Commander Ackbar's role in the project, and the Roche Asteroid Field.
- **The Verpine Species:** First-ever RPG introduction, cultural notes, and First Edition D6 statistics for the technologically gifted insectoid Verpine.
- **Full-Color Station Map:** A detailed interior map of Research Station Shantipole included for tactical ground encounters and evacuation planning.
- **Prototype B-Wing & Imperial Forces:** Complete D6 game statistics for the prototype B-wing starfighters, station defenses, and the attacking Imperial task force.
- ***Star Warriors* Combat Scenario:** A companion space-combat scenario designed for use with West End Games' *Star Warriors* tactical starfighter board game.
