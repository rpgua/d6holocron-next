Published by West End Games in February 1996 and written by Timothy S. O'Brien, George R. Strayton, and Eric S. Trautmann, *The Kathol Outback* (WEG40118) is the 96-page second installment of *The DarkStryder Campaign* for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (Second Edition)*. Picking up directly after the events of the 1995 boxed set in 8 ABY, the supplement follows the New Republic corvette *FarStar* as it leaves the fringes of Imperial-charted territory and pushes deeper into the isolated frontier known as the Kathol Outback.

Cut off from New Republic resupply lines, the *FarStar* crew must navigate unreliable hyperlanes, barter for vital resources, and untangle local political rivalries while following Moff Kentor Sarne's trail toward the mysterious Kathol Rift. The book blends regional gazetteer material with five linked adventures that test crew cohesion, diplomacy, and survival on the galaxy's rim.

## Key Contents

- **The Kathol Outback Gazetteer:** Detailed background on the subsector's geography, astrogation hazards, frontier worlds, trade routes, and independent polities beyond standard Republic jurisdiction.
- **Denizens of the Outback:** Profiles, lore, and D6 game statistics for regional alien species, local factions, wildlife, and key NPCs encountered along the *FarStar*'s route.
- **Five Linked Adventures:** Sequential scenarios advancing the hunt for Moff Sarne—*Plant Food*, *Little Empires*, *Wildfire*, *The Masters of Exocron*, and *Galaxy's Edge*.
- **Frontier Campaign Support:** Gamemaster guidance for tracking ship supplies, managing tensions among the *FarStar*'s mixed crew, and planting clues leading into *The Kathol Rift*.
