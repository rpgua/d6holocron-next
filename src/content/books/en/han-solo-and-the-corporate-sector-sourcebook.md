Written by Michael Allen Horne and published by West End Games in 1993 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition), the *Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook* adapts Brian Daley's classic *Han Solo Adventures* trilogy (*Han Solo at Stars' End*, *Han Solo's Revenge*, and *Han Solo and the Lost Legacy*). Set before the Battle of Yavin, the book establishes the Corporate Sector Authority (CSA)—a vast, profit-driven fiefdom in the Outer Rim chartered by the Galactic Empire and governed by mega-corporations.

The sourcebook provides gamemasters with a self-contained frontier setting ideal for smuggling, heist, and fringe campaigns that operate outside the direct jurisdiction of the Imperial Navy. Alongside the history of the Corporate Sector Charter and the exploits of Han Solo and Chewbacca, it details the bureaucratic machinery and ruthless security apparatus that keep corporate space profitable.

## Key Contents

- **Corporate Sector History and Geography:** Origins of the Corporate Sector Charter, its voting and contributing corporate sponsors, and regional overviews of CSA space.
- **Security Division and Stars' End:** Organization, tactics, and law enforcement structure of the Espos (Security Police), plus a dedicated chapter detailing the infamous automated prison facility Stars' End.
- **Characters and Fringe Operatives:** Second Edition statistics for Han Solo, Chewbacca, Bollux, Blue Max, CSA executives, independent scoundrels, and resistance figures from Daley's novels.
- **Starships and Vehicles:** Profiles for CSA picket ships, *Victory*-class and *Invincible*-class vessels, IRD starfighters, corporate transports, and ground vehicles.
- **Equipment, Droids, Aliens, and Creatures:** Comprehensive stat blocks for specialized weapons, labor and security droids, regional alien species, and native fauna.
