*Freddy B's Compilation Guide to TIE Fighters* is a fan-made technical sourcebook created by community contributor Freddy B and archived through The Rancor Pit and the D6 Holocron for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (2E-RE). Designed as a single-volume reference for gamemasters and players running Imperial Navy campaigns or starfighter-heavy Galactic Civil War scenarios, it consolidates Sienar Fleet Systems' Twin Ion Engine (TIE) lineage from across official West End Games sourcebooks, Expanded Universe novels, comics, and video games.

Rather than leaving gamemasters to hunt through dozens of scattered supplements, the guide standardizes every model into consistent Second Edition Revised and Expanded D6 starfighter statistics—including scale, skill requirements, maneuverability, hull codes, shields, sensor ranges, and weapon fire controls.

## Key Contents

- **Standard Imperial Lineup:** Consolidated D6 profiles for core Sienar Fleet Systems craft, including the TIE/ln Fighter, TIE Interceptor, TIE Bomber, TIE Scout, and TIE Shuttle.
- **Advanced & Elite Variants:** Detailed technical specifications and background notes for high-performance craft such as the TIE Advanced x1, TIE Avenger, TIE Defender, TIE Phantom, and TIE Aggressor.
- **Experimental & Rare Models:** Game statistics for specialized prototypes and obscure Expanded Universe designs, including the TIE Experimental series (M1 through M5) and niche atmospheric or heavy-assault variants.
- **Pilot & Squadron Support:** Ready-to-use D6 statistics for rookie, standard, and veteran Imperial TIE pilots alongside operational notes for deploying squadrons in tabletop space combat.
