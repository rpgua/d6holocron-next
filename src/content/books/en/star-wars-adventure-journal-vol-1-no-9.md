*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 9* was published by West End Games in February 1996, edited by Peter M. Schweighofer for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Blending Rebellion-era espionage, smuggling operations, and post-Endor fringe drama, this ninth quarterly issue equips gamemasters with new starships, contraband tactics, full adventure material, and official rules clarifications.

Designed for groups looking to expand starship ownership and covert operations, the issue pairs narrative short fiction with highly practical gaming supplements. Players running tramp freighters benefit from an entire lot of secondhand vessels and smuggling techniques, while Rebel-aligned groups receive a scenario tied directly to General Crix Madine's covert investigations.

## Key Contents
- **Short Fiction:** Includes Angela Phillips's *"Slaying Dragons"* (following a young slicer at Kuat Freight Port aiding the Rebel Alliance, later reprinted in *Tales from the Empire*) and Paul Danner's *"Easy Credits"* set on Rydonni Prime.
- **Death-Hunter:** A full roleplaying adventure by Tony Russo in which Rebel agents working for General Crix Madine uncover a dangerous Imperial project.
- **Fizzi's Slightly Used Starships:** A starship catalog by John J. Richardson III presenting full D6 game statistics, quirks, and floor-lot descriptions for seven secondhand tramp freighters.
- **Lumrunners:** A smuggling guide by John Beyer and Wayne Humfleet outlining contraband runs, hidden compartments, and tactics for evading Imperial Customs patrols.
- **Combat Moon & Departments:** Includes the *"Combat Moon"* adventure/source feature alongside *"Galaxywide NewsNets"* by Paul Sudlow and Bill Smith's *"ISB Intercepts"* rules Q&A column.
