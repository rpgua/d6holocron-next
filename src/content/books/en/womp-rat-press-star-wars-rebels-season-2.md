*Star Wars Rebels: Season Two Sourcebook* is a fan-created supplement produced by Womp Rat Press (released around 2017) for the *Star Wars* D6 Roleplaying Game (*Revised, Expanded, and Updated*). Continuing directly from the collective's Season One volume, it adapts the expanding galactic conflict depicted in the second season of *Star Wars Rebels*, as the Lothal cell joins Phoenix Squadron and attracts the direct attention of Darth Vader and the Imperial Inquisitorius.

Formatted in the classic West End Games sourcebook tradition, the supplement provides updated statistics, new factions, and larger-scale military hardware reflecting the transition from isolated street-level sabotage to organized fleet operations. It serves as a practical toolkit for Gamemasters running pre-Yavin Rebel Alliance campaigns, Jedi survival stories, or Imperial counter-insurgency scenarios.

## Key Contents

- **Expanded Rebel Alliance & Allies:** Updated profiles for the *Ghost* crew alongside key Season Two allies, including Ahsoka Tano, Commander Jun Sato, Captain Rex, Wolffe, Gregor, and Hondo Ohnaka.
- **Imperial Threats & Inquisitors:** D6 statistics for Darth Vader in the Dark Times era, the Fifth Brother, the Seventh Sister, Admiral Konstantine, and elite Imperial strike units.
- **New Worlds & Ancient Sites:** Setting details for Phoenix Squadron's hidden bases, Seelos, Shantipole, Garel, Lira San, and the ancient Sith temple on Malachor.
- **Starships & Prototype Hardware:** Game stats for the B-wing prototype, A-wing interceptors, Hammerhead corvettes, *Pelta*-class command frigates, Imperial *Interdictor* cruisers, and modified AT-TE walkers.
- **Force Lore & Campaign Hooks:** Rules and narrative guidance for ancient Force relics, inquisitor spinning lightsabers, and multi-cell Rebel fleet operations.
