Released by West End Games in October 1992 and written by Bill Smith, *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition* (WEG40055) is a 176-page hardcover overhaul of the original 1987 D6 System. Drawing on five years of player feedback and the revisions first tested in *The Star Wars Rules Companion* (1989), Second Edition modernized the ruleset while maintaining broad compatibility with existing First Edition sourcebooks and adventures.

This edition marked a major mechanical turning point for the line. It introduced the signature Wild Die to inject cinematic unpredictability into every roll, replaced First Edition's complex damage-chart modifiers with a unified scale system for interactions between characters, vehicles, starfighters, and capital ships, and formalized skill specializations and Character Points. Set primarily during the Galactic Civil War with hooks for New Republic play, it served as the primary rulebook for West End Games' mid-1990s output until 2E-RE arrived in 1996.

## Key Contents

- **The Wild Die & Character Points:** Introduces the exploding six / complication one Wild Die mechanic alongside Character Points for heroic dice boosts and character advancement.
- **Unified Scale System:** Standardizes combat and damage resolution across five scales—Character, Speeder, Walker, Starfighter, and Capital—simplifying mixed-scale encounters.
- **Updated Character Creation & Templates:** Features revised character templates with Force-sensitivity options, skill specializations, and rules for building non-template and alien characters.
- **Streamlined Combat & Starships:** Reworks initiative, multiple-action penalties, movement, vehicle chases, and starship combat into faster, clearer sequences.
- **Expanded Force & Gamemastering:** Consolidates *Control, Sense,* and *Alter* powers, Dark Side rules, and structured chapters on episodic adventure design and running campaigns.
