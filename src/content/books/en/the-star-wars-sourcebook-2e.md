Published by West End Games in June 1994, *The Star Wars Sourcebook (2nd Edition)* updates Bill Slavicsek and Curtis Smith's landmark 1987 reference book to the rules of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Revised and edited by Bill Smith and Peter M. Schweighofer, this edition preserves the foundational in-universe essays and 15-chapter structure of the original while recalibrating all game mechanics to match Second Edition scale, movement, and combat rules.

Intended as a core tabletop companion for Galactic Civil War campaigns, the updated volume ensures that classic ships, droids, creatures, and film characters remain directly compatible with 1990s West End Games supplements. Gamemasters using Second Edition or *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* can use its stat blocks without manual conversion from First Edition.

## Key Contents

- **Updated Starship & Vehicle Rules**: Revised Second Edition D6 statistics—including updated scale, maneuverability, hull, and shield ratings—for starfighters, capital ships, freighters, repulsorlift vehicles, and Imperial ground walkers.
- **Droids & General Equipment**: Recalibrated 2E profiles for astromech, protocol, medical, and assassin droids, standard adventuring gear, and lightsaber mechanics.
- **Aliens & Creatures**: Second Edition species templates and statistics for classic Star Wars aliens (Wookiees, Mon Calamari, Sullustans, Twi'leks, Ithorians, Quarren, Ewoks, and more) plus iconic creatures.
- **Military Installations & Stormtroopers**: Detailed layouts and organizational charts for Rebel bases, standard Imperial garrisons, and specialized stormtrooper corps.
- **Revised Heroes & Villains**: Updated 2E character dossiers for Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa, Han Solo, Chewbacca, Darth Vader, Grand Moff Tarkin, and other original trilogy figures.
