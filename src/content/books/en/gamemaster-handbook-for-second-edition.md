The *Gamemaster Handbook for Second Edition* (WEG40065) is a 256-page core reference manual published by West End Games in 1993 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Written and developed by Bill Smith, Ed Stark, Chuck Truett, Steven H. Lorenz, Eric Trautmann, and John Terra, the book serves as an exhaustive masterclass in designing, pacing, and sustaining cinematic *Star Wars* campaigns using the D6 System.

Whereas the Second Edition core rulebook focuses primarily on game mechanics, the *Gamemaster Handbook* addresses the craft of running the table. It guides gamemasters from running introductory one-shots to managing multi-year story arcs, while also answering common rules questions and helping veteran groups adapt First Edition adventures and sourcebooks into the Second Edition ruleset.

## Key Contents
- **Adventure & Campaign Architecture:** Chapters covering *Beginning Adventures*, *The Star Wars Adventure*, *Encounters*, and *Campaigns*, detailing three-act pacing, cliffhangers, recurring plot threads, and escalation.
- **Worldbuilding & Gamemaster Characters:** Dedicated sections on *Settings* and *Gamemaster Characters* for crafting memorable planets, recurring Imperial villains, allies, and supporting casts.
- **Equipment, Artifacts & Props:** Practical guidance on designing custom gear and ancient relics alongside physical table handouts and audio-visual props to heighten immersion.
- **Improvisation & Rules Guidance:** Strategies for handling unexpected player choices on the fly, paired with a *Star Wars Rules Questions* section and *The Star Wars Questionnaire* for campaign alignment.
- **Tales of the Smoking Blaster:** A complete ready-to-play adventure by Bill Smith demonstrating the handbook's design principles in action.
