*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide* is a community-produced conversion guide from The Rancor Pit that adapts Wizards of the Coast's *Star Wars Roleplaying Game: Saga Edition — Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide* (2008) for West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Built to be used alongside the original *Saga Edition* hardcover, the document converts the era's species, ancient Force powers, archaic weaponry, droids, warships, and iconic characters into D6 statistics.

Set roughly 4,000 years before the Battle of Yavin across the Great Sith War, Mandalorian Wars, Jedi Civil War, and Dark Wars, the sourcebook draws on both the *Tales of the Jedi* comics and the *Knights of the Old Republic* video games. This conversion lets D6 groups explore the ancient Republic era without abandoning classic WEG mechanics.

## Key Contents

- **Old Republic Species:** Complete D6 attribute dice and racial abilities for era-prominent species, including Arkanians, Cathar, Draethos, Feeorin, Miraluka, Rakata, Selkath, Snivvians, and Sith purebloods (Kissai and Massassi).
- **Ancient Force Powers & Traditions:** D6 *Control*, *Sense*, and *Alter* conversions for forgotten Force abilities, the Jedi Covenant, and non-Jedi mystical groups such as the Krath.
- **Era Equipment, Armor & Droids:** Converted D6 stats for vibroweapons, archaic blasters, Mandalorian battle armor, personal energy shields, and Old Republic assassin and astromech droids.
- **Starships & Vehicles:** Full D6 vehicle and starship blocks for swoops, starfighters (including the *S-100 Stinger* and *Star Saber XC-01*), light freighters, and warships like *Hammerhead*-class cruisers and *Praetorian*-class frigates.
- **Canon Personalities & Adversaries:** D6 character sheets for major figures and foes—including Revan, Darth Malak, Bastila Shan, Mandalore, Ulic Qel-Droma, Exchange enforcers, and Mandalorian neo-crusaders.
