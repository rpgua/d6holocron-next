The *Sector Guide* is the player-facing gazetteer from West End Games' 1997 *Lords of the Expanse* boxed set, written by Paul Sudlow and Eric S. Trautmann for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (2E-RE). Presented largely from an in-universe perspective, it introduces players and gamemasters alike to the history, social customs, and star systems of the Tapani Sector during the Galactic Civil War era.

Located in the Colonies region along key hyperspace lanes such as the Shapani Bypass, the sector is divided between the aristocratic provinces of the Expanse and the independent worlds of the Freeworlds Region. The guide establishes the everyday realities of Tapani life—from noble etiquette and heraldry to regional commerce and the ever-present tension between ancient traditions and Imperial rule.

## Key Contents

- **Tapani History & Culture:** A chronological overview from the founding of the Tapani Empire through its integration into the Galactic Republic and Galactic Empire, plus customs, festivals, and social hierarchy.
- **The Seven Great Houses:** Public profiles of the Greater Houses (Mecetti, Cadriaan, Melantha) and Lesser Houses (Pelagia, Barnaba, Reena, Calipsa), detailing their territories, reputations, and rivalries.
- **Planetary Gazetteer:** Descriptions of major systems, provincial capitals, industrial centers, and Freeworlds hubs across the sector.
- **Regional Gear & Vessels:** Game statistics and background for Tapani-manufactured starships, droids, lightfoils, and specialized equipment common to local characters.
