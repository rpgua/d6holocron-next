*Gamemaster Screen, Revised* (WEG40135) was published by West End Games in December 1996 to accompany *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. Combining a four-panel cardstock screen with an expanded 64-page reference booklet, the accessory updates every core mechanical table to match the final, most comprehensive official iteration of the WEG D6 ruleset.

Unlike earlier screens that leaned heavily on pre-written adventures, the 1996 revised booklet functions as a dense all-in-one tabletop reference manual. It equips gamemasters with condensed skill and Force power indexes, ready-made NPCs, starship profiles, Imperial legal codes, and blank design worksheets for building original planets, sectors, and vessels.

## Key Contents
- **Revised Four-Panel Screen:** Double-sided cardstock reference screen displaying updated *Revised and Expanded* combat, scale, difficulty, equipment cost, and starship tables for both the gamemaster and players.
- **Player Templates & Generic NPCs:** Over a dozen fresh character templates supplementing the core rulebook and *Heroes & Rogues*, plus quick-use profiles for common opponents and bystanders.
- **Skills, Force Powers & Vehicles:** Condensed reference lists of standard skills, specializations, Force powers, and combat statistics for common starships and planetary vehicles.
- **Imperial Laws & Galactic Timeline:** Detailed summaries of Imperial legal codes, restricted goods, and official punishments alongside a chronological *Star Wars* universe timeline.
- **Custom GM Worksheets:** Blank reproducible forms for creating custom starships, planets, and sector maps.
