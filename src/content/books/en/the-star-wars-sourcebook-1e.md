Published by West End Games in late 1987 alongside the launch of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, *The Star Wars Sourcebook* by Bill Slavicsek and Curtis Smith is one of the most influential reference works in Star Wars publishing history. Serving as the first comprehensive technical and background guide to the original trilogy, it established canonical names, manufacturers, species lore, and military terminology—such as Twi'leks, Quarren, Sullustans, and Imperial Star Destroyer specifications—that shaped subsequent Expanded Universe novels and games.

Written primarily as an in-universe encyclopedia paired with First Edition D6 rules, the hardcover volume provides gamemasters and players with essential statistics for running Galactic Civil War campaigns. Its 15 chapters cover everything from hyperdrive theory and droid classifications to standard Imperial garrison layouts and major film characters.

## Key Contents

- **Spacecraft & Starships**: Four chapters detailing general spacecraft systems, Rebel and Imperial starfighters (X-wing, Y-wing, A-wing, B-wing, Z-95 Headhunter, TIE series), capital combat starships, and space transports such as the YT-1300.
- **Droids & Ground Vehicles**: Technical profiles and First Edition stats for astromech, protocol, labor, and assassin droids, repulsorlift craft, and Imperial walkers (AT-AT and AT-ST).
- **Aliens & Creatures**: Playable and NPC species profiles (Wookiees, Ewoks, Mon Calamari, Jawas, Gamorreans, Ithorians, Twi'leks, and others) alongside creatures including wampas, rancors, and tauntauns.
- **Equipment & Lightsabers**: Standard adventuring gear, comlinks, medical supplies, and dedicated background and rules for lightsabers.
- **Military Forces & Characters**: Chapters on stormtrooper variants, Rebel bases (Yavin 4 and Hoth), standard Imperial garrison structures, and First Edition D6 statistics for core movie heroes and villains.
