Written by Peter M. Schweighofer and Curtis Smith and published by West End Games in June 1996 (WEG 40122), the *Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook* adapts Lucasfilm's ambitious 1996 multimedia project for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Spanning Steve Perry's novel, the Dark Horse comic series, and the companion video game, it fills the narrative gap between *The Empire Strikes Back* and *Return of the Jedi*.

With Han Solo frozen in carbonite and Luke Skywalker still recovering from his duel on Bespin, the supplement focuses on the covert struggle between Darth Vader and Prince Xizor, the charismatic Falleen Underlord of the Black Sun crime syndicate. Gamemasters receive a rich portrait of Imperial-era Coruscant, galactic organized crime, and the Rebel Alliance's desperate search for Solo.

## Key Contents

- **Rebel and Imperial Profiles:** Second Edition stats for Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa, Lando Calrissian, Chewbacca, Darth Vader, Emperor Palpatine, and key Imperial officers during the inter-film period.
- **Black Sun and the Underworld:** In-depth coverage of the Black Sun syndicate, Prince Xizor, his human-replica droid assassin Guri, Vigoes, and rival fringe operators including Dash Rendar, Leebo, and bounty hunters.
- **Planets and Imperial Coruscant:** Setting profiles for Imperial Center (Coruscant), Xizor's palace and Skyhook, Rodia, Kothlis, Bothawui, and Gall.
- **Starships and Vehicles:** Game statistics for Dash Rendar's *Outrider*, Xizor's *Virago* StarViper fighter, Boba Fett's *Slave I*, and swoop bikes.
- **Aliens, Droids, and Comics Section:** Species rules for the Falleen, profiles for human-replica and security droids, specialized gear, and a dedicated section covering characters and events from the Dark Horse *Shadows of the Empire* comics.
