*Star Wars Campaign Pack* (1988, `WEG40004`) is an early First Edition gamemaster accessory and adventure set published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Designed to bridge the gap between standalone modules and ongoing play, the pack provides both essential table references and a complete framework for launching a multi-session Rebel campaign shortly after the Battle of Yavin.

At the core of the 32-page booklet is *The Long Shot Campaign*, centered on a unit of Rebel Irregulars known as Reekeene's Roughnecks operating in the Fakir sector aboard the Alliance starship *Long Shot*. Rather than scripting every encounter in full, the booklet models how Gamemasters can build an episodic campaign: the opening scenario is fully detailed, while four subsequent episodes are supplied as structured adventure outlines covering sabotage, deep-space raids, counter-espionage, and a high-stakes rescue. Its campaign material was later reprinted and updated in *Classic Campaigns* (1995).

## Key Contents
- **First Edition Gamemaster Screen:** A full-color, three-panel reference screen compiling combat modifiers, difficulty tables, vehicle charts, and core D6 rules.
- **Full-Color *Long Shot* Floor Plans:** Large-scale deck plans depicting the player characters' home-base starship for tactical miniatures play.
- **Five Linked Campaign Scenarios:** The complete introductory script *"Tests of the Godking"* plus four adventure outlines (*"The Heat of Freedom"*, *"Assault on Repair Station M13"*, *"The Plant"*, and *"Lens Reekeene Is Missing"*).
- **Fakir Sector & Crew Dossier:** Regional background, Rebel cell leadership under General Lens Reekeene, and full stats for the *Long Shot*'s crew.
- ***Star Warriors* Combat Scenario:** A tactical starfighter encounter complete with ship stats and cardboard counters compatible with West End Games' *Star Warriors*.
