*Galaxy Guide 14: The Dark Side* is an unofficial, fan-authored sourcebook created for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Continuing the community-maintained *Galaxy Guide* numbering, this 81-page supplement fills a gap in official West End Games coverage by consolidating dark side history, traditions, villains, and expanded Force mechanics into a single reference volume.

Designed primarily as a Gamemaster resource for crafting formidable dark side antagonists—or for running darker-toned campaigns—the book draws on both Galactic Civil War lore and *Tales of the Jedi* Expanded Universe material.

## Key Contents

- **Dark Side Traditions:** Dedicated chapters examining Palpatine's Imperial dark-siders, the ancient Sith orders and philosophies, and the Krath sorcerous cult from the *Tales of the Jedi* era.
- **Dark Side Force Powers:** Expanded Control, Sense, and Alter powers tailored to corrupted Force users, alongside guidance on temptation, corruption arcs, and Dark Side Points.
- **Creatures & Dark Side Fauna:** D6 game statistics and descriptions for monstrous beasts and dark-side-warped creatures inhabiting Sith worlds and blighted regions.
- **Artifacts & Equipment:** Specialized weapons, Sith alchemy gear, and occult relics wielded by dark side practitioners.
- **Character Creation & Templates:** Rules adjustments and character templates for building dark side adepts, fallen Jedi, and corrupted NPC villains.
