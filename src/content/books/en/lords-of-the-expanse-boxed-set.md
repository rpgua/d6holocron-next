Published by West End Games in 1997 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), *Lords of the Expanse* is a comprehensive boxed campaign setting written by Paul Sudlow and Eric S. Trautmann. Set during the Galactic Civil War era, it shifts the spotlight away from the standard Outer Rim rebellion to the Tapani Sector—a wealthy, ancient Colonies-region domain ruled by rival aristocratic dynasties.

Rather than focusing purely on military conflict between the Rebel Alliance and the Galactic Empire, the set establishes a sandbox of political intrigue, corporate espionage, honor duels with lightfoils, and uneasy Imperial oversight. Its 286 pages are divided across three core softcover volumes supported by visual player aids, making it one of the most ambitious regional campaign settings produced for the D6 System.

## Key Contents

- **Sector Guide:** A player-accessible gazetteer covering the history, culture, geography, and Seven Great Houses of the Tapani Sector, along with regional equipment and ships.
- **Gamemaster Guide:** A GM-only volume detailing secret societies, local law enforcement, criminal syndicates, Imperial intelligence operations, and major non-player characters.
- **Campaign Guide:** Frameworks for running noble, corporate, or fringe campaigns, notable adventure locations, and the introductory scenario *"Hunting Grounds"*.
- **Maps and Player Aids:** A double-sided full-color poster map of the Tapani Sector (player and GM sides), character template booklets, and full-color character cards tailored to the Expanse.
