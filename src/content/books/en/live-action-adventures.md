Published by West End Games in August 1996 and written by Anthony P. Russo and Scott Heinig, *Star Wars: Live-Action Adventures* (WEG40130) is the official 128-page live-action roleplaying (LARP) adaptation of the Star Wars D6 tabletop system. Released alongside the Second Edition, Revised and Expanded era, the book translates the six core D6 attributes, character templates, and cinematic pacing of the tabletop game into a physical, improvisational format suitable for small living-room gatherings of three to ten players as well as large convention events.

Rather than relying on heavy tabletop bookkeeping, the rules emphasize in-character social interaction, secret agendas, and fast conflict resolution designed to keep scenes moving in real time. The guide serves both players preparing costumes and personas and gamemasters acting as event organizers and supporting cast directors. It was later supported by the companion volume *The Gamemaster Toolkit: Live-Action Adventures* (WEG40152), which provided ready-to-run scenarios and the Ord Mynock Freeport setting.

## Key Contents

- **Live-Action Adventuring Concepts:** Core principles for transitioning from tabletop roleplaying to physical LARP, managing play spaces, using props safely, and coordinating a "cast of thousands" of supporting NPCs.
- **Character Creation & Templates:** Adapted D6 character sheets built around the six standard attributes (*Dexterity, Knowledge, Mechanical, Perception, Strength,* and *Technical*), paired with personal goals, secret motives, Character Points, and Force Points.
- **Streamlined Conflict & Combat Resolution:** Portable mechanics for resolving blaster fire, melee encounters, skill challenges, and Force use without interrupting live-action momentum.
- **Costuming, Props & Event Logistics:** Practical advice on budget-friendly Star Wars costuming, alien makeup, blaster prop safety, and scheduling multi-hour or multi-day live-action games.
- **Scenario Design Guidelines:** Frameworks for gamemasters to write, pace, and adjudicate multi-faction plots with intersecting player objectives.
