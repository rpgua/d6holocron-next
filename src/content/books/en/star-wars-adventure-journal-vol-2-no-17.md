*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 2, No. 17* was the second of three planned West End Games issues (Numbers 16–18) cancelled in 1998 following the publisher's bankruptcy and the end of its *Star Wars* RPG license. Intended for the *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* era under editor Peter M. Schweighofer, the issue reached late editorial and layout stages before publication was halted.

Preserved through surviving editorial drafts, archival tables of contents, and community-compiled reconstructions, this unreleased issue offers a glimpse into the final wave of West End Games' Expanded Universe publishing. While some of its flagship short fiction was later rescued for official Del Rey/Bantam anthologies, its roleplaying material remained unpublished in print and survives through fan preservation efforts.

## Key Contents
- **Jade Solitaire:** Timothy Zahn's short story recounting how Mara Jade acquired her personal starship, the *Jade's Fire*, and how the Togorian H'sishi joined Talon Karrde's organization (later published in *Tales from the New Republic*).
- **Shifting Gears:** A short story by Jean Rabe following a compromised Rebel mission on Vengler alongside an assassin-droid infiltration plot on Tatooine.
- **Love Is a Warm Blaster:** A New Republic-era story by Paul Danner intertwining underworld figures with galactic political intrigue.
- **Treasure Hunt:** A short story pairing smugglers Talon Karrde and Booster Terrik on a joint expedition.
- **Special Ops: The Art of Infiltration:** Planned D6 roleplaying source material detailing espionage doctrine, covert insertion techniques, and tactical options for infiltration-focused campaigns.
