Published by West End Games in June 1993 and written by Michael Allen Horne, the *Dark Empire Sourcebook* adapts Tom Veitch and Cam Kennedy's landmark Dark Horse Comics series for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Set six years after the Battle of Endor, the supplement depicts a galaxy plunged back into crisis after the fall of Coruscant and the secret resurrection of Emperor Palpatine in cloned bodies hidden within the Deep Core.

Structured as a comprehensive era guide interspersed with in-universe narratives and a color art section, the book equips gamemasters to run campaigns during the New Republic's darkest hour—when its leadership is forced back into guerrilla warfare from Pinnacle Base while confronting both dark side adepts and apocalyptic Imperial superweapons.

## Key Contents

- **Factions and Characters:** Profiles and Second Edition statistics for New Republic leaders (Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa Solo, Han Solo, Lando Calrissian, Admiral Ackbar), the reborn Emperor Palpatine and his Imperial loyalists, and underworld figures including Boba Fett, Dengar, Salla Zend, and Shug Ninx.
- **The Force and Dark Side Lore:** Expanded rules and background covering dark side powers, Force storms, Sith holocrons, Jedi adepts, and dark side corruption.
- **Deep Core Worlds:** Planetary dossiers for pivotal locations such as the Emperor's dark side throne world of Byss, Nar Shaddaa, Calamari, and Pinnacle Moon.
- **Superweapons and Starships:** Full technical breakdowns and game stats for World Devastators, the *Eclipse*-class and *Sovereign*-class Star Dreadnoughts, E-wing starfighters, Imperial TIE/D automated fighters, and new ground vehicles.
- **Equipment and Droids:** Game profiles for weapons, armor, combat droids, and dark side technology introduced in the *Dark Empire* storyline.
