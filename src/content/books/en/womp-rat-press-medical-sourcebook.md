*Medical Sourcebook* is a fan-made rules and equipment supplement produced by Womp Rat Press for the *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* D6 System, specifically tailored to the *Revised, Expanded, and Updated* (REUP) edition. While the core D6 rules handle wound levels and basic first aid abstractly, this supplement expands the medical side of the galaxy into a detailed subsystem for groups that want grittier survival play or dedicated medic characters.

Drawing from classic West End Games sourcebooks, Expanded Universe lore, and community house rules, the book equips Gamemasters and players with comprehensive references for battlefield triage, long-term recovery, biological hazards, and cybernetic replacement. It is suitable for campaigns across any era, from Clone Wars field hospitals and Rebel Alliance medical frigates to back-alley Outer Rim clinics.

## Key Contents

- **Expanded Healing & Trauma Rules:** Optional D6 mechanics for surgical procedures, critical injuries, shock, rehabilitation, and bacta tank treatment.
- **Medical Gear & Facilities:** Game statistics, costs, and availability for medpacs, diagnostic scanners, surgical droids, portable field units, and dedicated medbays.
- **Pharmaceuticals, Toxins & Diseases:** Catalog of stimulants, antivenoms, battlefield drugs, exotic poisons, and galactic pathogens with resistance difficulties and symptoms.
- **Cybernetics & Prosthetics:** Rules and equipment entries for limb replacements, sensory implants, neural interfaces, and maintenance requirements.
- **Medical Character Options:** Guidance, specializations, and equipment loadouts for playing combat medics, xenobiologists, and cybernetic surgeons.
