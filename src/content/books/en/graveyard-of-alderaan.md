*Graveyard of Alderaan* (1991, `WEG40019`) is a First Edition adventure module published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* and later reprinted in *Classic Adventures: Volume Two*. Set during the Galactic Civil War after the destruction of Alderaan, the scenario sends a Rebel team into the hazardous asteroid field known to spacers as the Graveyard.

Rumors reach the Rebel Alliance that an intact section of the Alderaan Royal Palace has been discovered drifting among the planetary debris. In reality, the rumor is part of *Operation Yavin Kill Two*, an Imperial trap orchestrated by Darth Vader to lure out Princess Leia and high-value Alliance operatives. Amid the ambush, the characters cross paths with Alderaanian survivor Orinn Tathis and race to locate *Another Chance*, a legendary automated Alderaanian armory frigate packed with pre-disarmament military hardware.

## Key Contents

- **The Alderaan Graveyard:** Rules, navigation hazards, and encounter locations within the shattered remains of the Alderaan system.
- **Ithorian Herd Ship *Bazaar*:** Background, locations, and non-player characters for the mobile Ithorian trading vessel that serves as a staging hub.
- **The War Frigate *Another Chance*:** Lore and game details for the automated armory ship and its three slave-circuited escorts (*Valiant*, *Fidelity*, and *Courage*).
- **Cinematic Cut-Away Scenes:** Scripted interludes and Imperial subplots designed to mirror the pacing of the original films.
- **NPC Profiles and Maps:** First Edition D6 statistics for Imperial forces, Alderaanian survivors, and supporting characters, plus tactical location maps.
