*Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back* is a First Edition film sourcebook written by Michael Stern and published by West End Games in 1989 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Framed as an in-universe intelligence dossier compiled by Rebel Alliance historian Voren Na'al, the book translates the characters, military forces, bounty hunters, and key worlds of *Episode V* into First Edition D6 statistics.

Organized into six chapters plus an introduction and epilogue, the original 1989 edition follows the narrative arc of the film from the frozen trenches of Hoth to the cloud-shrouded platforms of Bespin, establishing expanded backstories for numerous background figures that became staples of *Star Wars* Legends continuity.

## Key Contents

- **Hoth Profiles:** D6 stats and backgrounds for Echo Base Rebel personnel, snowtroopers, AT-AT pilots, Imperial probe droids, and native wampas.
- **Imperial Fleet Profiles:** Detailed write-ups for Darth Vader and senior officers of Death Squadron, including Admiral Ozzel, Admiral Piett, Captain Needa, and General Veers.
- **The Bounty Hunters:** Profiles, equipment, and starship notes for the hunters summoned to the *Executor*—including Boba Fett and *Slave I*, Bossk, IG-88, Dengar, 4-LOM, and Zuckuss.
- **Dagobah & Bespin:** Setting notes and First Edition stats for Jedi Master Yoda, the swamp world of Dagobah, Lando Calrissian, Lobot, and Cloud City personnel.
- **The Heroes of Yavin:** Updated Episode V-era profiles for Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, Princess Leia, Chewbacca, C-3PO, and R2-D2.
