*Mos Eisley Adventure Set* (1997, `WEG40212`) is a Second Edition Revised & Expanded boxed supplement published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* and *Star Wars Miniatures Battles*. Designed as a dual-purpose tactical and roleplaying kit, it focuses on Tatooine's notorious spaceport during the chaotic power vacuum following the death of Jabba the Hutt in *Return of the Jedi*, though its locations and hooks can be adapted to earlier eras.

The set pairs a 32-page adventure booklet with large-scale visual aids so Gamemasters can run street-level shootouts, smuggling deals, and underworld rivalries directly on miniatures-scaled maps. It expands Mos Eisley's neighborhoods, docking bays, and criminal factions into a playable sandbox for fringe, Rebel, or New Republic groups.

## Key Contents

- **Roleplaying Scenarios:** Includes *"The Passage From Perdition"*, *"Vested Interest"*, and *"Harvest Day"*, offering underworld plots set across Mos Eisley and the surrounding Tatooine desert.
- **Miniatures Battle Scenario:** Features *"A Line in the Sand..."*, a dedicated skirmish scenario compatible with *Star Wars Miniatures Battles*.
- **Fold-Out Mos Eisley Maps:** Detailed, miniatures-scale maps depicting spaceport streets, cantina districts, and docking areas for tactical encounters.
- **Underworld NPCs and Encounters:** Second Edition Revised & Expanded statistics for bounty hunters, moisture farmers, rival crime syndicates, and local hazards.
