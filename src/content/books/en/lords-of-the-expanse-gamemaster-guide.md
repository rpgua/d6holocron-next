The *Gamemaster Guide* is the GM-only reference volume from West End Games' 1997 *Lords of the Expanse* boxed set, written by Paul Sudlow and Eric S. Trautmann for the *Star Wars* D6 system (2E-RE). While the companion *Sector Guide* presents the public face of the Tapani Sector as its citizens and visitors perceive it, this volume pulls back the curtain on the hidden power struggles driving the region during the Galactic Civil War.

It details the true motivations behind the Seven Great Houses—including House Mecetti's ruthless expansionism, House Cadriaan's commercial maneuvering, and House Pelagia's quiet opposition to Imperial tyranny—alongside the clandestine organizations operating in their shadows.

## Key Contents

- **Political & Judicial Systems:** Breakdowns of the Great Council, provincial courts, Tapani law enforcement, and how aristocratic privilege affects investigations and trials.
- **Secret Societies & Syndicates:** Profiles of covert factions, intelligence networks, house assassins, smuggling rings, and underworld groups active in the Expanse and Freeworlds.
- **Imperial & Rebel Presence:** Details on Moff-level administration, Imperial Security Bureau monitoring, and the fragile Rebel Alliance cells attempting to recruit allies among disaffected nobles.
- **Gamemaster Characters (GMCs):** Full D6 statistics, backgrounds, and roleplaying notes for key noble lords, diplomats, security chiefs, and power brokers.
- **Adventure Seeds:** Numerous plot hooks tied directly to faction agendas and unresolved historical feuds.
