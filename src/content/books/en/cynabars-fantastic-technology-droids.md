*Cynabar's Fantastic Technology: Droids* (1997, `WEG40116`) is a 96-page sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), written by Drew Campbell and Eric S. Trautmann and published by West End Games. Framed as an illicit infonet scandoc—Version 4.7.1 of *Cynabar's Droid Datalog*—compiled by the smuggler Cynabar, the book serves as a companion to *Galladinium's Fantastic Technology* while focusing exclusively on mechanical characters and automata.

Combining in-universe commentary with extensive rules expansion, the supplement explores how droids fit into galactic society, law, and everyday adventure. It supports both gamemasters looking for diverse mechanical NPCs and players who want to build, customize, or roleplay droid characters with depth and personality.

## Key Contents

- **Chapter 1 — Droids in the Galaxy:** Background on droid history, legal status, ownership conventions, memory wipes, restraining bolts, and the phenomenon of independent or rogue droids.
- **Chapter 2 — The Owner's Manual:** Practical rules and gamemaster advice covering droid maintenance, programming quirks, hardware upgrades, and integrating droids effectively into a party.
- **Chapter 3 — Roleplaying Droids:** A step-by-step droid construction and customization system for designing custom models from scratch, accompanied by guidelines for droid player characters and adventure hooks.
- **The Droid Datalog:** An illustrated catalog of dozens of droid models with full D6 statistics, organized across all five standard galactic classifications—First Degree (medical and scientific), Second Degree (astrogation and engineering), Third Degree (protocol and service), Fourth Degree (security and combat), and Fifth Degree (labor and heavy utility).
