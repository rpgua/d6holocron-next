*Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Three: Elrood Sector* (1993, `WEG40072`) is an official West End Games sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Unlike the first two volumes in the series, which presented scattered worlds across the galaxy, Volume Three focuses entirely on a single cohesive region: the backwater Elrood Sector, bound together by the Elrood–Derilyn Trade Route (known locally as the "E-D Run").

Designed as a self-contained sandbox for Rebellion-era campaigns, the book explores how Imperial expansion, martial law, and local economic rivalries shape life along an Outer Rim trade corridor. Along with Volumes One and Two, its contents were later compiled in *The Star Wars Planets Collection* (1994).

## Key Contents

- **Elrood Sector Overview:** Sector history, astrogation notes for the Elrood–Derilyn Trade Route, regional trade networks, and the impact of Imperial military occupation.
- **The Elrood Campaign:** Guidance, recurring themes, and adventure hooks for launching an extended sector-based campaign.
- **Six Detailed Star Systems:** In-depth profiles of Elrood, Cynavan (Coyn), Kidron, Merisee, Derilyn, and Kuras II—covering everything from Elrood's struggling political hub to Derilyn's transformation into a heavily fortified Imperial naval base under martial law.
- **Local Factions and NPCs:** Planetary governments, independent colonists, Imperial garrisons, and regional characters complete with Second Edition D6 game statistics.
