*Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back (Second Edition)* is West End Games' 1996 rules revision and expansion of Michael Stern's 1989 Episode V sourcebook, updated for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition* by Pablo Hidalgo. Framed in-universe as the historical research journal of Rebel Alliance historian Lieutenant Voren Na'al, the book documents the people, creatures, military forces, and worlds of *The Empire Strikes Back* (3 ABY) during the height of the Galactic Civil War.

Alongside full Second Edition D6 game statistics for film characters, droids, and wildlife, the revised edition expands background biographies, clarifies planetary entries, and adds new playable campaign material set on Bespin.

## Key Contents

- **Hoth Wastes & Echo Base:** Profiles of the Hoth system, wampas, tauntauns, Imperial Viper probe droids, and Rebel personnel including General Carlist Rieekan, Major Bren Derlin, 2-1B, FX-7, K-3PO, and Renegade Flight.
- **Battle of Hoth & Imperial Fleet:** Detailed entries for Echo Base troopers, Rogue Group snowspeeder pilots (Wedge Antilles, Zev Senesca, Wes Janson, Hobbie Klivian, Dack Ralter), cold assault stormtroopers, AT-AT drivers, Darth Vader, and Imperial fleet officers.
- **Bounty Hunters:** Complete 2E statistics and background dossiers for Boba Fett (with *Slave I*), Dengar, IG-88, Bossk, Zuckuss, and 4-LOM.
- **Dagobah & Bespin:** Planetary guides to the swamp world of Dagobah (with Yoda) and Cloud City on Bespin (covering Lando Calrissian, Lobot, Cloud City Wing Guards, and local figures).
- **Heroes of Yavin & New Adventure:** Updated Episode V stats for Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa, Han Solo, Chewbacca, C-3PO, and R2-D2, plus *"Freedom No More"*, an all-new Second Edition adventure set on Cloud City.
