*Asylum* is a fan-created adventure module produced by members of the Google+ Star Wars D6 community and preserved in the D6 Holocron archive. Written for use with *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (REUP / Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), it belongs to the wave of collaborative community releases shared prior to the closure of Google+ that kept the classic West End Games D6 ruleset active with ready-to-run scenarios.

Designed as a self-contained adventure for a standard group of player characters, *Asylum* places the party in a tense, high-stakes situation where survival, investigation, and difficult moral choices take precedence over straightforward combat. The module is structured to be dropped easily into an ongoing Galactic Civil War or fringe-sector campaign, providing Gamemasters with scene-by-scene guidance, atmospheric framing, and adaptable hooks for Rebel agents, smugglers, or independent spacers.

## Highlights

- **Ready-to-Run Scenario Structure:** Organized into clear narrative acts and encounter scenes that guide the Gamemaster from the opening hook through the climax and aftermath.
- **Gamemaster Guidance & Pacing:** Includes scene setups, investigative leads, and contingency notes to handle divergent player choices.
- **NPC Profiles & Opposition:** Provides D6 game statistics, motivations, and tactical notes for key non-player characters and adversaries encountered during the scenario.
- **Campaign Integration:** Features adaptable hooks and era-flexible framing so groups can insert the adventure into Rebel Alliance, fringe, or independent campaigns using REUP rules.
