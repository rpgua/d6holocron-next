*Classic Adventures: Volume Five* (1998, `WEG40165`) is the final anthology in West End Games' *Classic Adventures* series for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Released near the end of West End Games' license, the 128-page collection updates three foundational First Edition adventure modules with revised rules formatting, updated statistics, and new interior artwork.

Set during the Galactic Civil War, the volume brings together a prison-break aboard an Imperial warship, the origin mission of the Alliance's B-wing starfighter, and a deadly rescue operation on an Outer Rim world.

## Key Contents

- **Gamemaster Introduction**: Concise guidance on integrating the three scenarios into an ongoing Rebellion-era campaign and adjusting encounters for Revised and Expanded groups.
- ***Starfall*** (by Rob Jenkins and Michael Stern): Captured and imprisoned aboard an Imperial *Victory*-class Star Destroyer, the player characters seize the chaos of a surprise Rebel fleet attack to break out, rescue key prisoners, and escape the crippled warship.
- ***Strike Force: Shantipole*** (by Ken Rolston and Steve Gilbert): Operating in the Roche Asteroid Field under Commander Ackbar, the heroes protect the secret Verpine-engineered prototype B-wing starfighter project from Imperial discovery and escort the craft to safety.
- ***The Game Chambers of Questal*** (by Robert Kern): Volunteering to search for missing Alliance agent Tiree on the planet Questal, the characters uncover a lethal trap where they become the quarry in a deadly hunt.
- **Revised Game Statistics**: Updated 2E Revised and Expanded profiles for starfighters, Imperial personnel, Verpine technicians, and deck plans.
