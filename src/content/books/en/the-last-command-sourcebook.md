Published by West End Games in March 1994 and written by Eric S. Trautmann, *The Last Command Sourcebook* concludes the three-book adaptation of Timothy Zahn's Thrawn Trilogy for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Set in 9 ABY at the climax of Grand Admiral Thrawn's campaign against the New Republic, the volume details the desperate siege of Coruscant, the raid on Wayland, and the decisive Battle of Bilbringi.

Structured to support campaigns running alongside or immediately after the events of the novel, the sourcebook combines strategic military overviews with granular location, species, and hardware profiles. It also provides framing commentary from both Imperial and New Republic perspectives on the aftermath of Thrawn's defeat.

## Key Contents

- **New Republic, Empire & The Fringe**: Updated D6 statistics and dossiers for returning heroes, Imperial officers, dark side adversaries, smugglers, and independent operatives.
- **Tactics and Battles**: Strategic breakdowns of New Republic and Imperial military doctrines, including cloaked asteroid sieges and fleet engagements at Bilbringi.
- **Mount Tantiss & Planets**: In-depth guide to Emperor Palpatine's secret storehouse and Spaarti cloning facility on Wayland, alongside profiles of 25 featured worlds.
- **The Noghri, Aliens & Creatures**: A dedicated chapter on Noghri culture, Honoghr's ecological recovery, and key Noghri characters, plus ten alien species and beasts such as vornskrs.
- **Equipment, Droids, Vehicles & Starships**: Game statistics for cloning technology, weapons, droids, atmospheric vehicles, starfighters, and capital ships featured in the finale.
