*Jedi's Honor* (1990, `WEG40103`) is the second entry in West End Games' *Star Wars Solitaire Adventures* series for First Edition *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, published alongside *Scoundrel's Luck*. Designed for solo play without a Gamemaster, it adapts the D6 ruleset to a branching gamebook format in which numbered entries track decisions, skill checks, combat, and Force use.

Set around 0 ABY, shortly after the Battle of Yavin, the story places the reader in the role of Luke Skywalker. Dispatched by the Rebel Alliance to scout the Sil'Lume Asteroid Belt for a potential hidden base, Luke learns that Imperial forces under General Sebastian Parnell have imprisoned Erling Tredway, a local rebel agitator rumored to be the son of a Jedi Knight. The player must balance duty to the Alliance with the urge to rescue a possible Force-sensitive ally.

## Key Contents

- **Branching Solitaire Format:** Numbered story sections combining narrative choices with First Edition D6 skill rolls, starfighter encounters, and lightsaber or blaster combat.
- **Luke Skywalker Character Sheet:** Pre-generated game statistics and equipment tailored for solo play immediately following *A New Hope*.
- **Original Lore and Characters:** Introduces the Sil'Lume Asteroid Belt, General Sebastian Parnell, Erling Tredway, and Imperial pursuit forces later referenced in *Star Wars* Legends continuity.
- **Multiple Outcomes:** Replayable decision paths reflecting how well the protagonist upholds Rebel objectives and the ideals of the Jedi.
