*Live-Action Adventures: Gamemaster Toolkit* (1996, `WEG40152`) is a companion supplement to West End Games' *Star Wars Live-Action Adventures* rulebook. Published during the Second Edition Revised & Expanded era of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, it equips Gamemasters with organizational guidance, a reusable spaceport setting, and ready-to-run scenarios for live-action roleplaying (LARP) events ranging from small home sessions to convention games.

Rather than relying on tabletop dice mechanics, the toolkit focuses on freeform interaction, physical props, and streamlined task resolution suited to players moving around a shared space. Its scenarios explore familiar *Star Wars* themes—including apprentice Force-users, fallen Jedi, smuggling intrigue, and mysterious artifacts—and can be run independently or linked into a larger multi-group event.

## Key Contents

- **The Gamemaster Primer:** Practical guidance on casting, venue setup, pacing, safety, and diceless or low-friction skill resolution for live-action play.
- **Ord Mynock Freeport:** A detailed spaceport hub with recurring locations, factions, and Gamemaster character roles designed to anchor ongoing LARP sessions.
- **Four Ready-to-Run Scenarios:** Complete live-action adventure modules featuring character briefs, secret objectives, and plot timelines that scale for different player counts.
- **Photocopiable Play Aids:** Printable item cards, equipment charts, blank character sheets, and supporting character summaries for rapid event preparation.
