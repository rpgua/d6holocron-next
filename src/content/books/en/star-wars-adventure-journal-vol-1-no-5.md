*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 5* (WEG41005), published by West End Games in February 1995 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer, is the fifth quarterly issue of the anthology series for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Focusing heavily on the Outer Rim, smugglers, and independent starship crews during the Galactic Civil War, this volume supplies gamemasters with new locations, alternative starships, species profiles, and campaign hooks.

Its mix of ongoing character fiction—continuing storylines for both Alexandra Winger and Drake Paulsen—and practical D6 source material makes it a versatile resource for fringe and Rebel-aligned campaigns alike.

## Key Contents
- **Short Fiction:** Includes Patricia A. Jackson's *"A Bitter Winter"* (following Drake Paulsen to Tatooine), Charlene Newcomb's *"Turning Point"* (continuing Alex Winger's story), Peter M. Schweighofer's *"Vengeance Strike"*, and Paul Danner's *"One of a Kind"*.
- **Space Station Gazetteer:** *"Zirtran's Anchor"* by Anthony P. Russo provides a detailed setting guide to the Outer Rim space station, complete with locations, factions, and adventure hooks.
- **Starship Source Article:** *"A Buyer's Guide to Alternative Starships"* presents unconventional ship models, equipment, and D6 vehicle stats framed through the droids C-D20 and R2-RD.
- **Outer Rim & Species Lore:** *"Smugglers of the Outer Rim"* by Doug Shuler expands on fringe operations and introduces the Bosph species for D6 play.
- **Recurring Columns & Industry Interview:** Features *"Galaxywide NewsNets"*, *"Smuggler's Log"* (covering Criton's Point and Imperial Customs personnel), and *"An Inside Look at Star Wars Gaming"*, an interview with West End Games developer Bill Smith.
