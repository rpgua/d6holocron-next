*Classic Adventures: Volume Four — The Best of the Journal* (1997, `WEG40138`) is a Second Edition, Revised and Expanded anthology published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Unlike the earlier *Classic Adventures* volumes that reprinted standalone softcover modules, Volume Four curates eight standout adventures and campaign source articles originally published in the *Star Wars Adventure Journal*.

Compiled for gamemasters seeking flexible scenarios across both the Rebellion and New Republic eras, the collection covers wilderness survival, corporate intrigue, Imperial warlord politics, and frontline military operations, with all rules updated to Second Edition, Revised and Expanded standards.

## Key Contents

- ***"Stranded"*** (by Chuck Truett) & ***"Relic"*** (by George R. Strayton): Tightly focused survival and exploration scenarios easily dropped into any Outer Rim campaign.
- ***"The Kaal Connection"*** (by Peter Schweighofer and Timothy Zahn): An espionage and smuggling scenario expanding on characters and underworld threads tied to Zahn's Expanded Universe fiction.
- ***"Counterstrike"*** (by George R. Strayton) & ***"Stand at Bhir'khi Pass"*** (by Timothy S. O'Brien): Action-oriented military scenarios featuring desperate defensive engagements and Rebel strike operations.
- ***"You're in the Army Now!"*** (by James Cambias): A scenario placing player characters into the realities of frontline military service and command.
- **Campaign Sourcebooks**: Includes ***"The Gree Enclave"*** (by Timothy S. O'Brien) detailing an enigmatic ancient alien polity and ***"The Pentastar Alignment"*** (by Anthony P. Russo) profiling Grand Moff Ardus Kaine's post-Endor Imperial splinter faction.
