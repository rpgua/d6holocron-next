Written by Greg Gorden and published by West End Games in October 1989 (WEG 40006), the *Imperial Sourcebook* is one of the most influential supplements ever released for First Edition *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Framed as a collection of in-universe Imperial briefings, memos, and technical reports, it established much of the Galactic Empire's enduring military doctrine, bureaucracy, and organizational terminology across the Star Wars Expanded Universe.

Designed for campaigns set during the height of the Galactic Civil War, the book gives gamemasters an authoritative blueprint of how the Empire governs, polices, and conquers the galaxy. From the ideological apparatus of COMPNOR to the exact composition of Sector Groups and Stormtrooper legions, every section pairs rich in-universe prose with First Edition D6 statistics.

## Key Contents

- **Imperial Power and COMPNOR:** Overview of Imperial governance, regional Moffs, the Commission for the Preservation of the New Order, the Imperial Security Bureau (ISB), and CompForce.
- **Imperial Intelligence:** Structure, tradecraft, and operations of the Empire's espionage agency, including the well-known in-universe guide to reading an Imperial Intelligence scandoc.
- **Military and Sector Group Organization:** Command hierarchies for the Imperial Army and Navy, Order of Battle breakdowns, and how Sector Fleets project force across space.
- **Capital Ships and Ground Vehicles:** First Edition statistics and technical profiles for *Imperial*-class Star Destroyers, *Interdictor* cruisers, escort ships, AT-ATs, AT-STs, hovertanks, and artillery.
- **Special Forces and Indoctrination:** Coverage of the Stormtrooper Corps, Imperial Royal Guard, infantry support weapons, custom ordnance, and Imperial Academy recruitment and training.
