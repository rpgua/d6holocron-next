*Mission to Lianna* (1992, `WEG40052`) is a First Edition espionage adventure published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set during the Galactic Civil War, it marks the first detailed appearance of the industrial corporate world of Lianna—home to the powerful Santhe family and the primary headquarters of Sienar Fleet Systems, manufacturer of the Imperial TIE starfighter line.

The scenario begins as a routine intelligence drop to a local Rebel network, intended as an easy assignment for tired Alliance agents. Once on Lianna, however, the player characters uncover evidence of a clandestine Imperial research initiative developing experimental cloaking shield technology. To confirm the threat and escape alive, the team must navigate corporate security, local politics, and Imperial counter-intelligence across Lianna's urban sprawl and high-security facilities.

## Key Contents

- **Lianna World Profile:** Setting overview, planetary data, local customs, and corporate background covering Santhe/Sienar operations.
- **Espionage Campaign Arc:** Multi-part adventure emphasizing infiltration, covert investigation, social engineering, and urban evasion over open firefights.
- **Cloaking Shield Subplot:** Background and plot hooks surrounding Sienar Fleet Systems' secret Imperial weapons and stealth development program.
- **Maps and NPC Profiles:** Facility layouts, urban encounter areas, and First Edition D6 statistics for corporate security, Imperial agents, and Rebel contacts.
