*Galaxy Guide 11: Criminal Organizations* is a 1994 Second Edition sourcebook written by Rick D. Stuart for West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set roughly six years after the Battle of Endor—in the aftermath of Jabba the Hutt's death and the upheaval of the *Dark Empire* era—the book examines the galactic underworld as rival syndicates, smugglers, and cartels vie for territory between the New Republic and the fractured Empire.

Designed for both Gamemasters running fringe campaigns and players portraying underworld operatives, the supplement details the internal hierarchies, rackets, and day-to-day logistics of galactic crime, from small-time local gangs to interstellar black-market networks.

## Key Contents

- **Criminal Archetypes & Syndicates:** Profiles of underworld operators, corrupt officials, enforcers, and major organized crime groups operating across Core and Outer Rim space.
- **The Hutt Crime Empire:** In-depth coverage of the Hutt *kajidics*, their clan politics, and the power struggle triggered by the collapse of Jabba Desilijic Tiure's holdings.
- **The Invisible Market & Havens:** Mechanics and setting material for black-market commerce, spice smuggling, fencing stolen goods, and lawless frontier ports and shadowports.
- **Tools of the Trade:** D6 game statistics for specialized underworld gear, security bypass devices, weapons, and modified starships.
- **The Opposition:** Profiles of New Republic and Imperial law enforcement, customs agencies, and counter-crime forces that pursue criminal characters.
