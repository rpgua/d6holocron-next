*Riders of the Maelstrom* (1989, `WEG40021`) is a First Edition adventure module for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* published by West End Games. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the scenario blends high-society intrigue with fast-paced boarding-action combat aboard a besieged luxury vessel.

Fleeing pursuing stormtroopers, a team of Rebel agents books passage aboard the *Kuari Princess*, a grand luxury starliner bound through the turbulent, hazard-filled region of space known as the Maelstrom. Unbeknownst to the heroes, two Imperial Moffs are conducting a clandestine meeting aboard the same liner. Mid-voyage, the *Kuari Princess* is intercepted and boarded by the Riders of the Maelstrom, a ruthless pirate gang led by Big Jak Targrim. Rather than merely plundering the ship, the pirates intend to lock the starliner on a collision course with Oasis—a nearby mining moon that shelters both a rival buccaneer faction and a hidden Rebel safe world. Assisted by a mysterious Rebel operative codenamed "Dagger," the characters must outmaneuver Imperials and pirates alike to regain control of the ship before impact.

## Key Contents
- **The *Kuari Princess*:** Detailed deck descriptions, key locations, crew profiles, and First Edition D6 statistics for the iconic luxury starliner.
- **The Riders of the Maelstrom:** Background lore and complete NPC stats for Big Jak Targrim, his pirate raiders, and their assault craft.
- **The Maelstrom & Oasis:** Setting information on the hazardous nebula, navigational perils, and the threatened mining colony moon.
- **Three-Way Intrigue:** Encounters balancing covert Rebel espionage ("Dagger"), Imperial Moffs and their bodyguards, and an active pirate hijacking.
- **Gamemaster Aids:** Ship diagrams, character dossiers, and pull-out handouts for tracking the countdown to collision.
