*Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi (2nd Edition)*, published by West End Games in 1995, is the Second Edition rules revision of Michael Stern's 1990 sourcebook, updated by George Strayton for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Continuing Lieutenant Voren Na'al's in-universe historical journal, the volume documents the culminating events of the Galactic Civil War in *Return of the Jedi* (4 ABY) with revised 2E statistics, refined Force power listings, and updated vehicle and equipment rules.

The book serves as a complete Episode VI reference for Second Edition gamemasters running late-Rebellion or early-New Republic campaigns, bringing every major and minor character, droid, creature, and battlefield in line with 2E standards.

## Key Contents

- **Tatooine Underworld (2E):** Revised statistics for Jabba the Hutt, Bib Fortuna, EV-9D9, Salacious Crumb, Ephant Mon, Gamorrean guards, Weequays, the rancor, the sarlacc, and Jabba's luxury sail barge.
- **Alliance & Imperial Command:** Updated 2E profiles for Mon Mothma, Admiral Ackbar, General Madine, Wedge Antilles, Nien Nunb, Bren Derlin, Emperor Palpatine, Darth Vader, Moff Jerjerrod, Imperial Royal Guards, and biker scouts.
- **Endor & Ewok Culture:** Detailed entries for Wicket W. Warrick, Chief Chirpa, Logray, Teebo, and Paploo, along with Ewok village layouts and 2E specs for the Aratech 74-Z speeder bike.
- **The Heroes of Yavin (Episode VI):** Full Second Edition character sheets for Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa, Han Solo, Chewbacca, Lando Calrissian, R2-D2, C-3PO, and Force spirit entries for Obi-Wan Kenobi, Yoda, and Anakin Skywalker.
- **Post-Battle Scenario:** Includes the 2E-updated adventure *"Long Live the Alliance"*, set on Endor in the immediate aftermath of the second Death Star's destruction.
