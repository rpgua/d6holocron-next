*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 14* (WEG41014), published by West End Games in August 1997, is the fourteenth quarterly issue of the anthology series for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. Notably, it marks the final issue edited by longtime editor Peter M. Schweighofer, who guided the magazine from its inception.

Spanning bounty hunting, underworld intrigue, and covert military operations across the Rebellion and New Republic eras, Issue 14 pairs fiction from prominent Expanded Universe novelists with tactical D6 source material and scenario seeds.

## Key Contents
- **Featured Short Fiction:** Includes Barbara Hambly's *"Murder in Slushtime"* (featuring Callista Ming and Jos Vondar) and *"Crimson Bounty"* by Charlene Newcomb and Rich Handley, delving into the galactic underworld.
- **Adventure & Bounty-Hunting Fiction:** Features Jean Rabe's *"The Breath of Gelgelar"* and Paul Danner's *"No Disintegrations, Please"*, offering both narrative fiction and direct hooks for bounty-hunter and fringe campaigns.
- **Tactical Source Material:** *"Special Ops: Drop Points"* by John Beyer and Kathy Burdette provides mission-planning doctrine, insertion tactics, and D6 game rules for special operations teams.
- **Gamemaster Aids:** Includes campaign seeds, character profiles, and reference material compatible with *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*.
