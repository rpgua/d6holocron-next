*Goroth: Slave of the Empire* (1995, `WEG40098`) is a 96-page planetary sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition), written by Nigel D. Findley and published by West End Games. Set in the Mid Rim's Goroth system during the Galactic Civil War, the book examines Goroth Prime—a once-prosperous trade world devastated millennia earlier by a planetary war known as the Scouring and now systematically exploited by the Galactic Empire for its radioactive hyperbaride deposits.

The supplement offers a detailed portrait of Imperial colonial occupation, combining environmental catastrophe, corporate exploitation, and local political intrigue. Gamemasters can use Goroth Prime as a gritty campaign backdrop for Rebel liberation efforts, smuggling runs, espionage, or survival scenarios under heavy Imperial oversight.

## Key Contents

- **History and the World Today:** Chronological background tracing Goroth Prime from its ancient trade era and the devastation of the Scouring to its current radioactive ecology, polluted industrial zones, and mining complexes.
- **The Native Gorothites:** Comprehensive cultural, biological, linguistic, and religious overview of the indigenous Gorothite species, including their subjugation as forced labor and playable Second Edition species statistics.
- **The Empire on Goroth:** Profiles of Imperial military garrisons, administrative governors, security forces, and Imperial-backed corporate subsidiaries overseeing hyperbaride extraction.
- **The Resistance:** Details on indigenous underground movements and competing socio-political factions—including the *J'Mer Sab*, *M'Nes*, and *j'ber V'Sook*—working to oppose Imperial rule.
- **Adventure Seeds and Campaign Tools:** Location maps, key NPCs, equipment notes, and scenario hooks tailored for Rebel agents, smugglers, bounty hunters, or independent crews operating on Goroth Prime.
