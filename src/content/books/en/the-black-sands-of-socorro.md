*The Black Sands of Socorro* (1997, `WEG40154`) is a planetary sourcebook published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Written by Patricia A. Jackson, the supplement presents an atmospheric deep dive into Socorro—the scorched, black-sand Outer Rim world famed as the homeworld of smuggler Lando Calrissian's associate Drake Paulsen and a legendary haven for fringe spacers, pirates, and outlaws.

Framed with rich in-universe perspective and structured across twelve chapters plus a prologue and epilogue, the book explores Socorro's harsh desert geography, its honor-bound nomadic clans, its symbiotic underground economy, and the delicate balance between local tradition and galactic criminal syndicates. It is ideal for running gritty Outer Rim smuggling, bounty-hunting, or exploration campaigns.

## Key Contents
- **Planetary Overview & Economy:** Detailed geography, climate, and social customs of Socorro, alongside an examination of its symbiotic smuggling economy and the bustling Soco-Jarel Spaceport.
- **Settlements & Wilderness:** Gazetteer entries for Vakeyya ("A City on the Edge"), the desert wilderness, native flora and fauna, and hidden nomad camps across the Doaba Badlands.
- **Clans & Criminal Factions:** In-depth profiles of the planet's major power brokers, including the secretive Black Bha'lir smuggler guild, the Ibhaan'I nomads, Cjaalysce'I, Abdi-Badawzi, Saadoon-Kauldi, and Neftali.
- **Swoopchasing Addendum:** Specialized background and rules material dedicated to Socorro's dangerous desert swoop-racing subculture.
- **Story Starters & NPCs:** A full chapter of adventure hooks alongside D6 game statistics for local guides, guild agents, clan leaders, vehicles, and indigenous creatures.
