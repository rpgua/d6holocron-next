*The Star Wars Planets Collection* (1994, `WEG40100`) is a hardcover omnibus sourcebook published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Compiled and updated by the West End Games staff (edited by Bill Smith), the volume collects and revises the full contents of the three earlier *Planets of the Galaxy* sourcebooks—*Volume One* (1991), *Volume Two* (1992), and *Volume Three* (1993)—bringing all game statistics, alien species, equipment, and creatures into full compliance with Second Edition D6 rules.

Designed as an all-in-one galactic gazetteer and world-building toolkit, the collection spans standalone adventure settings across the galaxy alongside a cohesive regional campaign setting in the Outer Rim's Elrood Sector. It serves Gamemasters looking for immediate planetary backdrops as well as structured rules for designing original worlds.

## Key Contents
- ***Planets of the Galaxy, Volume One* Worlds:** Updated Second Edition profiles for ten diverse planets, including Baralou, Celanon, Essowyn, Garnib, Gorsh, Isen IV, Joralla, Mutanda, Rordak, and Veron.
- ***Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two* Worlds:** Revised regional profiles focusing on industrial, mining, and criminal fringe worlds such as Gacerian and Korbin, complete with local factions and adventure hooks.
- **The Elrood Sector (*Volume Three*):** A unified Outer Rim sector campaign setting detailing Elrood, Coyn, Kidron, Merisee, and neighboring systems, along with sector-wide Imperial politics, Rebel cells, and trade routes.
- **Planet Generation System:** A comprehensive, chart-driven creation toolkit allowing Gamemasters to randomly or deliberately generate star systems, planetary terrain, atmospheres, governments, tech levels, and native species.
- **Adventure Hooks & D6 Stats:** Dozens of ready-to-use scenario outlines, local NPCs, planetary vehicles, and indigenous wildlife updated for Second Edition play.
