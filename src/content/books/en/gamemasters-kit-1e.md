The First Edition *Gamemaster's Kit* (WEG40048) was published by West End Games in September 1991 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, First Edition*. Co-written by Bill Smith and Ed Stark, the release bundles a multi-panel reference screen with a companion sourcebook and campaign booklet centered on *"The Bissillirus Campaign"*—a linked series of Rebellion-era missions set in the Mid Rim's Trax sector.

Combining practical tabletop reference charts with a complete mini-campaign, the kit was designed to help gamemasters transition from standalone adventures into sustained sector-level play. Its campaign material introduces the Bissillirus system, the frontier world of Draenell's Point, and the xenophobic native Inchichtok species, and was later reprinted by West End Games in the *Classic Campaigns* compilation.

## Key Contents
- **First Edition GM Screen:** Cardstock reference screen summarizing essential 1E combat modifiers, weapon ranges, damage charts, starship rules, and skill difficulty numbers.
- **The Bissillirus Campaign Setting:** Background gazetteer detailing the Bissillirus system, the Terr'skiar Pass hyperlane, Draenell's Point, the Inchichtok species, and the Imperial Bissillirus Resupply Base.
- **Six Linked Adventure Scenarios:** A full campaign arc following Rebel operatives through *"Blades and Blasters"*, *"Rebel Enslaved!"*, *"Changing the Balance"*, *"Breakthrough in Bissillirus"*, *"All Our Sins Remembered"*, and *"Showdown"*.
- **Templates & Player Resources:** Includes Rebellion-era character templates (such as Mon Calamari, spacers, scouts, and outlaws) alongside player handouts and gamemastering notes.
