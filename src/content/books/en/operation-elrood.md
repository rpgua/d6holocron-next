*Operation: Elrood* (1996, `WEG40132`) is a Second Edition Revised & Expanded campaign book published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Written for three to six players and a Gamemaster, it serves as a companion adventure to *Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Three* (and the *Planets Collection*), bringing the remote Outer Rim Elrood Sector into active conflict during the Galactic Civil War.

Long regarded as a quiet backwater far from the main front lines, the Elrood Sector slides toward turmoil as pirate raids disrupt commercial shipping, rival corporations maneuver for control of newly charted worlds, and the Galactic Empire launches a military campaign to lock down the strategic corridor between Derilyn and Berea. Over three connected acts, the player characters move from corporate troubleshooting to a decisive strike against Imperial naval power.

## Key Contents

- **Act One: Industrial Intrigue:** Hired by the Radell Mining Corporation, the characters rescue Rebel operatives from the pirate gang known as The Scourge and race Imperial forces to claim the newly discovered world of Alluuvia.
- **Act Two: The Fixer, the Spy and the Chud:** An espionage mission requiring an uneasy alliance with a shady data broker to extract a vital Rebel spy hunted by Imperial Security Bureau (ISB) agents.
- **Act Three: Death of a Star Destroyer:** Trapped on Derilyn under Imperial martial law, the heroes seize a daring opportunity to cripple or destroy the Imperial Star Destroyer *Brazen*.
- **Sector Support Material:** Maps, ship profiles, corporate and Imperial NPCs, and adventure hooks tied directly to Elrood Sector planets.
