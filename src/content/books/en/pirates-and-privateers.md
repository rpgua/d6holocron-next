*Pirates & Privateers* (1997, `WEG40143`) is an official West End Games sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), written by Timothy S. O'Brien. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the book examines the lawless fringes of galactic shipping—covering both ruthless independent pirate gangs and state-sanctioned Rebel Alliance privateers operating under Letters of Marque against Imperial supply lines.

Serving as both a tactical manual and a campaign sourcebook, *Pirates & Privateers* provides Gamemasters and players with everything needed to run corsair-focused campaigns or introduce raider threats into standard Rebellion and fringe games.

## Key Contents

- **Pirate Life and Raiding Tactics (Chapters 1–3):** Background on pirate organizations, Rebel privateering, ambush planning, boarding actions, and shipboard culture.
- **Plunder, Gear, and Raider Ships (Chapters 4–5):** Specialized boarding equipment, contraband, cargo valuations, and D6 statistics for pirate vessels, modified freighters, and pocket warships.
- **The Opposition (Chapter 6):** Details on Imperial anti-piracy patrols, corporate security forces, and convoy defense doctrines.
- **Pirate's Gallery (Chapter 7):** Profiles and game statistics for notable pirate captains and crews, including Urias Xhaxin, Free Lance, Gunda Mabin, and others.
- **Ports of Call and Campaigns (Chapters 8–9):** Descriptions of outlaw havens such as Ord Antalaha, StarForge Station, and Virmeude, plus campaign guidelines and an index to previously published pirates.
