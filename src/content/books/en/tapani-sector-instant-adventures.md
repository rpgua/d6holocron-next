*Tapani Sector Instant Adventures* (WEG40147) is a 64-page softcover adventure anthology published by West End Games in 1997 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (2E-RE). Created to support the *Lords of the Expanse* campaign setting, the book follows West End Games' *Instant Adventures* format—providing self-contained, low-preparation scenarios that gamemasters can run as standalone one-shots or insert seamlessly into an ongoing Galactic Civil War campaign.

Each scenario highlights a distinct facet of life in the Tapani Sector, ranging from high-society galas and inheritance disputes to corporate espionage, smuggling runs, and the shadow war between rival noble houses. While tailored to Tapani lore, the adventures include hooks allowing outsiders or traditional Rebel Alliance crews to become entangled in Expanse politics.

## Key Contents

- **Blood Inheritance:** A scenario centered on noble lineage, contested claims, and dangerous family secrets among the aristocracy.
- **Lost Destiny:** An expeditionary and investigative adventure drawing player characters into hidden plots across the sector.
- **The Bacta Heist:** A fast-paced caper revolving around valuable medical cargo, underworld competition, and high-stakes double-crosses.
- **The Event of the Season:** A social-intrigue scenario set amid Tapani high society, where etiquette, rumors, and covert objectives collide.
- **The Mecetti File:** An espionage thriller involving sensitive intelligence tied to the ambitions of House Mecetti.
