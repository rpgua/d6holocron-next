*Planet of the Mists* (1992, `WEG40049`) is a First Edition adventure supplement published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the module combines wilderness exploration, diplomacy with an indigenous alien culture, and covert guerrilla action against a hidden Imperial industrial complex.

The scenario begins when Rebel operatives stumble upon a concealed Imperial hyperbaride synthesis facility on Marca, a fog-shrouded world in the Marcellus system of the Sumitra sector. The outpost is commanded by Vost Tyne, a Dark Jedi serving the Galactic Empire. Outgunned by the local Imperial garrison and hindered by Marca's dense, disorienting mists, the player characters must establish contact with the native reptilian Sekct tribes, earn their trust, and help arm and organize them to overthrow the Imperial occupation and confront Tyne directly.

## Key Contents
- **Planetary Gazetteer of Marca:** Environmental rules for navigating heavy fogs, regional geography, and profiles of native wildlife such as the *Thunderl* and *Mosrk'teck*.
- **The Sekct Culture:** Lore, tribal structure, and NPC statistics for indigenous allies, including key tribal figures who guide the heroes' insurgency.
- **Dark Jedi Antagonist:** Complete First Edition D6 character profile and Force powers for Vost Tyne, along with stats for his Imperial garrison and security forces.
- **Facility Infiltration & Uprising:** Structured episodes covering reconnaissance, diplomatic negotiations, guerrilla raids, and the final assault on the hyperbaride synthesis plant.
- **New Equipment & Game Stats:** Specialized planetary gear and technology introduced for the module, including items such as the HyperGym.
