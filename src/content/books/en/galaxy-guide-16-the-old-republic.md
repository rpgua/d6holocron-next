*Galaxy Guide 16: The Old Republic* is a large-scale fan conversion sourcebook compiled by contributors from **The Rancor Pit** community for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Because West End Games never held the license during the *Knights of the Old Republic* and *The Old Republic* eras, this community project translates thousands of years of ancient galactic history, factions, and technology into classic D6 mechanics.

Set primarily during the Cold War and Great Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the resurgent Sith Empire—following the Sacking of Coruscant—the supplement equips Gamemasters and players to run campaigns in an era where thousands of Jedi and Sith actively clash across the galaxy.

## Key Contents

- **Era Timeline & Factions:** Historical overview of the Old Republic, the Mandalorian Wars, and detailed organizational profiles for the Republic military, the Sith Emperor's Dark Council, and independent powers like the Hutts.
- **Playable Species & Templates:** D6 species profiles for era-defining aliens—including Sith Purebloods, Miraluka, Cathar, Rattataki, and Arkanians—plus character archetypes for Jedi, Sith Warriors, Republic Troopers, Smugglers, and Bounty Hunters.
- **The Force & Lightsabers:** Era-adapted Force traditions, ancient powers, and lightsaber customization guidelines reflecting the widespread presence of Force users.
- **Planetary Gazetteer:** Campaign guides and adventure hooks for key Old Republic worlds such as Tython, Korriban, Coruscant, and contested frontier sectors.
- **Starships, Droids & Gear:** Full D6 statistics for Old Republic blasters, armor, capital ships, interceptors, freighters, and battle droids.
