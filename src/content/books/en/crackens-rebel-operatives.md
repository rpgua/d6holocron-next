*Cracken's Rebel Operatives* (1994, `WEG40084`) is a Second Edition NPC sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Michael Stern and published by West End Games. Presented as a classified Rebel Alliance Intelligence dossier compiled by General Airen Cracken during the Galactic Civil War, the book provides gamemasters with a ready-to-use roster of allies, contacts, informants, and specialists operating across the galaxy.

Each dossier combines an in-universe intelligence profile—covering operational history, personality quirks, security clearances, and known associates—with full Second Edition D6 character statistics, equipment loadouts, and suggested ways to introduce the operative into a campaign as a patron, contact, rival, or playable replacement character. The book's format proved popular enough that West End Games continued publishing follow-up operative profiles in *The Star Wars Adventure Journal*.

## Key Contents

- **Military Section:** Profiles of Alliance commandos, saboteurs, guerrilla fighters, and extraction specialists suited for direct-action missions.
- **Intelligence Section:** Undercover spies, deep-cover informants, slicers, and couriers working behind Imperial lines to gather actionable intelligence.
- **Droids Section:** Notable specialized droids assigned to clandestine reconnaissance, technical support, and covert operational roles.
- **Support Services Section:** Quartermasters, medics, technicians, and logistics officers who keep scattered Rebel cells armed, supplied, and hidden.
- **Independent Operatives Section:** Freelance smugglers, information brokers, bounty hunters, and fringe assets whose sympathies or business interests align with the Rebellion.
