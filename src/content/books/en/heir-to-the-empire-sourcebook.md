Published by West End Games in November 1992 and written by Bill Slavicsek, the *Heir to the Empire Sourcebook* adapts Timothy Zahn's bestselling 1991 novel for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Taking place five years after *Return of the Jedi*, it was one of the first RPG supplements to define the post-Endor New Republic era, bridging the gap between the Battle of Endor and Grand Admiral Thrawn's return from the Unknown Regions.

Combining mechanical game data with short in-universe vignettes—including *A Grand Admiral Returns*, *Pirate's Dilemma*, and *Commando Mission*—the book lays out the fragile political state of the New Republic alongside the disciplined, calculating threat posed by Thrawn's Imperial Remnant. It remains a foundational reference for campaigns set during the Thrawn campaign.

## Key Contents

- **The New Republic:** Political structure, military leadership, and updated Second Edition profiles for Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa Solo, Han Solo, Lando Calrissian, Admiral Ackbar, and Mon Mothma.
- **Remnants of the Empire:** Detailed dossiers and stats for Grand Admiral Thrawn, Captain Gilad Pellaeon, Imperial military forces, and tactical assets deployed against the New Republic.
- **The Fringe and Underworld:** Profiles for Talon Karrde's smuggling organization, Mara Jade, and independent fringe operators navigating the post-Imperial economy.
- **The Force and Force-Blocking Biology:** Rules and lore covering Luke Skywalker's Jedi progression, dark side threats, and the Force-neutralizing ysalamiri and vornskrs of Myrkr.
- **Planets and Species:** Setting guides and game statistics for key worlds such as Myrkr, Wayland, Sluis Van, and Nkllon, along with Noghri commandos and Bothan operatives.
