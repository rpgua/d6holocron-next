*Galaxy Guide 13: Rogue One* is an unofficial fan-produced sourcebook created by Misfit Toys and hosted through The Rancor Pit community for West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 System / Revised, Expanded, and Updated). Styled in the classic layout and editorial tradition of West End Games' original *Galaxy Guide* series—which officially concluded with *Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens—Enemies and Allies*—this supplement brings the characters, locations, hardware, and espionage-driven tone of *Rogue One: A Star Wars Story* (2016) to the D6 tabletop.

Set immediately prior to the Battle of Yavin (0 BBY), the book focuses on the gritty, high-stakes side of the early Galactic Civil War, highlighting Rebel Alliance intelligence operations, partisan cells, and the Imperial security apparatus guarding the Death Star project.

## Key Contents

- **Heroes & Rebel Factions**: Full D6 character templates, stats, and backgrounds for key figures including Jyn Erso, Captain Cassian Andor, K-2SO, Chirrut Îmwe, Baze Malbus, Bodhi Rook, and Saw Gerrera's Partisans.
- **Imperial Forces**: Game statistics for Director Orson Krennic, Imperial death troopers, coastal defender shoretroopers, Imperial combat assault tank pilots, and garrison personnel.
- **Key Locations**: Campaign gazetteer entries for pivotal settings from the film, including the holy moon of Jedha, the rain-swept research world of Eadu, the trading outpost Ring of Kafrene, and the heavily fortified tropical archive world of Scarif.
- **Starships & Vehicles**: Complete D6 profiles for iconic craft such as the UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft, TIE Striker, Imperial *Zeta*-class cargo shuttle, TX-225 GAVw Occupier combat assault tank, and AT-ACT walker.
