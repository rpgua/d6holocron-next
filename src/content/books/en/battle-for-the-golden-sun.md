*Battle for the Golden Sun* (1988, `WEG40017`) is a First Edition adventure module for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Douglas Kaufman and published by West End Games. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the scenario takes a team of Rebel Alliance agents to the remote, unexplored ocean world of Sedri after a navigational accident draws Imperial forces to the planet.

Intelligence reports suggest that Sedri conceals the secret of the "Golden Sun," a mysterious power that could tip the balance of the war in the Empire's favor. When an impetuous young Alliance dignitary disappears while investigating the rumors, the player characters are dispatched with a half-sane Mon Calamari guide to rescue the envoy, navigate local factional politics among the aquatic Sedrians, and prevent Imperial occupation forces from claiming the planet's secret.

## Key Contents

- **Complete Planetary Scenario**: A 40-page adventure booklet structured into linked episodes covering arrival on Sedri, underwater diplomacy, espionage, and a climax inside the Sun Caves.
- **Introductory Script**: A playable opening scene with assigned dialogue roles to establish the rescue mission and brief the players in-character.
- **Sedri Setting & Species**: Background lore, environmental hazards, underwater village descriptions, and game statistics for the native Sedrians and local aquatic wildlife.
- **Imperial Sea Garrison**: Full-color tactical battle map and unit profiles for Imperial aquatic troopers, garrison defenses, and submersibles.
- **Rules Insert**: A 4-page pull-out pamphlet providing specialized underwater combat, movement, and equipment rules for First Edition D6 gameplay.
