*Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races (2nd Edition)*, published by West End Games in 1994, is the Second Edition rules revision of Troy Denning's 1989 species compendium, updated and expanded by Chuck Truett. Retaining the Rebellion-era in-universe framing of Senior Anthropologist Obo Rin's *Catalog of Intelligent Life in the Galaxy*—now presented as an updated Imperial *Revised Edition*—the book converts the entire roster of over thirty sentient species to the standardized mechanical format introduced in *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*.

Rather than altering the species list, the Second Edition reorganizes and deepens each entry with clearer mechanical rules, expanded notes on temperament and behavioral psychology, and practical gamemaster advice for integrating alien characters into ongoing campaigns.

## Key Contents

- **Standardized 2E Species Format:** Complete Second Edition stat blocks for over thirty species (including Abyssins, Aqualish, Arcona, Bith, Duros, Hutts, Mon Calamari, Rodians, Trandoshans, Twi'leks, Verpine, and Whiphids) with explicit attribute minimums and maximums.
- **Special Abilities & Story Factors:** Formalized mechanical rules for natural armor, heightened senses, regeneration, environmental adaptations, Movement rates, physical scale, and roleplaying Story Factors.
- **Expanded Cultural & Behavioral Lore:** Reorganized entries placing greater emphasis on species psychology, social norms, personality tendencies, and galactic reputation.
- **Gamemaster Guidance:** Dedicated GM notes offering rationales, adventure hooks, and balance guidelines for running each species as player characters or recurring NPCs.
- **Updated Imperial Framing:** Revised introductory communiqués from Obo Rin and Darth Vader contextualizing the catalog within the Imperial era.
