Published by West End Games in April 1994 (WEG 40091), the *Rebel Alliance Sourcebook (2nd Edition)* is Peter M. Schweighofer's Second Edition revision of Paul Murphy's original 1990 companion to the *Imperial Sourcebook*. Anchored in the classic Galactic Civil War timeframe following the Battle of Yavin, it presents the Alliance to Restore the Republic through in-universe documents, intelligence files, and operational manuals.

This revised edition updates all character profiles, starship specifications, vehicle stats, and equipment mechanics to the *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition) ruleset, while updating layout and trade dress and making minor continuity adjustments to align with WEG's mid-1990s line. It provides players and gamemasters with the organizational depth needed to run authentic Rebel cell, starfighter, or commando campaigns.

## Key Contents

- **Birth and Government of the Rebellion:** History of the Corellian Treaty, the Declaration of Rebellion, Mon Mothma's Civil Government, and the Alliance Cabinet's ministries.
- **Alliance Intelligence:** Structure of Rebel espionage, clandestine cell networks, cryptology, counter-intelligence, and field agent operations against Imperial security.
- **Alliance Military and SpecForce:** Command hierarchy of High Command, Fleet Command, Sector Forces, and elite Special Forces divisions including Pathfinders, Heavy Weapons Specialists, and Urban Guerrillas.
- **Starships and Starfighter Corps:** Second Edition statistics for Mon Calamari Star Cruisers, Nebulon-B frigates, Corellian corvettes, and X-wing, Y-wing, A-wing, and B-wing squadrons.
- **Bases, Support Services, and Gear:** Operational details for hidden Rebel bases, medical and logistics support, droids, ground combat vehicles, and guerrilla equipment.
