Originally published by West End Games in September 1993 as a 160-page hardcover (and later reprinted in softcover), *The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook* consolidates, revises, and updates material from *Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope*, *Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back*, and *Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi* into a single volume for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Written by Grant S. Boucher and Michael Stern, it serves as an all-in-one tabletop reference for the entire original film trilogy.

Framed in-universe as a historical report compiled by New Republic Director of Council Research Voren Na'al for Master Historian Arhul Hextrophon, the book traces the Galactic Civil War from the theft of the Death Star plans to the Battle of Endor. Alongside updated Second Edition D6 statistics, the volume includes film stills, pre-production concept art, poster reproductions, and short framing fiction.

## Key Contents

- **Heroes and Villains**: Revised Second Edition D6 profiles and biographies for major and supporting characters across all three films, including Rebels, Imperials, scoundrels, and bounty hunters.
- **Key Locations & Installations**: Detailed breakdowns of iconic settings such as the first Death Star, Echo Base on Hoth, Jabba the Hutt's palace on Tatooine, and the forest moon of Endor.
- **Military Forces & Battles**: Coverage of the Imperial Fleet, Rebel strike forces, planetary defenses, and major engagements at Yavin, Hoth, and Endor.
- **In-Universe Fiction & Visuals**: Framing narratives by Voren Na'al, short stories such as *Camie's Story* and *The Letter Home*, movie stills, concept art, and full-color reproductions of original theatrical posters.
