*Classic Campaigns* (1995, `WEG40108`) is an official West End Games compilation for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition), authored by Paul Murphy, Bill Smith, and Ed Stark. Designed to bring foundational First Edition campaign material into the Second Edition ruleset, the volume collects and updates the classic *Campaign Pack* and *Gamemaster Kit* campaign arcs, set during the height of the Galactic Civil War.

Rather than presenting isolated one-shots, *Classic Campaigns* focuses on sustained, multi-session play. It opens with practical gamemaster guidance on structuring long-term Star Wars campaigns, managing recurring antagonists, and balancing narrative pacing before diving into two complete campaign frameworks that can be run independently or woven into an ongoing Rebel Alliance storyline.

## Key Contents

- **Running a Star Wars Campaign:** Practical advice for gamemasters on campaign planning, character progression, recurring story arcs, and integrating player backgrounds into galactic events.
- **The Long Shot Campaign:** Complete Second Edition update for the adventures aboard the modified space yacht *Long Shot*, including crew dossiers, ship specifications, an overview of the Fakir Sector, adventure outlines, and the scenario *Tests of the Godking*.
- **Rebel Cell Construction:** Framework and operational guidance for establishing, supplying, and expanding a clandestine Rebel cell within Imperial-controlled space.
- **The Bissillirus Campaign:** An eight-part linked mission sequence (*Changing the Balance*, *Rebel Enslaved!*, *Return to Bundim*, *Going My Way?*, *Blades and Blasters*, *Breakthrough in Bissillirus*, *All Our Sins Remembered*, and *Showdown*) tracking Rebel efforts to sabotage an Imperial resupply base.
- **Updated 2E Statistics:** Revised Second Edition game stats for NPCs, starships, planetary locations, and Imperial opposition across both classic campaign sets.
