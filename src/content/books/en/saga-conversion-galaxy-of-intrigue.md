*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Galaxy of Intrigue* is a fan-compiled conversion document created by The Rancor Pit community to translate Wizards of the Coast's *Star Wars Roleplaying Game: Saga Edition — Galaxy of Intrigue* (2010) into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Designed as a mechanical companion to the original *Saga Edition* hardcover, it provides complete D6 statistics for the sourcebook's covert gear, political factions, NPCs, and espionage scenarios so D6 groups can run intrigue-driven campaigns in any *Star Wars* era.

Whereas many *Star Wars* supplements emphasize starship combat and frontline battles, *Galaxy of Intrigue* centers on espionage, diplomacy, political maneuvering, and covert operations. The Rancor Pit conversion preserves that focus by adapting non-combat conflict systems and specialized spy equipment into D6 skill checks and difficulty tiers.

## Key Contents

- **Espionage & Social Character Options:** D6 adaptations of character templates, skill specializations, and covert abilities suited for spies, diplomats, slicers, and political operatives.
- **Skill Challenges in D6:** Guidelines for translating *Saga Edition*'s structured Skill Challenge framework into multi-stage D6 skill rolls for negotiations, infiltrations, and investigations.
- **Covert Equipment & Spycraft Gear:** D6 statistics for surveillance devices, concealed weapons, slicing tools, counter-espionage gadgets, and disguised protective clothing.
- **Factions & World of Nyriaan:** Converted D6 NPC profiles and operational stats for major intelligence networks (such as the Bothan SpyNet), syndicates, and key figures on the intrigue-ridden planet Nyriaan.
- **Intrigue Encounters & The Perfect Storm:** Ready-to-use D6 stats and difficulty benchmarks for the sourcebook's modular stealth/social encounters and the included adventure *The Perfect Storm*.
