*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: The Unknown Regions* is a fan-compiled conversion guide from The Rancor Pit community adapting Wizards of the Coast's *The Unknown Regions* (2010)—the final official *Star Wars Saga Edition* sourcebook—into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Used alongside the original volume for regional lore and planetary descriptions, it converts the book's species, survival hardware, exploration tools, and uncharted-space threats into D6 mechanics and stat blocks.

Built as a sandbox toolkit for frontier and deep-space exploration across any era of the *Star Wars* timeline, the conversion brings uncharted hyperspace lanes, isolated alien civilizations, and bizarre planetary hazards to the D6 tabletop.

## Key Contents

- **Frontier & Obscure Species**: D6 attribute ranges and special abilities for deep-space and fringe species including Anzati, Chadra-Fan, Chiss, Shistavanen, Sluissi, Ssi-ruuvi, Verpine, and Krevaaki, plus a flexible Near-Human customization framework.
- **Explorer's Equipment**: Converted statistics for survival suits, deep-space navigation gear, scout vehicles, and specialized field technology designed for extended operations beyond galactic supply lines.
- **Planetary & Creature Generators**: D6 adaptations of the world-building and creature-generation tables used to create uncharted star systems, biomes, and alien wildlife on the fly.
- **Environmental Hazards**: Converted mechanics for space anomalies, hostile atmospheres, and extreme planetary conditions resolved through D6 skill checks and damage codes.
- **Regional Threats & Factions**: D6 stat blocks for powers operating in uncharted space, including the Chiss Ascendancy, Ssi-ruuvi Imperium, Vagaari raiders, Killiks, the Sorcerers of Rhand, and remnants tied to Lehon (Rakata Prime).
