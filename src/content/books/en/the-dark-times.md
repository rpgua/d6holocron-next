*The Dark Times* is a fan-made campaign sourcebook produced by the Google+ Star Wars D6 community for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (REUP and Second Edition). While West End Games' original product line centered primarily on the Galactic Civil War of the Original Trilogy, *The Dark Times* expands the D6 ruleset into the tumultuous nineteen-year period immediately following *Episode III: Revenge of the Sith*, covering the fall of the Galactic Republic, the rise of Palpatine's Galactic Empire, and the Great Jedi Purge.

Built to support gritty survival and resistance campaigns, the sourcebook explores a galaxy in rapid political and military transition—where clone troopers give way to Imperial stormtroopers, former Republic worlds adjust to authoritarian rule, and nascent dissident cells have not yet united into the Rebel Alliance. It gives Gamemasters the setting background and mechanical tools needed to run stories starring hunted Jedi survivors, disenfranchised clone veterans, smugglers, and early rebels.

## Key Contents

- **Early Imperial Era Setting:** Details the political, military, and social shifts following Order 66 as the Empire consolidates control over the Core and Outer Rim.
- **Surviving the Great Jedi Purge:** Offers campaign guidance and rules considerations for Force-sensitive fugitives hiding their abilities while evading Darth Vader and the Imperial Inquisitorius.
- **Era-Specific Templates & NPCs:** Presents character archetypes and adversary profiles suited to the transition from the Clone Wars to Imperial occupation.
- **Transitional Gear & Starships:** Provides D6 statistics for surviving Republic-era equipment, early Imperial war machines, and frontier hardware commonly found during the Dark Times.
