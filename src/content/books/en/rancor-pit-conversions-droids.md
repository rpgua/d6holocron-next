*Rancor Pit Conversions: Droids* (`rp_droids`) is a community-compiled technical catalog created by contributors at The Rancor Pit for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). As part of the Rancor Pit Conversion Books series, it translates mechanical constructs introduced in the Prequel Trilogy, *The Clone Wars*, Expanded Universe media, and Wizards of the Coast's d20 and Saga Edition rulebooks into standard West End Games D6 statistics.

Whether a gamemaster needs Separatist battle droids for a Clone Wars campaign, specialized astromech and protocol units for a starship crew, or assassin and security droids as antagonists, the compilation organizes these models using familiar WEG droid degree classifications and equipment rules.

## Key Contents

- **All Five Droid Degrees:** Converted models spanning First-Degree medical/scientific units, Second-Degree astromech and technical droids, Third-Degree protocol droids, Fourth-Degree combat/security droids, and Fifth-Degree labor models.
- **Standardized D6 Stat Blocks:** Full attribute dice, programmed skills, Move values, height, cost, and availability ratings formatted for 2E-RE play.
- **Chassis & Built-In Equipment:** Lists of integrated appendages, sensor packages, vocabulator types, armor plating, and concealed or mounted weaponry.
- **Playable & NPC Utility:** Entries structured both for quick gamemaster deployment as adversaries or allies and as starting chassis references for players creating droid characters.
