*Platt's Smugglers Guide* (1997, `WEG40141`) is an official West End Games sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), written by Peter M. Schweighofer. Serving as a companion to *Platt's Starport Guide*, the book is narrated in-universe by veteran smuggler Platt Okeefe and offers players and Gamemasters a practical handbook for building and running fringe, trader, and smuggling campaigns during the Galactic Civil War.

Blending in-character streetwise advice with concrete D6 mechanics, the supplement expands every facet of a smuggler's career—from character origins and ship customization to managing underworld contacts and surviving Imperial customs crackdowns.

## Key Contents

- **Character Development and Origins (Chapters 1–2):** Guidelines for crafting smuggler backgrounds, career motivations, and crew dynamics.
- **Your Ship (Chapter 3):** Starship modification advice, smuggling compartments, and deck plans for classic light freighters including the Corellian YT-1300 and the Ghtroc 720.
- **Tools of the Trade (Chapter 4):** Specialized smuggling gear, concealment accessories, droids, and emergency equipment with full D6 statistics.
- **Contacts, Rivals, and Enemies (Chapters 5–6):** Rules and profiles for cultivating underworld networks while dealing with bounty hunters, crime lords, and Imperial authorities.
- **Character Templates (Chapter 7):** Eighteen new player character templates covering diverse smuggler, pilot, and starship crew roles.
