*Adventurer's Journal Issue 5* (subtitled *Mercenaries & Miscreants*, product code `AJ050420`) is the fifth issue of the fan-produced *Adventurer's Journal* anthology for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP), released in May 2020. Focusing on soldiers of fortune, smugglers, bounty hunters, and galactic outlaws, the issue delivers ready-to-use NPCs, unusual starships, prison-break hooks, and skirmish scenarios tailored to fringe campaigns.

Building on the shared locations established in earlier *Adventurer's Journal* releases—including Rotgut Station from *Galaxy Guide 1*—the issue provides gamemasters with modular threats, allies, and mobile adventure sites that can be inserted into Rebellion, New Republic, or Old Republic era games with minimal adaptation.

## Key Contents
- **Ships & Mobile Locations:** *Durance Vile: A Mobile Prison Ship* by Mathew Wedel, detailing a heavily guarded starship suitable for breakout or infiltration missions, and *Jawa Star Crawler* by Dustin Hawk.
- **Mercenaries, Rogues & Contacts:** *The Mercenary Lotus* by Ray Napoli, *Arev Praxx & LE-5 — Droid & Smuggler* by Julian Smith, and *Allies, Assets & Obstacles - 42* by Flynn O'Dinson, offering statted NPCs and campaign hooks.
- **Solo Adventure:** *Escape From Rotgut Station*, a solitaire scenario by Mark Dowson revisiting the frontier hub introduced in *Galaxy Guide 1*.
- **Miniatures Scenario & Aids:** *Acquiring The Asset*, a tactical scenario for *Star Wars Miniatures Battles* by David O. Little, accompanied by a blank D6 / REUP character sheet.
