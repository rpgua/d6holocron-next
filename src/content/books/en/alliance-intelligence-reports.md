Published by West End Games in June 1995 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*, *Alliance Intelligence Reports* is a 95-page supplement set roughly two years after the Battle of Yavin (2 ABY). Written by Craig Robert Carey (with development by Bill Smith), the book is formatted as an in-universe collection of classified datafiles compiled by the Rebel Alliance's Task Force on Alliance Security.

Unlike traditional Galactic Civil War supplements focused primarily on the Galactic Empire, *Alliance Intelligence Reports* spotlights non-Imperial threats endangering Rebel operations: criminal syndicates, slaver rings, mercenary outfits, hostile alien sects, and independent corporate actors. Each dossier pairs in-universe intelligence memos and short vignettes with ready-to-use Second Edition D6 statistics and campaign hooks.

## Key Contents

- **Task Force on Alliance Security**: Background on the specialized Alliance Intelligence unit monitoring external threats, including profiles of key operatives and members of the elite Eclipse Team.
- **Non-Imperial Threat Dossiers**: Detailed briefings on criminal, mercenary, and independent groups—including the Brosin Underground, Granse Confederacy, Hydrospeare Corporation, Mytaranor Slaving Council, m'Yalfor'ac Order, Ploovo's "Protocol Team," and the Zulirian Swordmasters.
- **NPCs, Species & Locations**: Second Edition D6 character statistics for notable adversaries (such as the Venithon Twins and Ssach'thirix), new alien species like the Ocsinin and Kalduu, and locales including Ichalin Station and the Fakir sector.
- **Custom Gear & Adventure Hooks**: Unique droids, vehicles, and equipment used by these factions, along with gamemaster notes and short in-universe stories such as *Graduation* and *An Offer They Couldn't Refuse*.
