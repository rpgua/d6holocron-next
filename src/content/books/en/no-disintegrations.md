*No Disintegrations* (1997, `WEG40151`) is a Second Edition Revised & Expanded adventure anthology published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Taking its title from Darth Vader's warning to Boba Fett in *The Empire Strikes Back*, the book shifts the spotlight away from Rebel Alliance missions to focus on professional bounty hunters and fringe operatives working during the Galactic Civil War prior to the Battle of Endor.

Each scenario is designed as an open-ended hunt emphasizing investigation, tracking, underworld negotiation, and tactical capture rather than linear dungeon crawls. Gamemasters can run the five adventures as standalone contracts or weave them into an ongoing bounty hunter campaign alongside *Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters*.

## Key Contents

- **Five Bounty Hunter Adventures:** Includes *"Gone to Ground"*, *"Abregado-Rae Intrigue"*, *"Seregar Turnabout"*, *"The Black Sphere"*, and *"Elusive"*, each presenting distinct targets, rival hunters, and moral complications.
- **Open-Ended Contract Design:** Flexible layouts allowing players to choose between stealth, deception, negotiation, or direct confrontation to bring in targets dead or alive.
- **Appendix: Additional Bounties:** Supplemental bounty postings, target profiles, and adventure hooks featuring quarries such as Narloch of Giju, Ort Hoogra-D'En, and Hahz Fallone.
- **2E Revised & Expanded Statistics:** Complete game stats for marks, competing hunters, criminal contacts, ships, and specialized capture gear.
