*The Scavenger's Handbook: A Guide to Salvage in the Galaxy* is a fan-made rules and setting supplement archived within the Google+ Star Wars D6 community collection for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (REUP). Drawing inspiration from classic fringe-focused West End Games supplements such as *Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters* and *Platt's Smugglers Guide*, it focuses on one of the galaxy's most perilous professions: stripping derelict starships, battlefields, and junk worlds for profitable technology.

Designed for Gamemasters and players running Outer Rim or frontier campaigns, the handbook turns scavenging from a background detail into a central campaign loop. It examines the daily realities of the salvager's lifestyle—navigating orbital debris fields, competing with rival crews, dodging Imperial patrols, and repairing worn-out equipment—while providing mechanics to adjudicate salvage operations and turn recovered wrecks into credits or ship upgrades.

## Key Contents

- **The Salvager's Lifestyle & Setting:** Explores salvage guilds, junkyard worlds, scrap markets, and the hazards of working derelict hulls and post-battle debris fields.
- **Salvage & Recovery Mechanics:** Provides structured guidelines for locating wrecks, assessing salvageable components, extracting intact systems, and fencing recovered hardware.
- **Specialized Scavenging Gear:** Details practical tools of the trade, including cutting torches, environmental suits, tow cables, diagnostic scanners, and improvised frontier equipment.
- **Salvage Vessels & Campaign Hooks:** Profiles utilitarian workhorses such as salvage tugs and recovery craft alongside adventure ideas for independent crews scraping by on the galactic rim.
