*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 1* (product code `WEG41001`, ISBN `0-87431-400-3`) is the premiere issue of West End Games' quarterly *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* magazine, published in February 1994 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer. Designed for the Second Edition D6 ruleset, the journal launched a landmark 15-issue run that bridged tabletop gaming and Expanded Universe fiction, introducing recurring characters, smugglers, and Rebel Intelligence dossiers that became staples of *Star Wars* Legends continuity.

Set primarily during the Galactic Civil War and early New Republic eras, the inaugural issue pairs short stories by major *Star Wars* authors—most notably Timothy Zahn—with ready-to-play adventures, a solitaire scenario, and the debut installments of several long-running sourcebook columns.

## Key Contents
- **Short Fiction:** Timothy Zahn's *First Contact* (featuring Talon Karrde investigating a Krish gunrunner), Charlene Newcomb's *A Glimmer of Hope*, Paul Balsamo's *Escape from Balis-Baurgh* (following an Ewok and a Rebel scout escaping an Imperial prison), and Peter M. Schweighofer's framing story *Chessa's Doom* (introducing Dirk Harkness and the Black Curs).
- **Smuggler's Log:** The debut installment by Peter M. Schweighofer introducing smuggler Platt Okeefe, her light freighter, and the cantina *Lorana's Labyrinth* on Kelada.
- **Wanted by Cracken:** The first magazine installment of General Cracken's Rebel Intelligence dossiers, profiling bounty hunter Cragus 12, his starship *The Claw*, and his security droid GX-99.
- **Adventures & Source Articles:** *Glah Ubooki's Strange & Wondrous Imports* by Shane Hensley, a solitaire scenario (*Regina Cayli*), and additional D6 adventure hooks, NPC profiles, and gaming articles by Paul Sudlow, Nicky Rea, Chuck Truett, Gary Haynes, and Ken Rolston.
