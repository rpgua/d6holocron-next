*Platt's Starport Guide* (1995, `WEG40107`) is an official West End Games sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition), written by Peter M. Schweighofer. Presented from the in-universe perspective of veteran smuggler Platt Okeefe, the book serves as a spacer's field manual to navigating starports, dodging Imperial bureaucracy, and finding work along the galaxy's hyperlanes during the Rebellion era.

In addition to providing Gamemasters with seven contrasting starport settings complete with maps, NPCs, and local services, the supplement establishes core procedures for spacefaring regulations, ship licensing, customs inspections, and starship repairs.

## Key Contents

- **Space Travel, Regulations, and You:** Detailed rules and lore covering the Imperial Space Ministry, the Bureau of Ships and Services (BoSS), starport classifications, operating licenses, arms permits, and customs infractions.
- **Seven Featured Starports:** Full profiles, maps, diagrams, commodities, and dockside contacts for Port Haven, Kuat, Gelgelar Free Port, Omze's Incredible Traveling Starport, Byblos Starport Tower 214, Darknon Station, and Kala'uun on Ryloth.
- **Included Adventure Scenarios:** Ready-to-run adventures tied to each port, including *Port Haven Betrayal*, *Setting Up Home*, *Cover Compromised*, *Kooroo Killer*, *Lady Felsing's Rescue Plan*, *Byblos Breakout*, *Into the Nebula*, and three scenarios set in Kala'uun.
- **Gamemaster Reference Rules:** Supplementary mechanics and a consolidated summary of starship repair rules for dockside downtime.
