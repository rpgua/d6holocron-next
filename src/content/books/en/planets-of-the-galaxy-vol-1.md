*Planets of the Galaxy, Volume One* (1991, `WEG40050`) is an official West End Games sourcebook for the First Edition of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Grant S. Boucher, Julie Boucher, and Bill Smith. As the inaugural installment in WEG's planetary gazetteer series, the book was later updated and collected alongside Volumes Two and Three in *The Star Wars Planets Collection* (1994).

In addition to expanding the galaxy with ten fully realized star systems for Rebellion-era campaigns, the supplement introduces a comprehensive toolkit enabling Gamemasters to design custom worlds from scratch, complete with standardized planetary data logs and reference tables.

## Key Contents

- **Planet Generation System:** Step-by-step rules, random generation charts, and a blank Planet Log sheet for creating original star systems, climates, governments, and economies.
- **Ten Detailed Worlds:** Full planetary profiles for Baralou, Celanon, Essowyn, Garnib, Gorsh, Isen IV, Joralla, Mutanda, Rordak, and Veron.
- **Diverse Environments and Cultures:** Covers settings ranging from the isolated tropical ocean world of Baralou and the cutthroat trade hub of Celanon to Garnib's artisan Balinaka culture and the Void Demon pirates of the Isen IV system.
- **Ready-to-Play Adventure Material:** Each planetary entry includes starport details, native species, local factions, points of interest, diagrams, and adventure hooks.
