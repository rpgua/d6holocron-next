*Death in the Undercity* (1990, `WEG40037`) is a 64-page First Edition adventure module for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Michael Nystul and published by West End Games. Set on the ocean world of Calamari (Mon Cala) during the Galactic Civil War, the scenario explores the tense political and cultural divide between the surface-dwelling Mon Calamari and the deep-sea Quarren.

While the Mon Calamari shipyards supply the Rebel Alliance with vital MC80 Star Cruisers, the raw ores required for construction are mined by Quarren workers in the submerged undercities. When a wave of destructive mining accidents stalls production and the player characters are framed for a politically charged double murder, the Rebel agents must evade hostile factions, navigate treacherous underwater facilities, and expose the true saboteurs before civil war erupts and cripples the Alliance fleet.

## Key Contents

- **Investigative Underwater Scenario**: A multi-part mystery and political thriller moving from floating Mon Calamari cities down into the pressurized Quarren mining complexes.
- **Quarren Species & Culture**: Expanded First Edition background material, social structure notes, and game profiles detailing the Quarren and their grievances against the Mon Calamari.
- **Underwater Maps & Diagrams**: Tactical blueprints of submerged mining stations, undercity transit corridors, and key encounter locations.
- **Gamemaster Aids & NPCs**: Includes an introductory script, pre-generated NPC profiles, environmental hazard guidelines, and campaign hooks; later reprinted and updated in *Classic Adventures: Volume Three* (`WEG40133`).
