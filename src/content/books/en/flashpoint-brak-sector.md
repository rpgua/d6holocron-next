*Flashpoint! Brak Sector* (1995, `WEG40077`) is a Second Edition sector sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Sterling Hershey and published by West End Games. Set in the immediate weeks following the Battle of Yavin (0 ABY), the book presents the Brak Sector—a strategic staging region near the Expansion Region and the Slice—as a self-contained sandbox campaign setting defined by escalating conflict among the Galactic Empire, a powerful mining conglomerate, local criminal syndicates, and an emerging Rebel cell.

Rather than focusing on a single linear storyline, the supplement maps out competing factions, key personalities, and flashpoint locations so gamemasters can run guerrilla warfare, corporate espionage, smuggling, or bounty-hunting campaigns across an interconnected regional theater.

## Key Contents

- **The Empire in Brak Sector:** Breakdown of Imperial naval assets, garrisons, and sector leadership—including Moff Lesan Ramier, Admiral Garrik Trier, General Andrel Lacmar, and ISB Agent Cass Maston—plus coverage of the isolationist Aramandi species in the Aramand Cluster.
- **The Rebel Alliance:** Background on General Trep Reskan's regional resistance, hidden installations such as Oracle Base in the Tel system, key Rebel officers, and operations including the *Ambush of Convoy LMC-55c* and *Operation: Insignificant*.
- **Lant Mining Corporation (LMC):** Corporate hierarchy, Imperial-aligned industrial operations, and full D6 statistics for LMC gear, mining droids, security speeders, bulk cruisers, ore haulers, and *Lormar*-class refinery stations.
- **Criminal Elements:** Profiles of regional syndicates and brokers, including the Nalmar Crime Family, Bazak's Gang, Soach's Organization, and Ti'mere's InfoServices.
- **Brak Sector Gazetteer:** System and planetary entries for worlds such as Bacrana, Demar, Genesia, Plu Makor, Fae Kalena, and the Latoma system, paired with adventure hooks.
