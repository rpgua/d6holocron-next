*Creatures of the Galaxy* (1994, `WEG40080`) is a 96-page softcover bestiary for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition), edited and co-authored by Bill Smith alongside contributors Phil Brucato, Rick D. Stuart, and Chuck Truett, and published by West End Games. The book was developed through an unusual reverse workflow: West End Games commissioned illustrations of bizarre alien fauna first—from artists including Mike Vilardi, Tom O'Neill, and Pete Venters—and then tasked the writing team with crafting ecologies, behaviors, and D6 game statistics to match each design.

Rather than treating wildlife purely as combat encounters, the supplement encourages gamemasters to integrate alien animals into planetary environments as mounts, guard beasts, beasts of burden, trade commodities, or biological hazards. Several species introduced here, such as bergruutfas, were later adopted into broader *Star Wars* Legends novels and reference guides.

## Key Contents

- **Using Creatures in Campaigns:** Introductory gamemaster guidance on reading creature stat blocks and incorporating wildlife as mounts, domesticated pets, ecological hazards, or sources of valuable materials and medicine.
- **Over 50 Illustrated Non-Sentient Species:** Detailed entries covering predators, grazers, scavengers, and exotic organisms—including bergruutfas, gornnts, humbabas, kashoonaras, maungurs, and electricity-consuming storm worms (*raen sovra*).
- **Standardized D6 Stat Blocks:** Full Second Edition attributes (Dexterity, Perception, Strength), movement rates, natural weapons, scales, and special biological abilities for every creature.
- **Ecology and Adventure Possibilities:** Each entry pairs habitat, diet, and behavioral notes with concrete adventure hooks so gamemasters can build encounters or whole scenarios around local fauna.
