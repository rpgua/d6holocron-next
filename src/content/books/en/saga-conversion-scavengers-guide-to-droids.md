*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Scavenger's Guide to Droids* is a community-compiled conversion document from The Rancor Pit that adapts Wizards of the Coast's *Scavenger's Guide to Droids* (2009) from *Star Wars Saga Edition* into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Designed to be used alongside the original *Saga Edition* text for background lore, it converts the book's extensive mechanical catalog, droid customization options, and hardware rules into standard D6 attribute dice, skill codes, and pricing.

Because *Scavenger's Guide to Droids* spans multiple eras of the *Star Wars* timeline—from the Old Republic and Clone Wars through the Galactic Civil War and beyond—this conversion serves as a versatile reference for any D6 campaign featuring droid player characters, mechanical companions, or specialized automated adversaries.

## Key Contents

- **Droid Character Creation & Chassis**: D6 adaptations of the chassis-based droid design framework, stock droid player templates, and manufacturer traits for major corporations such as Baktoid, Czerka, and Cybot Galactica.
- **Droid Equipment & Modifications**: Converted statistics and costs for specialized locomotion systems (including magnetic feet and aquatic drives), appendages, processors, hidden weapon mounts, cutting torches, comm jammers, and holographic accessories.
- **The Droid Codex**: A large catalog of pre-built droid stat blocks translated into D6 format, spanning first- through fifth-degree models—from astromech, medical, protocol, and administration droids to shadow droids, turret droids, and commando battle droids.
- **Gamemaster Usage**: Streamlined profiles and modification notes enabling Gamemasters to deploy droids either as equipment-style utility assets or as fully realized NPCs and villains.
