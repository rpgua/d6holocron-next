*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Jedi Academy Training Manual* is a fan-made rules conversion compiled by The Rancor Pit community to adapt Wizards of the Coast's *Star Wars Roleplaying Game: Saga Edition — Jedi Academy Training Manual* (2009) into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Intended for use alongside the original *Saga Edition* volume, this supplement translates its extensive catalog of Force powers, mystical traditions, lightsaber customization rules, artifacts, and legendary Force-users into classic D6 *Control*, *Sense*, and *Alter* mechanics.

Spanning the Old Republic, Rise of the Empire, Rebellion, and New Jedi Order eras, the *Jedi Academy Training Manual* is one of *Saga Edition*'s most comprehensive Force sourcebooks. This conversion makes its breadth of Jedi, Sith, and non-aligned Force options immediately playable in D6 campaigns.

## Key Contents

- **Force Powers & Lightsaber Forms:** D6 conversions of expanded Force powers (such as *Force Track*, *Inertia*, and *Inspire*), Force training regimens, and lightsaber combat form techniques.
- **Lightsaber Construction & Artifacts:** Adapted D6 rules for custom lightsaber fabrication, rare focusing crystals, variant hilt designs, and interacting with Jedi and Sith holocrons.
- **Affiliated Force Traditions:** D6 mechanical adaptations for alternative Force-using orders across the galaxy, including the Aing-Tii Monks, Baran Do Sages, Fallanassi, Iron Knights, and Wardens of the Sky.
- **Instructors & Alumni:** Full D6 character statistics for notable historical masters, knights, and Force-users from across the timeline, including Luke Skywalker, Master Thon, and Cay Qel-Droma.
- **Dangers of Study:** Converted D6 stat blocks for dark side adepts, Nightsisters of Dathomir, fallen Jedi, and Sith alchemy creatures such as battle hydras.
