*Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi (1st Edition)*, written by Michael Stern and published by West End Games in 1990, concludes the original trilogy film sourcebook series for First Edition *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Framed in-universe as the final volume of Rebel Alliance historian Lieutenant Voren Na'al's field journal, it chronicles the decisive events of *Return of the Jedi* (4 ABY), from the rescue of Han Solo on Tatooine to the Battle of Endor.

Designed as both a canonical reference and a practical campaign toolkit, the book provides character histories, personality sketches, location layouts, and First Edition D6 statistics for the heroes, villains, underworld figures, and indigenous peoples of Episode VI.

## Key Contents

- **Tatooine & Jabba's Palace:** Profiles and stats for Jabba the Hutt, Bib Fortuna, Salacious Crumb, Gamorrean guards, EV-9D9, Barada, Weequays, Ephant Mon, Oola, the rancor, Jabba's Ubrikkian luxury sail barge, and the Great Pit of Carkoon.
- **Rebel & Imperial Profiles:** Detailed dossiers for Mon Mothma, Admiral Ackbar, General Madine, Wedge Antilles, Nien Nunb, Arvel Crynyd, Emperor Palpatine, Darth Vader, Moff Jerjerrod, Imperial Royal Guards, AT-ST drivers, and scout troopers.
- **Forest Moon of Endor:** Coverage of Bright Tree Village with maps, Ewok leaders (Chief Chirpa, Logray, Wicket W. Warrick, Teebo, Paploo), and schematics for the Aratech 74-Z military speeder bike.
- **The Heroes of Yavin:** Episode VI First Edition statistics for Luke Skywalker (as a Jedi Knight), Leia Organa, Han Solo, Chewbacca, Lando Calrissian, R2-D2, C-3PO, Yoda, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Anakin Skywalker.
- **Included Adventure:** *"Long Live the Alliance"*, a ready-to-play scenario set on the Forest Moon of Endor immediately after the destruction of the second Death Star.
