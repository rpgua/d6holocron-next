*Scavenger Hunt* (1989, `WEG40020`) is a 40-page First Edition adventure module published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set during the Galactic Civil War following the Battle of Yavin, the scenario pairs traditional Rebel espionage with eccentric galactic salvage culture and lighthearted pulp adventure.

After an Imperial strike devastates a Rebel base on Dankayo, Imperial forces scoop up the wreckage—including intact databanks listing secret Alliance contacts across the sector—and load it onto the transport *Elusive*. Assigned to intercept the vessel before it reaches an Imperial research facility at Coronar, the Rebel heroes must first trace its route by negotiating with the Squibs, a species of compulsive scavengers, while fending off the trash-worshipping Ugors and pirates hired by Jabba the Hutt. Their pursuit leads through hazardous salvage fields and even the debris of the first Death Star—where notable relics such as Obi-Wan Kenobi's lightsaber, Darth Vader's spare helmet, and an unstable gravity-well projector can be encountered—before culminating in a chaotic space battle.

## Key Contents
- **New Alien Species:** First Edition D6 background lore and game statistics for the haggling Squibs and the religious, refuse-revering Ugors.
- **Multi-Faction Pursuit:** Encounters involving Imperial forces aboard the *Elusive*, Ugorian patrols, and Hutt-backed pirates competing for salvage.
- **Full-Color Shuttle Map:** A poster-sized interior deck plan of an Imperial *Lambda*-class shuttle included with the original release.
- ***Star Warriors* Integration:** Rules, scenario notes, and counters linking the adventure's climactic starfighter clash to West End Games' *Star Warriors* tactical board game.
- **NPCs & Starship Stats:** Complete profiles for key non-player characters, salvage vessels, and Imperial craft.
