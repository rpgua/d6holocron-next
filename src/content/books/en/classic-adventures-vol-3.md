*Classic Adventures: Volume Three* (1996, `WEG40133`) is a Second Edition, Revised and Expanded anthology published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Compiling three well-known First Edition standalone modules into a single volume, the book updates all character statistics, starship scales, and difficulty numbers for the Revised and Expanded D6 ruleset.

Set during the classic Rebellion era between *A New Hope* and *The Empire Strikes Back*, the anthology spans luxury-liner intrigue, underwater political sabotage, and a desert bounty-hunter chase across Tatooine.

## Key Contents

- ***Riders of the Maelstrom*** (by Ray Winninger): Aboard the luxury star-cruiser *Kuari Princess* traversing the hazardous Kuari Maelstrom, Rebel operatives uncover a clandestine meeting of Imperial Moffs while the vessel comes under siege by a notorious pirate gang.
- ***Death in the Undercity*** (by Michael Nystul): Set on the ocean world of Mon Calamari, the player characters are framed for a politically sensitive double murder while investigating sabotage against Quarren deep-sea mining operations vital to Alliance star cruiser construction.
- ***Tatooine Manhunt*** (by Bill Slavicsek and Daniel Greenberg): Dispatched to Tatooine to find Adar Tallon, a legendary Old Republic naval tactician long presumed dead, the heroes navigate Mos Eisley and the Jundland Wastes while competing against a roster of deadly Imperial-hired bounty hunters.
- **2E Revised & Expanded Conversions**: Includes updated NPC profiles, bounty hunter templates, ship statistics, location maps, and gamemaster running notes for all three scenarios.
