*Crisis on Cloud City* (1989, `WEG40022`) is a 40-page First Edition adventure module for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Christopher Kubasik (with game design contributions by Michael Stern, Douglas Kaufman, and Greg Gorden) and published by West End Games with cover art by Ralph McQuarrie. Set on Bespin's floating metropolis under Baron-Administrator Lando Calrissian, the adventure acts as a loose narrative follow-up to *Starfall*, continuing storylines involving Rebel engineer Walex Blissex and his Imperial-aligned daughter, Lady Lira Wessex.

Dispatched to escort a scientist who has developed an experimental prototype droid known as XO-XI ("Exo"), the Rebel player characters arrive to find their contact murdered, the prototype stolen, and themselves framed for the crime. To survive and save Cloud City from catastrophe, the heroes must navigate both opulent casinos and gritty industrial sub-levels while evading Imperial operatives and tracking down the rogue droid.

## Key Contents

- **Cloud City Investigation**: A multi-episode mystery and espionage plot spanning Cloud City's plaza districts, gambling halls, and Tibanna gas processing facilities.
- **Notable Characters**: Full First Edition profiles and roleplaying notes for Lando Calrissian, Walex Blissex, Lady Lira Wessex, Cloud City Wing Guards, and the rogue XO-XI prototype droid.
- **Pull-Out Sabacc Card Game**: Includes complete rules and a 76-card cut-out deck for playing Sabacc at the gaming table, marking the first official playable tabletop version of the iconic *Star Wars* gambling game.
- **Location Maps & Handouts**: Tactical layouts for key Cloud City encounters, introductory script, and gamemaster aids for running high-stakes urban pursuits.
