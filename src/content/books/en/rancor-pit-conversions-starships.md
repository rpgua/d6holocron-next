*Rancor Pit Conversions: Starships* is a fan-made fleet compendium compiled by The Rancor Pit community for West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Developed by community members to keep the D6 System current with newer *Star Wars* eras, the book gathers starships originally published in Wizards of the Coast's d20 and *Saga Edition* sourcebooks, technical manuals, novels, comics, and video games, translating them into standard WEG D6 starship stat blocks.

The collection spans multiple galactic eras—from Old Republic warships and Clone Wars starfighters to Galactic Civil War freighters and post-Endor fleet vessels. Every vessel is formatted using classic D6 parameters—including Scale, Length, Skill, Crew, Hyperdrive Multiplier, Space Move, Maneuverability, Hull, Shields, and Weapon Fire Control—allowing Gamemasters to integrate expanded-universe spacecraft seamlessly into space combat and hyperspace travel.

## Key Contents

- **Starfighters & Interceptors:** Single- and two-seat combat craft from across the timeline, complete with maneuverability dice, atmospheric speeds, and laser or missile loadouts.
- **Light Freighters & Transports:** Player-suitable tramp freighters, armored couriers, diplomatic shuttles, and smuggling vessels with cargo capacities, passenger quarters, and modification potential.
- **Patrol Craft & Gunships:** System defense boats, corvettes, customs cutters, and troop drop ships designed for planetary blockades, boarding actions, and escort missions.
- **Capital Ships & Cruisers:** Frigates, heavy cruisers, Star Destroyer variants, and carrier-class warships statted at Capital scale with full turret batteries and starfighter complements.
- **Standardized D6 Formatting:** Consistent hull, shield, sensor range, and weapon damage conversions aligned with *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* space combat rules.
