*The Politics of Contraband* (1992, `WEG40067`) is an official Second Edition adventure anthology published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Specifically tailored for independent smuggler and free-trader crews operating a modified light freighter, the book is set during the early **New Republic era** (between 5 ABY and 9 ABY), when a fledgling galactic government, stubborn Imperial remnants, corporate interests, and criminal syndicates all compete for influence along the space lanes.

Rather than a single linear campaign, the supplement provides **five standalone short adventures** designed for single-session play (approximately three to five hours each). Each scenario places fringe crews in morally gray situations—impounded ships, black-market auctions, mistaken identities, and double-crossing employers—where profit, survival, and local politics constantly collide.

## Key Contents

- **"The Politics of Contraband":** A crew whose freighter is caught in local red tape must navigate between a corrupt planetary governor and an overzealous New Republic ambassador, with the chance to tip a planetary power struggle.
- **"The Art of Betrayal":** Coerced into attending an exclusive black-market auction for cutting-edge personal shielding technology, the heroes must outmaneuver treacherous corporate agents, Hutts, and rival underworld buyers.
- **"Free Time":** A lighter, comedic scenario in which a rare shore leave spirals into mistaken identity involving local revolutionaries, quirky aliens, and touring musicians, featuring multiple branching endings.
- **"The Right Place...":** Beginning with a cantina brawl on Celanon, the crew is mistaken for a legendary smuggler and hired for a high-risk cargo run orchestrated by an employer playing both sides of a planetary conflict.
- **"Easy Money":** Set on the contested, lawless jungle world of Seikosha in the Borderland Regions, where a seemingly simple payout turns into a tangle of double-crosses, dangerous wildlife, and competing fringe factions.
