*Gundark's Fantastic Technology: Personal Gear* (1997, `WEG40158`) is an official West End Games equipment catalog for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), written by Paul Sudlow and Eric S. Trautmann. Framed as an illicit HoloNet buyer's guide compiled by the notorious arms dealer Gundark, the sourcebook combines complete D6 game statistics with in-universe commentary from smugglers, bounty hunters, Rebel operatives, and fringe specialists.

Designed as a comprehensive personal equipment reference for Galactic Civil War and New Republic campaigns, the volume expands far beyond standard core-rulebook gear. Entries include cost, availability, game mechanics, and black-market notes, allowing players and Gamemasters to outfit characters for espionage, wilderness survival, urban combat, or specialized technical operations.

## Key Contents

- **Weapons and Ordnance (Chapters 1–5):** Extensive catalogs covering melee weapons, projectile weapons and slugthrowers, energy weapons, missile and grenade launchers, and specialized explosives.
- **Armor and Personal Mobility (Chapters 6–7):** Personal armor suites, helmet and armor attachments, and compact individual conveyances.
- **Field and Technical Gear (Chapters 8–11):** Restraint devices, tools, wilderness survival kits, and entertainment or leisure gadgets.
- **Espionage, Computing, and Detection (Chapters 12–16):** Communications and imaging devices, portable computer hardware, security systems, scanners, and unusual specialty items.
