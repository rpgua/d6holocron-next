Published by West End Games in 1995, *The DarkStryder Campaign* boxed set (WEG40209) launches a four-part New Republic-era exploration saga set four years after the Battle of Endor (8 ABY). Conceived with an introductory story by Timothy Zahn and developed by Peter M. Schweighofer, Doug Shuler, Bill Smith, and Eric S. Trautmann, the campaign casts players as the crew of the *FarStar*—a heavily modified CR90 Corellian corvette hastily crewed by New Republic personnel, former Imperials, smugglers, and alien specialists.

After driving the rogue Imperial warlord Moff Kentor Sarne from the sector capital of Kal'Shebbol, the *FarStar* must venture beyond charted space into the remote Kathol Sector to hunt him down and uncover the origin of the bizarre DarkStryder technology he left behind. Distinctively, the campaign introduces a troupe-style play structure in which each player controls a primary bridge officer alongside supporting crew members, creating an ensemble starship-exploration format supported by three subsequent sourcebooks (*The Kathol Outback*, *The Kathol Rift*, and *Endgame*).

## Key Contents

- **The Campaign Book (96 pages):** Includes Timothy Zahn's introductory short story *"The Saga Begins"*, campaign background, full deck-by-deck details and game stats for the modified corvette *FarStar*, ship maintenance rules, and profiles of its diverse crew.
- **The Adventure Book (96 pages):** Provides a setting gazetteer of the Kathol Sector, gamemaster campaign notes, a rogue's gallery of allies and adversaries, and six sequentially linked adventures: *Omens*, *Artifact of Aaris*, *Death Is Remotely Possible*, *Shintel Downtime*, *Crisis*, and *Traitor in Our Midst*.
- **Character & Ship Cards:** Full-color cardstock sheets profiling the playable crew roster, supporting personnel, and starships encountered during the voyage.
- **Poster Map:** A double-sided 22" x 34" full-color poster detailing the *FarStar* and key campaign locations.
