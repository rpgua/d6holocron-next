*Star Wars Introductory Adventure Game* (1997, `WEG40602`) is a complete beginner's boxed starter set published by West End Games for the Revised and Expanded Second Edition era of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Crafted specifically for players and Gamemasters with no prior tabletop RPG experience, it introduces the D6 System incrementally through guided play rather than requiring upfront study of a full rulebook.

The set uses a streamlined subset of the D6 rules—consolidating select skills such as melee attack and parry into unified ratings—to keep early sessions fast and approachable. Its core campaign takes place shortly after the Battle of Yavin on the Outer Rim world of Edan II, where a fledgling Rebel cell stationed at Edan Base draws Imperial attention after raiding supply shipments. Across a series of escalating episodes, the heroes evade probe droids, defend against an Imperial assault, and fight to evacuate their allies safely.

## Key Contents
- **Three Core Booklets:** A *Player Booklet* (with a guided solo tutorial scenario), a *Narrator Booklet* (GM advice, streamlined rules, gear, droids, creatures, and starships), and a multi-episode *Adventure Booklet*.
- **Pre-Generated Character Templates:** Ready-to-play Rebel archetypes including smugglers, Wookiees, bounty hunters, and Force-sensitive youths, alongside customizable character sheets.
- **Double-Sided Tactical Maps:** Full-color poster maps depicting Edan Base, a YT-1300 light freighter, Mos Eisley Spaceport and Cantina, an Imperial scout post, and a bunker entrance.
- **Tabletop Components:** Punch-out stand-up paper miniatures for heroes, Imperials, and creatures, color reference cards for equipment and vehicles, and six six-sided dice.
