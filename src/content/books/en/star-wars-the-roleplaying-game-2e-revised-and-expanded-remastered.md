*The Star Wars Roleplaying Game: Second Edition, Revised and Expanded (Remastered)* is a ground-up community digital remaster of West End Games' classic 1996 hardcover core rulebook (*WEG40120*). Completed by Van Dyke Brown in 2026 after roughly three years of layout work and two months of community-assisted proofing, this **2nd Printing Ebook** represents the final polished edition of the project.

Unlike *REUP* (*Revised, Expanded, and Updated*), which folds in optional rules from across the wider WEG/OpenD6 catalog and expands into later eras, *Remastered* intentionally remains roughly 99% faithful to the original 1996 *2E Revised & Expanded* text and its late-1990s Expanded Universe (*Legends*) presentation. Its goal is to preserve the authentic West End Games ruleset in a crisp, readable modern edition without altering core mechanics.

## Key Highlights

- **Faithful 1996 Ruleset**: Retains the complete *Second Edition, Revised & Expanded* rules—character creation, Wild Die mechanics, combat, vehicles, starships, the Force, equipment, templates, and gamemastering—without heavy house-ruling.
- **Ground-Up Modern Typesetting**: Rebuilt from scratch with clean, high-resolution typography, refreshed tables, and consistent page layout across all 316 pages.
- **Community Proofing & Errata**: Incorporates official errata and community-verified corrections gathered during the proofing cycle leading to the 2nd Printing.
- **Digital-Ready Ebook**: Formatted for comfortable screen reading with structured navigation and PDF bookmarks for quick reference at the table.
