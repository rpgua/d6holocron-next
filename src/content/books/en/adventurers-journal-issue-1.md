*Adventurer's Journal Issue 1* (product code `AJ050418`) is the inaugural issue of the fan-produced *Adventurer's Journal* anthology series, released on May 4, 2018 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP). Created by the D6 community as a spiritual successor to West End Games' 1990s *Star Wars Adventure Journal*, the premiere issue establishes the magazine's recurring structure of short fiction, rules expansions, sector guides, solitaire play, and full group adventures.

Opening with a retrospective on West End Games' publishing history, the issue introduces several recurring columns—including *Location Scout*, *Beyond BoSS*, and *Behind the Screen*—that continue across later volumes. Its material is compatible with Second Edition, Revised & Expanded, and REUP rulesets.

## Key Contents
- **Historical Essay & Rules Expansions:** *West End Games: A History* by Andrew Zavadin, expanded martial arts rules in *Internal or External, Hard or Soft: New Martial Arts* by Jason Dray, and *Regulators & Renegades* by Daniel Sperelli.
- **Force Lore & Fiction:** *The Great Song: The Force as Sung by Ku-karn Low-garn* by John Gendall, exploring an alternative Force perspective, alongside *Another One Down* by Jason Arana Sr.
- **Group & Solo Adventures:** *Absent Friends*, a full group scenario by Ryan English, and *A Safe & Secure Society*, a solitaire adventure by Mark Dowson featuring Reska Jat.
- **Setting Guides & Templates:** *Behind the Screen: Aractnos Sector*, *Location Scout: Hawkbat City* featuring Aurek Jenth, *Beyond BoSS* featuring Dreena Matura, the new *Femme Fatale* character template, *General Quarters*, and a blank character sheet.
