*Imperial Double-Cross* (1997, `WEG40601`) is an official West End Games introductory solo adventure for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded), written by Peter M. Schweighofer with illustrations by Tim Bobko, Rich Hawran, and Mike Vilardi. Following the tradition of earlier WEG solitaire gamebooks such as *Scoundrel's Luck* and *Jedi's Honor*, it was crafted as an accessible gateway product for newcomers to tabletop roleplaying.

Set during the Galactic Civil War, the 64-page book places the reader in the role of a 14-year-old protagonist on a commerce-driven world who becomes entangled in an Imperial conspiracy. Through branching choices and simplified D6 resolution, players navigate escapes, betrayals, and tense encounters without requiring a Gamemaster or a full gaming group.

## Key Contents

- **Interactive Solitaire Format:** Contains 158 numbered story sections structured in a branching gamebook layout that tracks player decisions and consequences.
- **Introductory D6 Mechanics:** Gently introduces core concepts of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, including skill checks, character attributes, and combat basics.
- **Standalone Playability:** Designed to be played solo with minimal preparation, serving as a self-contained bridge into the full Second Edition, Revised and Expanded rulebook.
- **Classic Rebellion-Era Intrigue:** Features Imperial treachery, quick-thinking escapes, and moral choices tailored to capture the tone of a classic *Star Wars* adventure.
