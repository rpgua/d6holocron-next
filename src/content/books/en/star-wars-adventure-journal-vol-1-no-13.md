*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 13* (WEG41013), published by West End Games in May 1997 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer, is the thirteenth quarterly anthology for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. It serves as the direct companion to Issue 12 by delivering the second half of the celebrated Zahn–Stackpole collaboration *"Side Trip"*, alongside new short fiction, adventure modules, and gamemaster resources set across the Galactic Civil War and New Republic eras.

Designed to bridge Expanded Universe storytelling with tabletop play, the issue provides character backgrounds, underworld intrigue, and in-universe news reports ready to seed ongoing D6 campaigns.

## Key Contents
- **Conclusion of "Side Trip":** Features Part Three by Michael A. Stackpole and Part Four by Timothy Zahn, following Corran and Hal Horn inside crime lord Zekka Thyne's Corellian dungeons as they cross paths with bounty hunter Jodo Kast and Imperial agents.
- **Short Fiction & Scenarios:** Includes Laurie Burns's *"Conflict of Interest"* and Paul Danner's *"The Last Hand"*, blending Expanded Universe storytelling with D6 adventure hooks and playable scenarios.
- **In-Universe News Dispatches:** *"Galaxywide NewsNets"*, compiled by Peter M. Schweighofer, offers galactic headlines, political developments, and adventure seeds for gamemasters.
- **D6 Gaming Resources:** Provides supplementary game statistics, character profiles, and campaign tools formatted for *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*.
