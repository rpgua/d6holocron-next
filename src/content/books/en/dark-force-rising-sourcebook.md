Written by Bill Slavicsek and published by West End Games in October 1993, the *Dark Force Rising Sourcebook* is the second volume in WEG's adaptation of Timothy Zahn's Thrawn Trilogy for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Picking up directly after the events of *Heir to the Empire*, it covers the escalating war between Grand Admiral Thrawn's resurgent Imperial Remnant and a politically fractured New Republic.

Beyond military conflict, the supplement focuses heavily on intrigue inside the New Republic Provisional Council, the hunt for the legendary lost *Katana* fleet of two hundred *Dreadnaught*-class heavy cruisers, and Luke Skywalker's fraught encounters with the insane cloned Jedi Master Joruus C'baoth. It serves as both a narrative reference and a mechanical toolbox for post-Endor campaigns.

## Key Contents

- **Factions and Updated Profiles:** New and updated Second Edition statistics for New Republic officials (including Borsk Fey'lya and Garm Bel Iblis), Imperial officers under Thrawn, and smugglers and fringe operatives led by Talon Karrde and Mara Jade.
- **The Force:** Expanded Force powers, rules, and character context surrounding Joruus C'baoth, Luke Skywalker's continuing Jedi training, and Leia Organa Solo's Force sensitivity.
- **Planets and Alien Species:** Detailed entries on worlds such as Honoghr, Jomark, New Cov, and Pantolomin, alongside species lore and game stats for the Noghri, Bothans, and other inhabitants.
- **The Katana Fleet and Starships:** Game statistics for the slave-rigged *Dreadnaught*-class heavy cruisers of the Dark Force, Assault Frigates, starfighters, and smuggling transports.
- **Droids, Vehicles, and Gear:** Profiles for repulsorlift vehicles, walkers, droids, and specialized equipment featured throughout the novel.
