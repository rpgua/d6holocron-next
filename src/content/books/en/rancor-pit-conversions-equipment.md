*Rancor Pit Conversions: Equipment* is a fan-compiled rules supplement produced by The Rancor Pit community to expand the gear catalog of West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Created by forum contributors after the end of West End Games' license, the compendium translates personal equipment, protective armor, cybernetics, and technical gear from post-WEG *Star Wars* sources—including Wizards of the Coast's d20 and *Saga Edition* books, reference guides, and Expanded Universe media—into standardized D6 System game statistics.

Designed as an era-spanning reference for both players and Gamemasters, the supplement bridges technological gaps across the Old Republic, Clone Wars, Galactic Civil War, and New Jedi Order timelines. Each entry follows classic D6 formatting with cost, availability codes, game notes, and mechanical modifiers so items can be dropped directly into existing campaigns without re-balancing.

## Key Contents

- **Personal Armor & Protective Gear:** D6 statistics for combat suits, Mandalorian armor variants, environmental shells, stealth garments, and specialized personal shields with physical and energy protection codes.
- **Sensors, Communications & Surveillance:** Comlinks, holoprojectors, tracking devices, jammers, scanners, and espionage gadgets adapted with D6 skill bonuses and operational ranges.
- **Tools, Slicing & Medical Gear:** Technical kits, computer spikes, security bypass modules, medpacs, stims, and field surgery equipment mapped to *Technical* and *Knowledge* skill checks.
- **Survival & Utility Equipment:** Breath masks, climbing rigs, utility belts, field rations, and hostile-environment gear for frontier and wilderness expeditions.
- **Cybernetics & Upgrades:** Rules and game stats for bionic replacements, neural implants, and equipment modifications tailored to D6 attribute and skill scales.
