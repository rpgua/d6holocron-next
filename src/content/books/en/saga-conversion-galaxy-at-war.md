*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Galaxy at War* is a fan-made conversion guide compiled by The Rancor Pit community to adapt Wizards of the Coast's *Star Wars Roleplaying Game: Saga Edition — Galaxy at War* (2009) for West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Intended to be used alongside the original *Saga Edition* sourcebook, this document translates its game statistics, species, equipment, military vehicles, adversaries, and wartime campaign mechanics into D6 rules while leaving the original lore and narrative text intact.

*Galaxy at War* focuses on military-themed campaigns across all *Star Wars* eras, from Clone Wars frontlines and Imperial garrisons to Rebel Alliance strike teams. This conversion enables D6 Gamemasters to run structured military operations, squad-based missions, and large-scale battlefield encounters without manually re-statting the sourcebook's hardware or NPCs.

## Key Contents

- **Military Species & Character Options:** D6 attribute dice, special abilities, and skill adaptations for the new alien species and squad-oriented combat specialties introduced in *Galaxy at War*.
- **Military Hardware & Cybernetics:** Converted D6 statistics for combat cybernetics, heavy infantry weapons, tactical armor, and military field gear, alongside D6 adaptations of the Gear Requisition and Military Rank systems.
- **Battlefield & Campaign Mechanics:** D6 rules translations for battlefield environmental hazards, mission briefing and extraction structures, and guidelines for running Imperial or Rebel military units.
- **Fortified Bases & Encounters:** Converted statistics for garrison troops, hostile creatures, military droids, and combat vehicles featured in the sourcebook's base maps and encounter tables.
- **Operation: First Breach:** D6 System adversary stats and skill difficulty conversions for running the included military assault adventure.
