Published by West End Games in January 1989 and written by Greg Gorden, *The Star Wars Rules Companion* (WEG40043) is an 80-page softcover rules expansion for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (1st Edition)*. Released just over a year after the 1987 core rulebook, the supplement addressed common rules questions, refined combat and movement mechanics, and introduced expanded options for long-running Galactic Civil War campaigns.

Historically, *The Star Wars Rules Companion* served as the bridge between First Edition and Second Edition. Many of its innovations—such as clearer skill definitions, expanded capital ship engagements, updated droid and equipment mechanics, and a greatly enlarged catalog of Force powers—directly informed the 1992 Second Edition overhaul. Alongside its rules revisions, the book includes a full-color section, starship conversion notes for the *Star Warriors* board game, and a ready-to-run adventure.

## Key Contents

- **New Rules & Revisions:** Seven structured chapters covering *Attributes and Skills*, *Movement*, *Combat*, *Starships*, *Star Warriors Conversion*, *Droids and Equipment*, and *Capital Ship Combat*.
- **Expanded Force Rules:** Three dedicated Force chapters (*Force Rules*, *Revised Force Powers*, and *New Force Powers*) clarifying *Control, Sense,* and *Alter* mechanics and adding dozens of new Jedi abilities.
- **Capital Ship & Starfighter Integration:** Introduces formal rules for large-scale capital ship battles as well as conversion guidelines linking the RPG with West End Games' *Star Warriors* tactical board game.
- **Included Adventure — *To Free the Forgotten*:** A complete scenario designed to showcase the companion's revised rules in play.
- **Reference Aids:** Features a full-color section, a ship template and reference chart, and an index for quick lookup at the table.
