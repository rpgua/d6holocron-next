Published by West End Games in December 1996 and written by Timothy S. O'Brien, George R. Strayton, and Eric S. Trautmann, *Endgame* (WEG40112) is the 128-page fourth and final volume of *The DarkStryder Campaign* for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (2E-RE). Set in the New Republic era (8 ABY), it brings the *FarStar*'s long pursuit across the Kathol Sector, Outback, and Rift to its decisive conclusion.

Unlike the earlier installments, which collected multiple episodic scenarios, *Endgame* pairs extensive final-act source material with a single book-length climactic adventure. Having survived the hazards of the Kathol Rift, the battered crew of the *FarStar* finally reaches the hidden Kathol homeworld to confront Moff Kentor Sarne, rescue captured allies, and face the truth behind the ancient bio-technological construct known as DarkStryder.

## Key Contents

- **Campaign Update & Introductory Fiction:** Summarizes the *FarStar*'s journey to date and opens with George R. Strayton's short story *"The Saga Nears Its End"*, accompanied by Gamemaster Crew Notes on handling ship damage, morale, and ensemble character arcs.
- **The DarkStryder Planet:** Comprehensive setting material detailing the hidden homeworld of the ancient Kathol species, its surviving inhabitants, and the origins and capabilities of the DarkStryder bio-computer entity.
- **Ta-Ree Magic:** Complete D6 rules and background for Ta-Ree, the unique Force-based mystical tradition practiced by the indigenous Ascendants on the Kathol homeworld.
- **Book-Length Finale — *Endgame*:** A continuous, multi-part climax adventure guiding the *FarStar* crew through the final showdown with Moff Sarne's remaining forces and the resolution of DarkStryder's fate.
- **The Making of DarkStryder:** A behind-the-scenes retrospective essay chronicling the development of the four-part campaign, its ensemble design philosophy, and its creative ties to Timothy Zahn's opening story.
