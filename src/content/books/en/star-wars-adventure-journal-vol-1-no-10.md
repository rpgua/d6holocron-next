*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 10* (WEG41010), published by West End Games in May 1996 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer, is the tenth issue of the quarterly anthology for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Arriving just before the transition to the *Revised and Expanded* rulebook, the issue delivers a dense collection of Rebel Alliance intelligence dossiers, technical guides, and Galactic Civil War short fiction.

With a strong emphasis on espionage, droid lore, starfighter tactics, and independent trading worlds, Issue 10 equips D6 gamemasters with ready-made equipment, NPCs, and planetary settings that drop cleanly into Rebellion-era campaigns.

## Key Contents
- **Short Fiction:** Features Kathy Tyers's *"Only Droids Serve the Maker"* (continuing the bounty hunter adventures of Tinian I'att and Chenlambec), Erin Endom's *"Do No Harm"* (later reprinted in *Tales from the Empire*), Nora Mayers's *"The Capture of Imperial Hazard"*, and Carolyn Golledge's *"Desperate Measures"*.
- **Alliance Intelligence & Technical Dossiers:** Includes *"Alliance Intelligence Report: TIE Fighters"* by Timothy O'Brien (tactical breakdowns and D6 stats for TIE variants) and *"Cracken's Rebel Field Guide: Slicing Aids"* by C. Robert Carey and J.P. Pietrzak.
- **NPCs & Bounties:** Provides new Rebel agent profiles in *"Cracken's Rebel Operatives"* and criminal targets in *"Wanted by Cracken"*, both by C. Robert Carey.
- **Planetary & Droid Resources:** Offers *"A Free-Trader's Guide to the Planets"* by Timothy O'Brien and *"Toria Tell's Droid Journal"* by Christian Piccolo with new droid profiles and game statistics.
- **In-Universe Dispatches:** Includes Paul Sudlow's *"Galaxywide NewsNets"* supplying galactic headlines and campaign plot seeds.
