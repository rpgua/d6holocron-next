*Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies* is the final official entry in West End Games' *Galaxy Guide* line, published in 1995 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Written by an anthology team of eight authors—including Pablo Hidalgo, Peter M. Schweighofer, and Jean Rabe—the book is framed as excerpts from the *University of Sanbra Guide to Intelligent Life* compiled by the sentientologist Tem Eliss.

The supplement expands the D6 system with nearly forty sentient alien species, blending recognizable background species from the original film trilogy with original creations tailored for roleplaying campaigns across the Galactic Civil War and New Republic eras.

## Key Contents

- **Nearly 40 Sentient Species:** Complete D6 write-ups covering film species such as the Gran, Nikto, Weequay, Klatooinians, Snivvians, Dressellians, and Baragwin alongside RPG-original species like the Anomids, Chevin, Houk, Kerestians, Tiss'shar, and Tunroth.
- **Full Game Statistics:** Attribute dice codes, movement rates, special abilities, story factors, and standard sample NPC profiles ready for immediate tabletop use.
- **Homeworlds & Cultures:** Detailed notes on biology, society, technology levels, languages, and galactic standing for each species.
- **Player & GM Utility:** Guidelines and roleplaying hooks for incorporating alien species as player characters, allies, underworld rivals, or Imperial collaborators.
