*Adventurer's Journal: Galaxy Guide 2 — Ships & Salvage* (product code `AJ-GG-2-0521`) is a fan-produced sourcebook released in May 2021 by the *Adventurer's Journal* community project for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP). Continuing the project's revival of West End Games' *Galaxy Guide* format, this second volume centers on independent starship operations, shipyard repair yards, salvage crews, and the underworld figures who profit from derelict vessels and black-market parts.

Designed for fringe, smuggler, and mercenary campaigns, the book pairs technical shipyard lore and starship-focused rules with underworld NPCs, serialized fiction, and a complete solo scenario. Gamemasters can draw on its locations and crime bosses to anchor ship-maintenance subplots, debt-driven hooks, and deep-space salvage runs.

## Key Contents
- **Shipyards & Salvage Operations:** Source material on *Cordova Shipyards, Garage & Salvage*, providing a detailed operational hub for repairs, modifications, and scrap-hunting contracts, alongside *Damage Control* by S. Will Whicker.
- **Underworld Contacts & Antagonists:** *Four To Owe* by Mathew Wedel, profiling four distinct crime bosses with D6 statistics and campaign hooks, plus *Tunroth Retributor Squads* by Yuber Okami.
- **Characters & Fiction:** *Istu Dar of Panara* by Mark Dowson and the fifth installment of Dustin Hawk's serialized narrative *True Blue: A Star Wars Story*.
- **Solo Adventure:** *Absence of Malice*, a solitaire adventure written by Mark Dowson for single-player D6 / REUP play.
