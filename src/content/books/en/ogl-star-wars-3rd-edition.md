*OGL Star Wars 3rd Edition* is a fan-built core rulebook adaptation that reimagines *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* using West End Games' OpenD6 Open Game License (OGL) framework. After WEG lost the official *Star Wars* license in the late 1990s, the company evolved its house engine into the generic *D6 System* toolkits—most notably *D6 Space* (2004)—and released the core rules under the OGL in 2009. Because an official third edition of *Star Wars D6* was never published, fan creators built this volume to merge classic *Star Wars* setting material with the modernized OpenD6 ruleset.

Serving as an alternative to the traditional Second Edition and REUP lineage, *OGL Star Wars 3rd Edition* adapts OpenD6's streamlined attributes, advantages and disadvantages, and updated combat resolution to the *Star Wars* galaxy. It offers groups familiar with West End Games' original trilogy books a way to run campaigns with the mechanical refinements of the later OGL era.

## Key Contents

- **OpenD6 Core Framework:** Rebuilds character attributes, skills, and task resolution around the OGL OpenD6 and *D6 Space* architecture, including standardized Wild Die and modifier rules.
- **Character Creation & Customization:** Incorporates OpenD6-style character templates, species profiles, advantages, disadvantages, and special abilities tailored to *Star Wars* archetypes.
- **Updated Combat & Damage Mechanics:** Adapts personal combat, scale differences, armor, and wound/body-point options from the generic D6 System to blaster firefights and lightsaber duels.
- **The Force & Galactic Gear:** Translates Force-sensitive abilities, iconic weapons, equipment, droids, and starships into consistent OpenD6 game statistics.
