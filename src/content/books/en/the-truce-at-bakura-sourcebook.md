Published by West End Games in February 1996, *The Truce at Bakura Sourcebook* adapts Kathy Tyers' 1993 novel for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Written by Eric S. Trautmann with numerous original short fiction vignettes contributed directly by Kathy Tyers, the book is set immediately after the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, during the fragile transition from the Rebel Alliance to the Alliance of Free Planets.

The sourcebook chronicles the temporary truce forged between Rebel strike forces and the Imperial garrison on the remote Outer Rim world of Bakura to repel an invasion from the Unknown Regions by the reptilian Ssi-ruuvi Imperium. Framed in part as a report to the Provisional Council, it gives gamemasters a complete immediate post-Endor setting with an alien adversary distinct from the Empire.

## Key Contents

- **Post-Endor Context & The Bakura Incident**: Background on the immediate aftermath of the Battle of Endor, the provisional government, and the diplomatic and military crisis at Bakura.
- **Forces of the Alliance & Empire**: Second Edition D6 statistics and dossiers for Rebel heroes, Bakuran leaders, and local Imperial officers, interspersed with original short fiction by Kathy Tyers.
- **Bakura Planetary Guide**: Detailed coverage of Bakura's geography, politics, repulsorlift industry, local culture, and defense forces.
- **The Ssi-ruuvi Imperium**: An extensive chapter examining Ssi-ruuk biology, caste society, mythology, and military organization, plus P'w'eck subordinates.
- **Entechment & Ssi-ruuvi Technology**: Rules and technical specifications for entechment, enteched battle droids, paddle beamers, and Ssi-ruuvi starships such as *Shree*-class battle cruisers and *Swarm*-class battle droids.
