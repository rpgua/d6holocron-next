*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Starships of the Galaxy* is a community-produced conversion supplement from The Rancor Pit that adapts Wizards of the Coast's *Starships of the Galaxy* (2007) for *Star Wars Saga Edition* into West End Games' *Star Wars* D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Intended as a statistical companion to the original hardcover, it translates the book's extensive multi-era vessel catalog and spacefaring rules into D6 scale, hull, shield, maneuverability, hyperdrive, and weapon dice codes.

Covering spacecraft from the Old Republic and Clone Wars through the Galactic Civil War and New Jedi Order eras, the conversion provides D6 Gamemasters and players with standardized vehicle profiles for vessels that postdate West End Games' original publishing run.

## Key Contents

- **Starfighters**: D6 game statistics for a wide range of interceptors, bombers, and superiority fighters across multiple eras, including classic Rebel and Imperial craft as well as Republic, Separatist, and expanded-universe variants.
- **Space Transports**: Converted stats for light and medium freighters, couriers, patrol craft, luxury yachts, and utility shuttles suited for player crews.
- **Capital Ships**: Full capital-scale D6 stat blocks for corvettes, frigates, cruisers, carriers, and Star Destroyers, detailing crew complements, hyperdrive ratings, and weapon batteries.
- **Space Stations & Orbital Installations**: Statistics for defensive platforms, shipyards, and fortified stations such as Golan Defense Platforms and superweapon installations.
- **Shipyard & Space Combat Options**: D6 adaptations of ship modification concepts, black-market components, starship maneuvers, and space hazard guidelines.
