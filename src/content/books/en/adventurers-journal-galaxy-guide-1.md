*Adventurer's Journal: Galaxy Guide 1* (subtitled *Rotgut Station*, product code `AJ-GG-1-1118`) is a fan-made setting sourcebook published in November 2018 by the *Adventurer's Journal* community project for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP). Following the format of West End Games' original *Galaxy Guide* line and *Star Wars Adventure Journal*, this special release builds a self-contained frontier hub designed to drop into fringe, smuggler, Rebel, or bounty hunter campaigns.

The volume centers on Rotgut Station, an isolated spaceport and waypoint far from Core World oversight where spacers, outcasts, and opportunists cross paths. It combines descriptive location guides, recurring in-universe news dispatches, character templates, and playable scenarios so gamemasters can use the station either as a recurring campaign base or a standalone stopover.

## Key Contents
- **Rotgut Station Setting & Maps:** Detailed location material including *Location Scout: Rot-Gut Station* featuring Aurek Jenth, exterior station artwork, and *A Somewhere in the Middle of Nowhere* by Daniel Sturman.
- **Adventures & Story Hooks:** Multiple ready-to-run scenarios and plot frameworks, including *Zero Hour* by Cian Shay, *Another Day At Rot-Gut*, and *Left of Center vs. Rites of Passage*.
- **Solo Adventure:** *Greetings From Gutter Town*, a solitaire scenario by Mark Dowson starring Ferris Hol.
- **Game Stats & Templates:** New D6 / REUP material featuring the *Detective* character template, *Mongdoolian Monks* game statistics, a dedicated *Starship STAT!* profile, *HoloNews* dispatches, *Galactic Guidance* notes, and a blank character sheet.
