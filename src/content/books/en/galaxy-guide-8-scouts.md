*Galaxy Guide 8: Scouts*, written by Bill Smith and Bill Olmesdahl and published by West End Games in 1993, is a Second Edition career and exploration sourcebook notable for being the first *Galaxy Guide* to center on the New Republic era. Focusing on the New Republic Scout Service alongside independent pathfinders and corporate survey teams, the book equips players and gamemasters for deep-space expeditions into Wild Space and the Unknown Regions.

Blending frontier atmosphere with practical exploration mechanics, it supports campaigns built around charting dangerous new hyperlanes, conducting first-contact missions, and surviving uncharted worlds far from civilized space.

## Key Contents

- **The Scout Service & Frontier:** Detailed overview of New Republic Scouts, independent explorers, corporate outfits (such as the Karflo Corporation), scout psychology, and the geography of Wild Space and the Unknown Regions.
- **Scout Ships & Bases:** Second Edition specifications for dedicated exploration vessels—including the Lone Scout, Deep-X Explorer, and Scout Shuttle—plus profiles of frontier scout bases and support outposts.
- **Survey Equipment & Survival Gear:** Specialized scanning instruments, environmental suits, survival kits, droids, and field hardware built for prolonged off-grid expeditions.
- **Planetary Survey & System Generation:** Step-by-step rules for conducting planetary surveys, plotting uncharted hyperspace routes, and procedurally generating star systems, worlds, planetary features, aliens, and native creatures.
- **Included Adventure:** *"The Prize"*, a complete Second Edition scout adventure designed to introduce players to frontier exploration and survey operations.
