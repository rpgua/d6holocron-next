*Martial Arts Revised* is a fan-compiled rules supplement from the Google+ Star Wars D6 community, compatible with REUP and West End Games' *Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*. Across the official WEG publishing run, unarmed combat and martial arts rules appeared piecemeal in books such as *Rules of Engagement: The Rebel SpecForce Handbook* and *Alien Encounters*, as well as in early fan net-guides. *Martial Arts Revised* consolidates, balances, and standardizes those disparate systems into a single cohesive framework for close-quarters combat.

Rather than leaving unarmed fighters limited to basic *Brawling* rolls, the supplement treats martial arts as a structured specialization under *Brawling* (and complementary defensive parrying), allowing characters to progress through distinct combat disciplines. It supports both generalist hand-to-hand combatants and dedicated practitioners of iconic galactic styles, making unarmed specialists viable alongside blaster-wielding mercenaries and lightsaber duelists.

## Key Contents

- **Unified Skill & Progression System:** Establishes a consistent advancement model in which increasing a Martial Arts specialization by each full die (+1D) above base Strength unlocks new combat techniques.
- **Named Galactic Styles vs. General Training:** Differentiates between specialized named styles—each granting a signature base ability and curated technique list—and general martial arts training with broader technique selection.
- **Expanded Combat Maneuvers:** Details tactical unarmed actions including strikes, flying kicks, foot sweeps, throws, holds, grapples, elbow smashes, and headbutts.
- **Defensive & Situational Techniques:** Provides rules for specialized melee defenses, weapon parries, disarm attempts, and blind fighting.
