*Rules of Engagement: The Rebel SpecForce Handbook* (1997, `WEG40113`) is a military campaign supplement published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Written by Peter M. Schweighofer and Eric S. Trautmann, the book examines the elite commando units of the Alliance to Restore the Republic—and their later evolution within the New Republic—from recruitment and basic training through covert field operations behind Imperial lines.

Structured across eleven chapters, the supplement serves both players building military characters and Gamemasters running tactical, squad-based campaigns during the Galactic Civil War. It grounds Rebel SpecForce operations in practical field doctrine, covering reconnaissance, raids, ambushes, survival in hostile environments, and the logistical realities of waging asymmetric warfare against the Galactic Empire.

## Key Contents
- **Organization & Regiments:** Complete structural breakdown of SpecForce divisions, regiments (1st through 7th), companies, platoons, squads, and specialized support roles such as scanner-communications operators, interrogators, and procurement specialists.
- **Field Doctrine & Tactics:** Practical rules and guidelines for mission planning, unit formations, hand signals, terrain traversal, securing drop zones, and optional survival mechanics including encumbrance, equipment maintenance, and contaminated water.
- **Quartermaster's Armory & Transport:** Extensive catalogs of fatigues, armor, sensors, survival gear, landing craft, troop transports, gliders, heavy weapons, explosives, and optional deadliness rules for gritty military play.
- **Notable Units & Bases:** Ready-to-use profiles and adventure hooks for Taskforce Shen, Team 19, the Nishr Taskforce, Nishr Base Seven, and Section Desh-32.
- **Imperial Opposition & Campaign Tools:** Detailed stats for Imperial Army special missions units, CompForce, specialized stormtrooper variants, Storm Commandos, and Royal Guards, plus custom martial arts rules, a random mission generator, and character templates.
