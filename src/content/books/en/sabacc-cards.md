*Sabacc Cards* is a printable card deck and tabletop play aid created by the Google+ Star Wars D6 community for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. First introduced in *Star Wars* fiction and given tabletop rules in West End Games supplements such as *Crisis on Cloud City* and *The Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook*, Sabacc is the galaxy's most iconic high-stakes card game—famous as the game in which Han Solo won the *Millennium Falcon* from Lando Calrissian.

While *Star Wars D6* normally abstracts gambling scenes to opposed *Gambling*, *Bargain*, and *Con* skill rolls, this supplement lets Gamemasters bring cantina wagers and casino heists directly to the table as a playable prop. Designed around classic Legends Sabacc rules—where players aim for a hand total of +23, -23, or the rare Idiot's Array while contending with periodic card shifts—the deck provides everything needed to print and play live hands during RPG sessions.

## Key Contents

- **Complete Classic Sabacc Suits:** Includes printable cards for all four traditional suits—Flasks, Sabers, Staves, and Coins—spanning numbered pip cards and face cards (Commander, Mistress, Master, and Ace).
- **Arcana & Negative Modifier Cards:** Features the iconic special cards, including The Idiot (0) and negative-value cards such as Endurance, Balance, Demise, Moderation, The Evil One, The Queen of Air and Darkness, and The Star.
- **Tabletop RPG Integration:** Allows groups to blend live card play with D6 character skills like *Gambling* and *Con* (or cheating devices such as skifters) during roleplaying scenes.
- **Print-and-Play Formatting:** Laid out for straightforward printing, cutting, and sleeving as a reusable campaign prop.
