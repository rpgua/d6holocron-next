*Rancor Pit Conversions: Weapons* is a fan-compiled armory supplement produced by The Rancor Pit community for West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). As newer *Star Wars* films, comics, video games, and Wizards of the Coast's d20 and *Saga Edition* roleplaying books introduced a wide array of firearms, archaic blades, and military ordnance, Rancor Pit contributors assembled this reference to bring those weapons into the D6 System with balanced, consistent mechanics.

Every entry is standardized to WEG D6 conventions, including weapon skill, cost, availability and legality ratings (such as Restricted or Military), fire rate, range brackets, ammo capacity, and damage dice. The compendium provides Gamemasters and players with ready-to-use armament spanning the Old Republic, Clone Wars, Imperial, and post-Imperial eras.

## Key Contents

- **Blaster Pistols, Carbines & Rifles:** Expanded personal energy weapons—from hold-out blasters and sporting models to military assault rifles and sniper blasters—calibrated to D6 damage scales.
- **Slugthrowers & Exotic Ranged Weapons:** Projectile firearms, bowcasters, disruptors, sonic weapons, flechette launchers, and species-specific arms with special damage or armor-piercing notes.
- **Melee & Archaic Weapons:** Vibroblades, force pikes, electrostaffs, cortosis-weave swords, and specialized lightsaber variants (such as shoto, double-bladed, and curved-hilt designs).
- **Heavy Weapons & Ordnance:** Repeating blasters, missile launchers, thermal detonators, mines, grenades, and anti-vehicle weapons with blast radius and scale rules.
- **Weapon Attachments & Modifications:** Targeting scopes, stun settings, extended power packs, and custom weapon upgrades tailored to D6 combat mechanics.
