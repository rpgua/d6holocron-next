*Adventurer's Journal Issue 4* (subtitled *The Force, That Which Binds Us*, product code `AJ050419`) is the fourth issue of the community-created *Adventurer's Journal* anthology for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP), released on May 4, 2019. Dedicated to the Force and Force-sensitive characters, this themed issue examines both traditional Jedi philosophy and non-Jedi mystical traditions across the galaxy.

Aimed at gamemasters seeking deeper mechanical and narrative frameworks for Force users in D6 campaigns, the volume gathers multiple essays on Force mechanics, ancient Force-imbued weaponry, two solitaire adventures, and a full group scenario, alongside updates to recurring *Adventurer's Journal* locations and serialized fiction.

## Key Contents
- **Force Rules & Traditions:** *The Cosmic Force* and *The Forcesaber* by Dustin Hawk, *The Singular Force* by Charles McNeill, *Understanding the Force* by Flynn O'Dinson, and *From A Certain Point of View: Alternate Force Traditions of the Galaxy* by Jason Dray.
- **Group & Solo Adventures:** *Of Dreams & Nightmares*, a group adventure by Ray Napoli, plus two solitaire scenarios by Mark Dowson: *You Are An Imperial's Nightmare* (featuring Zerun/Saekar) and *Aggressive Negotiations* (featuring Ku-karn Low-garn).
- **Fiction & Miniature Play:** Part two of Dustin Hawk's serialized story *True Blue: A Star Wars Story* and the tactical scenario *Target Locked: Raid at Jojo's Bar*.
- **Locations & Player Aids:** *Location Scout: The Cathor Hills Extended* featuring Aurek Jenth, *General Quarters*, an updated *Adventurer's Sheet*, and *Announcing Vagabond* by Damien Holder.
