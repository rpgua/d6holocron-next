*Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope* (First Edition), written by Grant S. Boucher and published by West End Games in 1989 (WEG 40038), was the inaugural entry in the long-running *Galaxy Guide* series for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Framed as an in-universe historical dossier compiled by Rebel Alliance researcher Voren Na'al, this 80-page sourcebook catalogs the people, alien species, droids, creatures, and Imperial personnel connected to the events of *Episode IV: A New Hope*.

Beyond its tabletop utility for First Edition D6 campaigns, the book played a foundational role in the early *Star Wars* Expanded Universe. It established canonical names, homeworlds, and backstories for numerous background characters and cantina patrons seen briefly on screen—lore that later novels, comics, and reference guides built upon for decades. The 1989 edition is also notable for its original black-and-white interior illustrations by artists including Ralph McQuarrie, Al Williamson, and Michael Manley.

## Key Contents

- **In-Universe Framework:** Presented through Voren Na'al's field reports and Rebel Alliance intelligence files documenting the road to the Battle of Yavin.
- **Tatooine Profiles:** Background lore and 1E D6 statistics for moisture farmers, Jawas, Tusken Raiders, banthas, krayt dragons, and desert hazards.
- **Mos Eisley Profiles:** Extensive dossiers on cantina patrons, spacers, smugglers, and alien species—giving many background characters their first published names and histories.
- **Death Star Profiles:** Game stats and biographies for Grand Moff Tarkin, Darth Vader, Imperial officers, stormtroopers, and the garbage-compactor dianoga.
- **Yavin & Heroes of Yavin:** Detailed profiles for Rebel leadership, starfighter pilots, ground personnel, and core heroes Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Han Solo, and Chewbacca.
