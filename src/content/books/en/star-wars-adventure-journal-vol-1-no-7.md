*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 7* was released by West End Games in August 1995 under editor Peter M. Schweighofer for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Notably acclaimed among Expanded Universe collectors, the issue debuts landmark short fiction by Timothy Zahn and Michael A. Stackpole alongside technical source material, Core Worlds lore, and modular adventure outlines.

Set across the Imperial, Rebellion, and early New Republic eras, the volume balances narrative depth with practical gamemaster utilities. Its fiction introduces the origin of Grand Admiral Thrawn's service to the Empire and previews the *X-Wing* novel series, while its RPG articles supply droid histories, Alliance intelligence networks, and instant scenario hooks.

## Key Contents
- **Landmark Short Fiction:** Features Timothy Zahn's *"Mist Encounter"* (recounting Captain Voss Parck's discovery of the exiled Chiss commander Thrawn), Michael A. Stackpole's *"Missed Chance"* (following Corran Horn prior to *Rogue Squadron*), Laurie Burns's *"Retreat from Coruscant"*, and Charlene Newcomb's *"Passages"*.
- **Into the Core Worlds:** A regional guide by Paul Sudlow exploring key systems, political institutions, and travel hazards in the heart of Imperial space.
- **The History of R-Series Droids:** A technical overview by Pablo Hidalgo tracing the evolution and D6 statistics of astromech units from the R1 through R7 models.
- **A Taste of Adventure:** Four concise adventure seeds and mini-scenarios by Tony Russo (including *"Silent Fury"*) built for rapid session prep.
- **Intelligence & Underworld Lore:** Includes *"Cracken's Rebel Operatives"* by Timothy O'Brien, *"The Kaal Connection"* by Peter Schweighofer, *"Old Corellian: A Guide for the Curious Scholar"* by Patricia A. Jackson, and *"Smuggler's Log"* introducing the Wroonian species.
