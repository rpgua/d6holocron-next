*Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim*, written by Simon Smith and Eric S. Trautmann and published by West End Games in 1993 for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*, is a Rebellion-era anthology of Outer Rim lore, rumors, characters, and campaign vignettes. Set between *A New Hope* and *Return of the Jedi*, the book departs from single-topic supplements to offer a wide-ranging collection of drop-in material that adds texture and unpredictability to fringe campaigns.

Each section presents self-contained profiles, cultural details, and Second Edition statistics that gamemasters can insert into any Outer Rim sector without committing to a fixed regional storyline.

## Key Contents

- **Rebel Operatives:** Background dossiers and 2E statistics for independent Rebel agents and mission specialists operating on the galactic fringe, including Corwin Shelvay.
- **Imperial Frontier Personnel:** Profiles of Imperial figures active in the Outer Rim, notably introducing High Inquisitor Tremayne alongside specialized Imperial officers and agents.
- **Crime Lords & Syndicates:** Details on underworld bosses, local gangs, mercenary crews, and independent spacer outfits thriving beyond direct Imperial oversight.
- **Outer Rim Culture & Commerce:** Modular entries covering regional corporations, frontier media outlets, prominent bands and music styles, tourist traps, exotic locales, and notable local beverages.
- **Frontier Force Traditions & Gear:** Obscure equipment, unusual items, and non-standard perspectives on Force training suited for fringe roleplaying campaigns.
