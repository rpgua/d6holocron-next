*Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin (2nd Edition)* is West End Games' 1995 revision of Jonatha Ariadne Caspian's classic 1989 planetary sourcebook, updated for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Retaining the setting timeframe between *A New Hope* and *The Empire Strikes Back*, this edition preserves the original gazetteer's lore while overhauling all game mechanics, stat blocks, and presentation to match WEG's mid-1990s Second Edition standards.

The volume remains a comprehensive guide to both the Yavin system's ancient Massassi ruins and Bespin's floating Cloud City, offering Gamemasters ready-to-run locations, factions, and adventure seeds without needing manual 1E-to-2E rules conversion.

## Key Contents

- **Updated Second Edition Mechanics:** Completely revised D6 2E statistics for all NPCs, creatures, vehicles, equipment, and planetary hazard rules throughout both sections.
- **Yavin 4 Gazetteer:** Detailed coverage of the gas giant Yavin, its moons, tropical wildlife, Massassi temple complexes, and post-evacuation Imperial and Rebel activity.
- **Bespin & Cloud City Guide:** Full descriptions of Cloud City's levels, tibanna gas mining operations, Ugnaught society, Bespin Wing Guard security, and Lando Calrissian's administration.
- **Refreshed Presentation & Hooks:** Updated mid-90s WEG layout and trade dress, minor editorial errata fixes, location maps, and ready-to-use adventure outlines for both systems.
