Published by West End Games in 1996, *The Kathol Rift* (WEG40121) is the 96-page third installment of *The DarkStryder Campaign*, written by Sterling Hershey, Pablo Hidalgo, Joshua A. Miller, Timothy S. O'Brien, and Eric S. Trautmann. Bridging *The Kathol Outback* and *Endgame* in the 8 ABY New Republic timeline, this volume guides the battered CR90 corvette *FarStar* into the Kathol Rift—a dense, Force-warped cosmic anomaly of charged gases, radiation storms, and sensor-blinding nebulae separating the Outback from Moff Kentor Sarne's hidden refuge.

As the ship enters the Rift, environmental hazards are compounded by psychological strain, dwindling provisions, and mounting distrust among the *FarStar*'s eclectic crew. The book combines specialized Rift navigation rules and campaign status updates with five linked adventures (though the back cover famously misprinted the count as six) that push the crew to the brink before the final confrontation in *Endgame*.

## Key Contents

- **Campaign Update & Crew Notes:** Summarizes the evolving hunt for Moff Sarne and provides gamemaster guidance on managing morale, paranoia, and ensemble character dynamics inside the Rift.
- **The Kathol Rift Source Material:** Lore, astrogation mechanics, sensor interference rules, and psychological anomaly effects governing travel through the radioactive, Force-distorted expanse.
- **Five Linked Adventures:**
  - *Harm's Way* — Resupplying contaminated rations at an abandoned Rift science outpost.
  - *Rogue Elements* — Confronting a tense mutiny erupting among factions aboard the *FarStar*.
  - *Waystation* — Investigating an enigmatic ancient structure concealed within the Rift.
  - *Home* — Exploring a personal storyline tied to a native passenger and an anomalous world.
  - *Showdown* — Answering a mysterious distress signal from a seemingly uninhabited planet that draws the crew toward the campaign's climax.
