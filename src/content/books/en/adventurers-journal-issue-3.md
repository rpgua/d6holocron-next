*Adventurer's Journal Issue 3* (product code `AJ020119`) is the third issue of the fan-made *Adventurer's Journal* anthology for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP), released on February 1, 2019. Strongly oriented toward military units, piracy, starfighter operations, and tactical combat, the issue delivers an extensive mix of D6 rules variants, unit profiles, miniature scenarios, and two solitaire adventures.

This issue also launches Dustin Hawk's serialized story *True Blue: A Star Wars Story*, which continues in subsequent releases, while expanding the *Adventurer's Journal* shared sandbox with new locations, pirate crews, and *HoloNet News* dispatches from Caerra Andin of TNN.

## Key Contents
- **Military Units & Combat Rules:** *Artillery: Lord of the Battlefield & Rebalancing the Scales* by Charles McNeill, *Nimbus Commandos* by Jason Dray, *Task Force 88* by Brandon Bragg, and *A Little Short for a Stormtrooper* by John Gendall.
- **Pirates, Starfighters & Templates:** *Kira's Corsairs* by Ray Napoli, the *Antarian Ranger* character template, *Pah Starfighter... STAT!*, and the miniature battle scenario *Target Locked!: Rotgut Ruckus* by David O. Little.
- **Two Solo Adventures:** *The Pirate's Lair* featuring Jorin Gar and *Jedi Resurgence*, both solitaire scenarios written by Mark Dowson.
- **Fiction & Setting Features:** *Distant Suns* and the debut installment of *True Blue: A Star Wars Story* by Dustin Hawk, *Location Scout: Cathor Hills* by Josh Moore, *HoloNet News and Reports*, *General Quarters*, and a blank character sheet.
