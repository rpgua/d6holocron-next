*Galladinium's Fantastic Technology: Guns and Gear for Any Occasion* (1995, `WEG40025`) is a Second Edition equipment sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Rick Swan and Eric S. Trautmann and published by West End Games. Presented as the 50th-anniversary edition of *Galladinium's Galactic Datalog of Fantastic Technology*, the book takes the form of an upscale in-universe mail-order catalog filled with advertisements, product pitches, and technical blurbs tailored for wealthy citizens, travelers, and adventurers during the Galactic Civil War era.

Each item combines tongue-in-cheek corporate sales copy with practical Second Edition D6 game statistics, costs, availability codes, and operational notes. Because the catalog mixes legitimate luxury goods with covert espionage gadgets and heavy weaponry, it serves both as a player equipment guide and a source of flavor for fleshing out everyday galactic commerce.

## Key Contents

- **Weapons and Self-Defense:** Specialized blasters, exotic melee weapons, concealed personal-defense tools, and protective gear with full D6 combat statistics.
- **Conveyances and Travel Aids:** Personal repulsorlift vehicles, compact speeders, survival kits, navigation gear, and specialized accessories for off-world travel.
- **Surveillance, Special Tools, and Droids:** Covert listening devices, security scanners, specialized technical instruments, and commercial or utility droid models.
- **Lifestyle, Medicine, and Luxury Goods:** Sections covering *For Home or Office*, *Jewelry*, *Medicine*, *Personal Gift Ideas*, and *Recreation*, supplying cybernetic curiosities, medical tech, and high-society props.
- **Non-Catalog Offerings:** A special insert profiling restricted, experimental, or black-market items unavailable through Galladinium's public storefront.
