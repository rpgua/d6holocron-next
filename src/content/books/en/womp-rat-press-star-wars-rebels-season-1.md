*Star Wars Rebels: Season One Sourcebook* is a fan-created supplement produced by Womp Rat Press for the *Star Wars* D6 Roleplaying Game (*Revised, Expanded, and Updated*). Bridging West End Games' classic mechanics with the modern animated series *Star Wars Rebels*, the book adapts the early rebellion period set roughly five years before the Battle of Yavin (5 BBY), when scattered insurgent cells were first beginning to resist Imperial rule in the Outer Rim.

Modeled after traditional WEG media sourcebooks, the volume translates the cast, locales, and hardware of the show's inaugural season into playable D6 game statistics. Gamemasters can use it either to run adventures directly alongside the Spectres cell on Lothal or to borrow early-Rebellion Imperial forces, local underworld figures, and starships for original campaigns set during the Dark Times.

## Key Contents

- **The *Ghost* Crew:** Full D6 character profiles, equipment, and Force abilities for Kanan Jarrus, Hera Syndulla, Ezra Bridger, Sabine Wren, Garazeb Orrelios, and Chopper (C1-10P).
- **Imperial Antagonists:** Game statistics for the Grand Inquisitor, Agent Kallus, Minister Maketh Tua, Commandant Aresko, and local Imperial garrison forces.
- **Lothal & Key Locations:** Setting notes and encounter hooks covering Capital City, Tarkintown, Imperial complexes, and surrounding Outer Rim sectors.
- **Starships & Vehicles:** Detailed D6 stat blocks for the modified VCX-100 light freighter *Ghost*, the *Phantom* auxiliary starfighter, the Inquisitor's TIE Advanced v1, Imperial Troop Transports, and AT-DP walkers.
- **Early Rebellion Gear & Allies:** Profiles for underworld figures such as Cikatro Vizago, Fulcrum contacts, specialized Mandalorian gear, and Ezra's hybrid lightsaber.
