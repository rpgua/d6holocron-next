*The Isis Coordinates* (1990, `WEG40036`) is a First Edition espionage and pursuit adventure for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Christopher Kubasik and published by West End Games. Set during the Galactic Civil War, the module revolves around **Isis**, a hidden Rebel safe world and secret starfighter manufacturing hub whose discovery would deal a crippling blow to the Alliance war effort.

Disaster looms when the Imperial Survey Corps vessel *The Wanderer* accidentally stumbles upon the system and begins scanning the planet. Although the Rebel player characters intercept the survey craft and disable its hyperdrive, the Imperials manage to secure proof of Isis's location and reach the surface. What begins as an urgent planetary chase through treacherous crystal canyons escalates into a desperate interception aboard a Corellian gunship—and ultimately a high-stakes infiltration inside the Imperial Star Destroyer *Desolator* to erase the planet's coordinates from the warship's main navigation computer before Imperial Command can act on them.

## Key Contents

- **Escalating Multi-Stage Pursuit:** Transitions seamlessly from orbital interception above Isis to a crash-landing and overland chase through hazardous crystal canyons, a gunship boarding action, and a Star Destroyer breakout.
- **The Hidden World of Isis:** Setting details for the Rebel Alliance's secret industrial safe world, its local inhabitants, and the environmental hazards of its crystalline terrain.
- **Imperial Survey Corps & Star Destroyer *Desolator*:** Profiles of the Imperial scout crew, Corellian gunship encounter details, and interior deck guidance for escaping the brig and reaching the *Desolator*'s navigation core.
- **Espionage & Sabotage Climax:** Mechanics and tactical options for slicing, altering, or wiping the navigation memory banks under intense Imperial pursuit.
- **Gamemaster Support:** Opening crawl text, scripted transitions, NPC statistics, and pacing notes tailored for cinematic Rebel espionage campaigns.
