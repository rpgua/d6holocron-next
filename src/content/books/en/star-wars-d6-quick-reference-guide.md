*Star Wars D6 Quick Reference Guide* is a condensed tabletop cheat sheet created by the Google+ Star Wars D6 community for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (REUP and Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Designed to serve either as a player handout or as a Gamemaster screen insert, it distills the core rules of the West End Games D6 engine into compact, easily scanned tables and step-by-step summaries.

Because classic *Star Wars D6* spreads combat modifiers, scale charts, wound effects, and starship maneuvers across hundreds of rulebook pages, a single-document reference dramatically speeds up play—especially for new players learning the Wild Die and multiple-action penalties. The guide focuses on the rules checked most frequently during active sessions, keeping momentum high during cinematic firefights and space battles.

## Key Contents

- **Core Dice & Difficulty Tables:** Summarizes attribute and skill rolls, pip progression (+1/+2), standard difficulty ranges (Very Easy through Heroic), and Wild Die outcomes.
- **Personal Combat Sequence:** Outlines initiative, action declarations, multiple-action penalties, range and cover modifiers, melee combat, and reaction skills (Dodge and Parry).
- **Damage & Wound Status:** Provides quick-lookup charts for Stunned, Wounded, Incapacitated, and Mortally Wounded thresholds, along with healing and recovery modifiers.
- **Scale & Starship Combat:** Condenses scale modifiers between character, speeder, walker, starfighter, and capital ship tiers, plus space movement, shields, and hyperspace travel rules.
- **The Force at a Glance:** Summarizes Control, Sense, and Alter usage, Force Points, Character Points, and Dark Side Point mechanics.
