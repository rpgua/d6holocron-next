*Adventurer's Journal Issue 6: The More Things Change* (product code `AJ111720`) is the sixth issue of the fan-produced *Adventurer's Journal* anthology for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (D6 / REUP), released in November 2020. Continuing the community project's revival of West End Games' *Star Wars Adventure Journal* format, the issue blends expanded combat mechanics, atmospheric adventures, and fringe-sector fiction.

A centerpiece of this issue is its dedicated D6 mechanical treatment of lightsaber combat forms, giving Force-using characters structured fighting styles compatible with Revised, Expanded, and Updated (REUP) rules. Alongside these mechanics, the magazine delivers both group and solitaire scenarios and expands the recurring Rotgut Ruffians storyline.

## Key Contents
- **Lightsaber Combat Mechanics:** *Lightsaber Forms* by Gavin Downing and Jeffrey Cook, adapting the classic seven forms of lightsaber combat into playable D6 / REUP rules and maneuvers.
- **Group Adventure:** *The Haunted Helm*, a full roleplaying scenario ready for tabletop play.
- **Solo Adventure:** *Lessons of the Past*, a solitaire adventure written by Mark Dowson for single-player D6 sessions.
- **Short Fiction & Fringe Lore:** *The Demon and the Toymaker* by Gavin Downing and *Rotgut Ruffians: Flight of the Herky Jerky* by Dustin Hawk, expanding the project's recurring frontier cast and vessels.
