*Heroes and Rogues* (1995, `WEG40086`) is an official West End Games character supplement for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition), written by Paul Sudlow and Rick D. Stuart. Its primary focus is expanding character creation, backstory depth, and campaign versatility across the Rebellion and New Republic eras, while also introducing formal guidance for playing characters aligned with the Galactic Empire.

Rather than limiting groups to the core rulebook's standard templates, *Heroes and Rogues* provides structured tools for building personal histories, defining homeworld origins, and populating campaigns with memorable allies and adversaries. It serves as both a player's handbook for customizing characters and a Gamemaster's resource for generating richly detailed supporting casts.

## Key Contents

- **Character Development (Chapter 1):** Free-form background generation systems covering family, education, former occupations, allies, and rivals, alongside a reference guide to notable homeworlds across the galaxy.
- **Roleplaying Imperials (Chapter 2):** Guidelines, campaign frameworks, and roleplaying advice for running Imperial military, intelligence, and administrative characters.
- **Independent Templates (Chapter 3):** New player character templates for scoundrels, mercenaries, bounty hunters, and other fringe archetypes.
- **Rebel Alliance and New Republic Templates (Chapter 4):** Specialized templates tailored for freedom fighters, military personnel, and covert operatives.
- **Heroes and Rogues Gallery (Chapter 5):** A large roster of ready-to-use Gamemaster Characters (GMCs) with full D6 statistics and adventure hooks.
