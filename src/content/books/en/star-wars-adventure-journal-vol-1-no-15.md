*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 15* (WEG41015), published by West End Games in November 1997, is the final officially printed issue of the *Star Wars Adventure Journal* before the company entered bankruptcy and lost the *Star Wars* RPG license in 1998. Built for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded*, it closes the magazine's print run with a themed "Scoundrels" adventure section, New Republic Jedi fiction, and Imperial intelligence dossiers.

Because of its late publication date and limited print circulation, Issue 15 became one of the most sought-after volumes in the West End Games D6 catalog.

## Key Contents
- **Headline Short Fiction:** Features Kevin J. Anderson's *"Firestorm"* (illustrated by Doug Shuler), set between *Jedi Search* and *Dark Apprentice*, following Tionne on Ossus and Luke Skywalker establishing the Jedi Praxeum on Yavin 4, alongside Patricia A. Jackson's *"Laughter After Dark"*.
- **Special Section — Scoundrels:** Bundles three playable RPG pieces: Daniel Wallace's *"The Great Herdship Heist"*, Tom Moldvay and Steve Miller's *"Tasariq: The Crystal Planet"*, and Angela Phillips's *"The Draw"*.
- **Alien Encounters:** Stephen Kenson's *"The Shard"* introduces D6 game statistics and lore for the crystalline, Force-sensitive silicate lifeforms capable of inhabiting droid bodies.
- **Imperial & Military Dossiers:** Includes Eric S. Trautmann's *"ISB Intercepts"* (in-universe Imperial Security Bureau dispatches) and the *"Special Military Unit Intelligence Update"* detailing specialized military units for D6 play.
