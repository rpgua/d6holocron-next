*Starfall* (1989, `WEG40016`) is a 40-page First Edition adventure module for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, published by West End Games. Set during the Galactic Civil War, it is widely regarded as one of the line's quintessential action-disaster scenarios, dropping the player characters *in media res* into a desperate fight for survival aboard a dying Imperial warship.

The adventure opens with the Rebel heroes locked inside the detention block of the *Victory I*-class Star Destroyer *Subjugator*, alongside a vital Alliance VIP: engineer Walex Blissex, the original designer of the *Victory*-class warship. When a Rebel fleet ambushes and severely cripples the *Subjugator*, the prisoners seize their chance to break free. To survive, they must escort Blissex across 900 meters of blackened, battle-torn corridors filled with zero-gravity pockets, malfunctioning droids, hull breaches, and desperate stormtroopers. Matters escalate when the ship's commander, Captain Kolaff, plots to detonate the crippled vessel to destroy the Rebel fleet, culminating in a tense hangar-bay battle against an AT-ST walker. *Starfall* was later updated for Second Edition in *Classic Adventures: Volume Five* (1998).

## Key Contents
- **Crippled Star Destroyer Setting:** Detailed deck-by-deck locations, environmental hazards, power outages, and zero-gravity rules aboard the *Subjugator*.
- **Key NPCs & Adversaries:** Complete First Edition D6 profiles for Alliance engineer Walex Blissex, Captain Kolaff, Imperial officers, stormtroopers, and rogue droids.
- **Full-Color Poster Map:** A large-scale tactical map of the *Victory*-class Star Destroyer's interior included with the original release.
- **Cardboard Counters & Aids:** Printable/punch-out character counters, player handouts, and encounter timelines tracking the ship's deteriorating systems.
- **Climactic Hangar Set Piece:** Tactical encounter guidelines for escaping the hangar bay under fire from an Imperial AT-ST walker and surviving troops.
