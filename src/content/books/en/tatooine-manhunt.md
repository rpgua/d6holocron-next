*Tatooine Manhunt* (1988, `WEG40005`) is one of the earliest official adventure modules published by West End Games for the First Edition of *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Written by Bill Slavicsek and Daniel Greenberg, the scenario is set during the Galactic Civil War and casts player characters as Rebel Alliance operatives racing against both Imperial forces and a pack of professional bounty hunters to locate **Adar Tallon**, a legendary Old Republic naval tactician long presumed dead.

Having faked his own death years earlier to escape Imperial rule, Tallon has been living quietly in the remote wastes of Tatooine. When Imperial intelligence uncovers his survival and dispatches the Star Destroyer *Relentless* to the system, rival bounty hunters converge on Mos Eisley hoping to claim the massive price on his head. The module uses a cinematic eight-episode structure that blends spaceport investigation, desert survival, underworld ambushes, and a final breakout past Imperial orbital forces.

## Key Contents

- **Eight-Episode Campaign Structure:** Guides the group from a lethal rendezvous on Kwenn Space Station (*The Mission Begins*) through Mos Eisley (*Welcome to Tatooine*, *Cantina Ambush*), the Dune Sea (*Blood on the Sand*, *Exploring the Wastes*, *Battle in the Desert*), and the climax at Tallon's hidden stronghold (*Slaughter at Tusken Fort*, *Escape from Tatooine*).
- **Rival Bounty Hunter Cast:** Full First Edition D6 statistics and tactics for memorable antagonists—including Jodo Kast, Zardra, Puggles Trodd, and other competing hunters—who actively pursue leads alongside the heroes.
- **Tatooine Setting & Encounters:** Detailed Mos Eisley contacts, moisture farm locations, desert hazards, Jawas, and Tusken Raiders for running urban and wilderness exploration.
- **Adar Tallon & Betrayal Plot:** Complete profile for Adar Tallon and his inner circle, culminating in the unmasking of the traitor Quist and a coordinated assault on the desert fort.
- **Gamemaster Aids:** Ready-to-use pullout maps, ship and vehicle stats, and encounter notes designed for introductory or mid-level Rebel groups.
