*Wretched Hives of Scum & Villainy* (1997, `WEG40153`) is a location sourcebook written by Paul Danner and published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Presented as an in-universe traveler's guide compiled by galactic drifter Cohden K'Reye, the book provides Gamemasters with eight fully realized cantinas, casinos, shadowports, and illicit gathering places that can be inserted into almost any era or sector.

Every establishment comes complete with architectural floorplans, menus and local customs, staff and regular patrons with full D6 game statistics, security measures, and multiple adventure hooks—making the book a practical drop-in resource for fringe, smuggling, espionage, and Rebel Alliance campaigns.

## Key Contents
- **High-Stakes & Neutral Grounds:** Features *The Ace of Sabres* (an opulent resort casino hiding cutthroat underworld deals) and *Exovar's Emporium* (a remote neutral sanctuary for smugglers, scouts, and bounty hunters far from Imperial reach).
- **Rough-and-Tumble Cantinas:** Details *The Broken Tusk* (a gritty bar built around a brutal dueling arena), *Fathoms*, and *Bantha Traxx*.
- **Fringe Hangouts & Deep-Space Hideouts:** Covers *The Pits* (a swoop-rider haven crowned by "The Hive"), *Glow Dome*, and *The Falling Star Saloon* aboard remote Starlyte Station—a place easy to vanish into, but hard to leave alive.
- **Maps, Patrons & Adventure Hooks:** Includes detailed venue maps, dozens of memorable NPCs, local rumors, and ready-to-use encounter seeds for each location.
- **Cantina Creation Appendix:** A dedicated Gamemaster toolkit with guidelines and tables for designing custom cantinas, shadowports, and criminal dens from scratch.
