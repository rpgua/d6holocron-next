*Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races (1st Edition)*, written by Troy Denning and published by West End Games in 1989, is the foundational species sourcebook for First Edition *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Set during the Rebellion era, the book is framed in-universe as excerpts from the *Catalog of Intelligent Life in the Galaxy*, an Imperial anthropological survey compiled by Senior Anthropologist Obo Rin at the personal command of Darth Vader.

Because the catalog is written from an Imperial perspective, its entries reflect Imperial propaganda and Humanocentric bias while supplying gamemasters and players with essential biological, cultural, and planetary lore for more than thirty sentient species. Many classic Star Wars species—including the Twi'leks, Rodians, Trandoshans, Duros, Bith, and Verpine—received their earliest comprehensive Expanded Universe histories in this volume.

## Key Contents

- **Over Thirty Sentient Species:** Entries covering iconic film aliens and game-original peoples, including Abyssins, Aqualish, Arcona, Bith, Duros, Hutts, Mon Calamari, Rodians, Trandoshans, Twi'leks, Verpine, and Whiphids.
- **Biology & Society:** Detailed notes on each species' physiology, native environment, social structure, technology level, and relations with the Galactic Empire.
- **Homeworld Overviews:** Concise planetary profiles describing native star systems, climates, and local hazards to support off-world encounters and character backstories.
- **First Edition D6 Statistics:** Attribute die-code ranges, typical skill packages, and special racial traits formatted for First Edition character creation and NPC design.
- **In-Universe Framing:** Official Imperial communiqués and field reports from Obo Rin that double as roleplaying handouts and adventure hooks.
