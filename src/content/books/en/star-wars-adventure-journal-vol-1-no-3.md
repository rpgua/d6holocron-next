*Star Wars Adventure Journal Vol. 1, No. 3* (WEG41003), published by West End Games in August 1994 and edited by Peter M. Schweighofer, is the third quarterly volume supporting *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Spanning the Rebellion and early New Republic eras, this issue is particularly notable for expanding the political and commercial landscape of the Expanded Universe with deep-dive articles on bacta production, mercenary operations, and the Imperial splinter faction known as the Pentastar Alignment.

Alongside its setting articles, the issue delivers full roleplaying scenarios, equipment statistics, and recurring in-universe columns tailored for immediate table use by D6 gamemasters.

## Key Contents
- **Setting & Faction Source Articles:** Features Anthony P. Russo's *"The Pentastar Alignment"* (detailing Grand Moff Ardus Kaine's breakaway Imperial faction) and Michael Kogge's *"The Business of Bacta"* covering Thyferra, the Vratix, and the galactic bacta trade.
- **Mercenary & Equipment Rules:** Includes *"Blasters for Hire"* by Anthony P. Russo for running mercenary campaigns, plus Philip Tobin's *"Outlaw Battle Armor"* with D6 game statistics for custom illicit armor.
- **Adventures & Short Fiction:** Contains the scenarios *"Mission to Zila"* by Charlene Newcomb and *"Changing the Odds"* by Dave Marron, alongside Patricia A. Jackson's short story *"When the Domino Falls"*.
- **Recurring Columns:** Presents Paul Sudlow's *"Galaxywide NewsNets"* (in-universe news dispatches and plot hooks), Peter M. Schweighofer's *"Smuggler's Log"* (a tour of a bulk freighter), and *"Wanted by Cracken"*.
- **Creator Interview:** *"Drawing A Daily Diet of Star Wars Comics"* by Ilene Rosenberg interviews veteran *Star Wars* comic strip artist Al Williamson.
