Published in August 1996 by West End Games, *The Star Wars Roleplaying Game: Second Edition, Revised and Expanded* (WEG40120) is the 288-page hardcover culmination of the classic D6 System line. Written by Bill Smith, Peter M. Schweighofer, George R. Strayton, Paul Sudlow, Eric S. Trautmann, and Greg Farshtey, this edition significantly expanded the 1992 Second Edition rulebook by folding in years of rules clarifications, supplemental mechanics, and setting material from across the Rebellion and New Republic eras.

Often regarded as the definitive official rulebook of the West End Games era, 2E-RE features full-color interior sections, reorganized chapters, and streamlined reference tables designed for both newcomers and veteran gamemasters. It retains the Wild Die and unified scale mechanics of Second Edition while substantially broadening the catalog of playable alien species, Force powers, starships, equipment, and campaign-building guidance.

## Key Contents

- **Complete D6 System Rules:** Refined rules for character creation, specializations, the Wild Die, Character Points, Force Points, and unified scale modifiers spanning character, speeder, walker, starfighter, and capital ship tiers.
- **Expanded Combat & Spaceflight:** Detailed personal combat, chases, vehicle operations, hyperspace travel, starship combat, and damage repair systems.
- **Comprehensive Force Chapter:** An extensive library of *Control, Sense,* and *Alter* powers—including combined powers, lightsaber combat, and Dark Side mechanics—consolidated from earlier supplements.
- **Templates & Alien Species:** A wide roster of updated character templates alongside playable alien species profiles and guidelines for designing custom characters and droids.
- **Equipment, Ships & Gamemastering:** Extensive catalogs of weapons, armor, droids, vehicles, and iconic starships, paired with in-depth advice on adventure structure, campaign pacing, and episodic Star Wars storytelling.
