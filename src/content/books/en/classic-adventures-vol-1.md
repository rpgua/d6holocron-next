*Classic Adventures* (1995, `WEG40083`) is the inaugural Second Edition anthology volume published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Designed to bring out-of-print First Edition modules up to date with Second Edition D6 rules, statistics, and formatting, the compilation pairs two contrasting standalone softcover books from 1992 into a single volume covering both the classic Rebellion and early New Republic eras.

Together, the two reprinted modules offer gamemasters a traditional Rebel Alliance rescue saga alongside an episodic underworld campaign tailored for independent smugglers and fringers operating after the Battle of Endor.

## Key Contents

- ***The Abduction of Crying Dawn Singer***: Originally published as `WEG40052` (by Chuck Truett) and set between *A New Hope* and *The Empire Strikes Back*, this full-length Rebel Alliance scenario tasks players with tracking down and rescuing the kidnapped Ithorian leader Crying Dawn Singer under Imperial rule.
- ***The Politics of Contraband***: Originally published as `WEG40066` (by Paul Arden Lidberg, Brian J. Murphy, William Olmesdahl, and Eric S. Trautman) and set in the early New Republic era, this six-episode smuggler campaign follows a light freighter crew navigating rival crime syndicates, New Republic officials, and Imperial remnants across Najarka, Laim, and Narg.
- **Second Edition Rules Updates**: Revised skill difficulties, starship combat scales, character templates, and NPC stat blocks converted for seamless use with *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*.
- **Maps & Gamemaster Aids**: Reproduces location layouts, handouts, and adventure scripts from both original modules in an updated layout.
