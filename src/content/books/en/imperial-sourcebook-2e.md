Published by West End Games (WEG 40092) as a Second Edition revision of Greg Gorden's landmark 1989 guide, the *Imperial Sourcebook (2nd Edition)* updates the definitive portrait of the Galactic Empire for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Retaining the acclaimed in-universe briefing format and twelve-chapter structure of the original book, this edition recalibrates all game statistics, skill listings, scale modifiers, and mechanical references to match the 1992 Second Edition ruleset.

Alongside refreshed interior trade dress and editorial cleanups, the revised edition preserves the foundational lore that defined Imperial military doctrine, intelligence operations, and bureaucratic control during the Galactic Civil War. It serves as an essential core reference for gamemasters running Second Edition campaigns against Imperial forces.

## Key Contents

- **Updated Second Edition Rules:** Revised D6 statistics, combat scales, and mechanical profiles aligned with *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game Second Edition*, including Wild Die-era formatting.
- **COMPNOR and Imperial Intelligence:** Full coverage of the Commission for the Preservation of the New Order, the Imperial Security Bureau (ISB), CompForce, and Imperial Intelligence operations.
- **Imperial Army, Navy, and Sector Groups:** Detailed Orders of Battle, command chains, and fleet deployment doctrine explaining how the Empire occupies and pacifies star systems.
- **Capital Ships, Walkers, and Artillery:** Updated Second Edition stats for *Imperial*-class Star Destroyers, *Interdictor* cruisers, support craft, AT-AT and AT-ST walkers, repulsorlift armor, and heavy artillery.
- **Stormtroopers and Ordnance:** Profiles for elite Stormtrooper divisions, special military forces, infantry support weapons, custom ordnance, and Imperial recruitment and indoctrination programs.
