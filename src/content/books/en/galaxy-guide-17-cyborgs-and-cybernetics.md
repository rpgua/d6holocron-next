*Galaxy Guide 17: Cyborgs & Cybernetics* is a community-created netbook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Because West End Games never released a dedicated sourcebook on cybernetic augmentation—limiting official coverage to brief sections in books like *Cracken's Rebel Field Guide*—this fan supplement compiles comprehensive rules, gear catalogs, and setting flavor for prosthetics and cyborgs in the *Star Wars* galaxy.

Intended for both players seeking medical or combat augmentations and Gamemasters introducing cybernetic gangs, black-market surgeons, or heavily augmented adversaries, the netbook covers everything from simple bionic replacements to full-body conversions.

## Key Contents

- **Cybernetics & Prosthetics Catalog:** Detailed rules, costs, and game statistics organized by anatomical location—covering fingers, hands, arms, feet, legs, eyes, ears, head implants, torso mods, synthetic organs, and miscellaneous gear—along with underworld cybernetic slang.
- **Cybernetic Species:** D6 profile and background for the Iskalloni, a heavily cybernetically augmented species.
- **Seven Character Templates:** Ready-to-play archetypes including the Cyber Doc, Cyber Gang Member, Cyborged Alien, Cyborged Bounty Hunter, Cyborged Pirate, Ripper Doc, and Ripper Gang Member.
- **Notable Cyborg NPCs:** Fifteen detailed character write-ups spanning canon and expanded lore, including Lobot, High Inquisitor Tremayne, Daye Azur-Jamin, and original cyborg adversaries, plus a blank cyborg character sheet.
