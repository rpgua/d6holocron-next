*Death Star Technical Companion* (1991, `WEG40008`) is an official West End Games sourcebook for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*, written by Bill Slavicsek. Originally released for First Edition in 1991 and later revised for Second Edition in 1993, the book is framed as Rebel Alliance Intelligence technical documentation assembled after the Battle of Yavin to analyze the design, capabilities, and internal layout of the first Death Star.

Designed as both an encyclopedic reference and a practical adventure setting, the companion breaks down the moon-sized battle station sector by sector. It gives gamemasters the architectural details, operational procedures, and security protocols needed to run infiltration, sabotage, escape, or reconnaissance missions aboard the Empire's ultimate weapon—or aboard similar massive Imperial installations.

## Key Contents

- **History and Technical Specifications:** Development timeline under Grand Moff Tarkin alongside breakdowns of structural layers, hypermatter power generation, propulsion, deflector shields, computer networks, and the planet-destroying superlaser.
- **Surface Geography and Hangar Bays:** Detailed examination of the city sprawls, equatorial trench, turbolaser batteries, thermal exhaust ports, and massive docking bays servicing TIE squadrons, shuttles, and capital ships.
- **General, Service, and Technical Sectors:** Coverage of crew quarters, mess halls, life support, automated droid control centers, maintenance shafts, and waste-processing trash compactors.
- **Military, Security, and Command Sectors:** Floor plans and operational profiles of Stormtrooper and Imperial Navy garrisons, Detention Block AA-23 and interrogation wings, Grand Moff Tarkin's command suite, the central conference room, and primary bridge systems.
- **Gamemaster Appendices:** D6 statistics, encounter guidelines, and rules for incorporating the Death Star's systems and personnel into active campaigns.
