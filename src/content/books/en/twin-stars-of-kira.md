*The Twin Stars of Kira* (1993, `WEG40060`) is a 96-page sector sourcebook and adventure anthology published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition). Edited by Paul Murphy with contributions from multiple writers, the supplement is set during the early New Republic era following the fall of the Galactic Empire and focuses on the Kira Run—a bustling frontier trade and smuggling corridor in the Expansion Region linking the Harrin Trade Corridor and the Enarc Run.

Combining regional gazetteer material with seven playable scenarios and mini-settings, the book is tailored for independent tramp-freighter crews, New Republic troubleshooters, and fringe scouts operating in a politically fractured sector where Imperial holdouts, mercenaries, and alien syndicates compete for influence.

## Key Contents
- **Sector Overview & Starmap:** An introduction by Stephen Crane detailing astrogation logs, hyperspace hazards, and a full sector map charting systems along the Kira Run including Kira, Roldalna, Nigel, Lazerian, Ropagi, and Enarc.
- **Planetary Mini-Settings:** In-depth profiles with adventure hooks for *Lazerian IV* (a pro-Imperial monarchy featuring ancient Jedi ruins, Force-sensitive Empeth crystals, and the aquatic Akwin) and *Ropagi II* (a bustling mercenary market home to the philosophical Ropagu and their Kalduu symbiotes).
- **Five Full Mini-Adventures:** Ready-to-run scenarios spanning diverse tones: *The Package* (spice smuggling on Roldalna and Nigel VI), *Den of Spies*, *For a Few Kilotons More*, *Treasure Hunt*, and *Operation: Pet Show*.
- **Regional Threats & Factions:** Introduces new species, ships, and recurring adversaries—including the aggressive cybernetic Iskallons—giving Gamemasters a self-contained sandbox for New Republic-era campaigns.
