*Classic Adventures: Volume Two* (1995, `WEG40128`) is a 128-page Second Edition anthology published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Continuing the line's effort to preserve and modernize sought-after First Edition releases, this volume updates two full-length Rebellion-era adventure modules by Bill Slavicsek and Jim Bambra to Second Edition D6 rules and stat blocks.

The collection pairs a spacefaring espionage thriller set among the shattered remains of Alderaan with a survival-horror scenario rooted in the dark side of the Force, providing wide tonal variety for Rebel Alliance campaigns.

## Key Contents

- ***Graveyard of Alderaan*** (by Bill Slavicsek): Beginning with a supply run to the Ithorian herdship *Bazaar*, Rebel agents are drawn into a murder investigation and a race through the Alderaan asteroid field—known as the Graveyard—to locate rumored remnants of the Royal Palace and a lost Alderaanian warship before rival underworld figures and Darth Vader's Imperial agents can seize them.
- ***Domain of Evil*** (by Jim Bambra): Stranded on the hostile swamp world of Trinta, the player characters must survive treacherous terrain and deadly local fauna while being stalked by the ruthless Taloron hunter Crutag and confronting physical manifestations of their own nightmares conjured by the dark side.
- **Updated 2E Statistics**: Fully converted Second Edition profiles for NPCs, Imperial forces, creatures, starships, and Force-related hazards.
- **Adventure Resources**: Retains original deck plans, planetary maps, introductory scripts, and gamemaster advice formatted for Second Edition play.
