Published by West End Games in April 1996 and written by Paul Sudlow, *The Jedi Academy Sourcebook* adapts Kevin J. Anderson's *Jedi Academy Trilogy* (*Jedi Search*, *Dark Apprentice*, and *Champions of the Force*) for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Set in 11 ABY during the early New Republic era, the volume charts Luke Skywalker's efforts to rebuild the Jedi Order at the Great Temple on Yavin 4 while the fledgling government faces renewed threats from hidden Imperial superweapons.

Designed as a campaign reference for both Force-sensitive and non-Jedi player groups, the book documents the political landscape of Coruscant, the spice mines of Kessel, and the secret Maw Installation hidden inside a black hole cluster. It provides gamemasters with full D6 statistics for the new generation of Jedi trainees, Imperial holdouts, and exotic technology introduced in the novels.

## Key Contents

- **The New Republic & Coruscant**: Profiles of key political and military figures, New Republic institutions, and locations across the galactic capital.
- **The Jedi Academy & Echoes of the Sith**: Detailed coverage of Luke Skywalker's Yavin 4 praxeum, first-class students (such as Kyp Durron, Cilghal, Streen, and Kirana Ti), and the dark side presence of Exar Kun.
- **Forces of the Empire & Maw Installation**: Game statistics and lore for Admiral Daala, her Star Destroyer fleet, Qwi Xux, and superweapons including the Sun Crusher and the Death Star prototype.
- **Kessel & The Fringe**: Setting guides for Kessel's spice mines, Moruth Doole's administration, smugglers, and independent factions.
- **Planets, Creatures & Hardware**: Dedicated chapters detailing featured worlds, indigenous creatures, starships, ground vehicles, droids, and specialized equipment.
