*Otherspace II: Invasion* (1989, `WEG40028`) is a First Edition adventure module published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* and the direct sequel to *Otherspace*. Continuing one of the classic D6 line's most unusual story arcs, it blends Galactic Civil War action with cosmic horror as an extragalactic threat breaches realspace.

Dispatched on a reconnaissance mission to Stronghold—a remote Rebel Alliance safe-world in the Taldot sector—the player characters arrive to find the hidden base devastated. The attackers are the Charon, a fanatical, world-destroying species from the extra-dimensional void of Otherspace who have broken through into the known galaxy. Caught between the invading Charon forces, Imperial Moff Ravik's ambitions, and the unstable Charon warlord Ber'asco (trapped in the body of Bane Nothos), the heroes must stop the invasion before it spreads beyond Stronghold.

## Key Contents

- **The Fall of Stronghold:** A complete multi-episode adventure investigating the ruined Alliance safe-world and mounting a desperate defense against the Charon incursion.
- **The Charon Threat:** First Edition D6 statistics, lore, and bio-technological equipment for the extra-dimensional Charon warriors, death-cultists, and their dreadnought remnants.
- **Returning Antagonists:** Profiles and plot threads for Ber'asco, Bane Nothos, and Imperial Moff Ravik linking directly to the events of *Otherspace*.
- **Pullout Play Aids:** Includes an introductory adventure script, tactical maps of the Stronghold Alliance base, and ready-to-use NPC templates.
