*Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley*, written by Martin Wixted and published by West End Games in 1993, was the first volume in the *Galaxy Guide* series created specifically for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition*. Set during the Rebellion era, it transforms Tatooine's infamous frontier spaceport into a fully realized urban campaign setting packed with competing criminal syndicates, corrupt Imperial officials, smugglers, bounty hunters, and desperate locals.

Rather than focusing on a single linear storyline, the book functions as a street-level gazetteer and sandbox reference, allowing gamemasters to run heist, smuggling, investigation, or survival scenarios anywhere in the city.

## Key Contents

- **Tatooine & Mos Eisley Overview:** Background on Tatooine's harsh environment and history, followed by an examination of Mos Eisley's urban layout, local economy, Imperial garrison, Tatooine Militia, and underworld politics.
- **Iconic & Original Locations:** Detailed write-ups for over twenty establishments, including Docking Bay 94, Chalmun's Cantina, Spaceport Customs, the Mos Eisley Spaceport Control Tower, Jabba's Townhouse, and additional docking bays (86, 87, and 92).
- **Shops, Inns & Services:** Street-level businesses such as Lup's General Store, Spaceport Speeders, Kayson's Weapons Shop, Dewback Stables, Zygian's Banking Concern, the Lucky Despot Hotel, Mos Eisley Inn, Dockside Cafe, and Jawa traders.
- **Denizens & NPCs:** Complete Second Edition statistics, motivations, and secrets for dozens of spaceport regulars, merchants, informants, law enforcers, and criminals.
- **Adventure Ideas:** A dedicated gamemaster chapter offering plot seeds, encounter hooks, and scenario outlines tailored to Mos Eisley's factions and locales.
