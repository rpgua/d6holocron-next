*Corellian Engineering Corporation: Capital Ships* is a fan-made starship catalog published by Womp Rat Press—the community collective best known for producing the *Star Wars D6 REUP* (Revised, Expanded, and Updated) core rulebook—and archived in the Google+ Star Wars D6 community collection. Whereas Corellian Engineering Corporation (CEC) is most famous among players for its modular YT-series light freighters, this supplement focuses specifically on the shipyard's capital-scale designs.

Formatted in the classic West End Games sourcebook style with clean, REUP-compatible stat blocks, the book serves as a dedicated fleet reference for Gamemasters and players running naval, Rebel Alliance, New Republic, or corporate security campaigns. It gathers canon and Legends CEC capital warships, corvettes, gunships, frigates, and heavy industrial hulls into one standardized catalog, making it easy to populate sector fleets or supply Rebel cells with versatile Corellian vessels.

## Key Contents

- **Standardized REUP Stat Blocks:** Provides complete D6 game statistics for each vessel, including craft type, scale, length, crew, passenger and cargo capacity, hyperdrive ratings, maneuverability, hull, shields, and weapon batteries.
- **Corvettes, Gunships & Frigates:** Covers iconic CEC escort and patrol lines—such as the CR90 Corvette, DP20 Corellian Gunship, and related military variants—alongside lesser-known capital designs.
- **Heavy Transports & Fleet Support Hulls:** Details large-scale Corellian bulk freighters, command ships, and modular industrial vessels adapted for wartime service.
- **Lore & Operational Notes:** Includes background history on Corellian shipyard design philosophy, modularity, and common field modifications used by Rebel, Imperial, and independent operators.
