*Solo: A Star Wars Story — An Open D6 Role-Playing Game* is a fan-made sourcebook and game guide distributed through The Rancor Pit community that adapts the characters, criminal syndicates, starships, and underworld settings of *Solo: A Star Wars Story* (2018) to the OpenD6 System. Built on the classic West End Games dice-pool framework (attributes and skills rated in D6 dice codes plus the Wild Die), it translates the film's heist-driven scoundrel era—set roughly a decade before *A New Hope*—into playable tabletop material.

Designed for campaigns centered on smuggling runs, double-crosses, and survival under early Imperial rule, the supplement gives Gamemasters and players everything needed to run fringe crews operating between corporate shipyards, war-torn Imperial fronts, and syndicate strongholds.

## Key Contents

- **Scoundrels & Archetypes**: OpenD6 character profiles and playable templates inspired by the film's cast, including young Han Solo, Chewbacca, Lando Calrissian, Qi'ra, Tobias Beckett's crew, L3-37, and Dryden Vos.
- **Criminal Syndicates**: Setting overviews and game statistics for underworld powers active during the early Empire, including Crimson Dawn, the Pyke Syndicate, Corellia's White Worms, and Enfys Nest's Cloud-Riders.
- **Planetary Settings**: Gazetteer descriptions and adventure hooks for Corellia, the mud-soaked battlefields of Mimban, the frozen mountain world of Vandor, the spice mines of Kessel, and the barren outpost of Savareen.
- **Starships, Vehicles & Gear**: OpenD6 stats for the pre-modification *Millennium Falcon* (with escape pod), Imperial *Y-45* armored transport haulers, Corellian landspeeders, coaxium transport rules, and Corellian Spike Sabacc mechanics.
