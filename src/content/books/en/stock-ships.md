*Stock Ships* (1997, `WEG40150`) is a 64-page starship sourcebook published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Written by Pablo Hidalgo, Steve Miller, Timothy S. O'Brien, Paul Sudlow, and Eric S. Trautmann, the supplement provides players and Gamemasters with an array of civilian light freighters, medium transports, and pleasure craft suited for smugglers, independent traders, scouts, and Rebel couriers.

Designed to expand starship choices beyond the ubiquitous Corellian YT-1300 (which famously appears on the cover art but is intentionally omitted from the interior roster), every vessel entry pairs factory-standard D6 game statistics and manufacturer lore with detailed interior deckplans and a fully statted customized variant ready for immediate campaign use.

## Key Contents
- **Fifteen Civilian Starships:** Complete profiles for 15 factory-spec vessels across multiple shipwrights, including the Corellian YT-2400 Transport, XS-800 Light Freighter, and HT-2200 Medium Freighter.
- **Diverse Manufacturers:** Features distinctive designs from lesser-seen yards, such as the Mon Calamari *DeepWater*-class Light Freighter, *Starwind*-class Pleasure Yacht, Lantillian Short Hauler, Hyrotil *Crescent*-class Transport, and Suwantek Systems TL-1800.
- **Additional Fringe Freighters:** Includes the Kazellis Light Freighter, Nova-Drive 3-Z Freighter, Rendilli-Surron Starlight Freighter, Starfeld Z-10 Seeker and ZH-25 Questor, *Darloz*-class Medium Freighter, and Surronian L19 Light Freighter.
- **Deckplans & Modified Variants:** Each ship features room-by-room floorplans for tactical boarding actions alongside a named, upgraded example vessel complete with crew hooks and aftermarket modifications.
