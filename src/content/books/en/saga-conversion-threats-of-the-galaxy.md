*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Threats of the Galaxy* is a fan-compiled conversion supplement from The Rancor Pit community adapting Wizards of the Coast's *Threats of the Galaxy* (2008) from *Star Wars Saga Edition* into West End Games' D6 System (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Functioning as a ready-to-use D6 adversary roster and bestiary companion to the original sourcebook, it translates dozens of NPC archetypes, alien creatures, and combat droids into classic D6 attribute dice, skill codes, and damage ratings.

Spanning multiple eras of the *Star Wars* galaxy, the conversion gives D6 Gamemasters a broad stock of drop-in allies, villains, wildlife, and mechanical opponents that require no on-the-fly d20-to-D6 math during play.

## Key Contents

- **Underworld & Fringe Archetypes**: D6 stat blocks for bounty hunters, smugglers, slicers, swoop gang members and leaders, thugs, and hired assassins.
- **Military & Law Enforcement**: Ready-to-run profiles for Imperial stormtroopers, Imperial officers, Navy troopers, Rebel soldiers, elite commandos, and planetary security personnel.
- **Force Users & Civilians**: Converted templates for Dark Side adepts, fallen Jedi, rogue Force-wielders, Jedi initiates and masters, as well as everyday civilians such as traders, medics, scientists, and politicians.
- **Galactic Bestiary**: D6 game statistics, natural weapon damage, and pack-tactic notes for alien predators, wild beasts, and semi-sapient monsters including creatures such as Dathomiri rancors and Mantellian savrips.
- **Droids**: Converted profiles for utility, protocol, astromech, labor, security, and assassin droids usable as companions or hostile encounters.
