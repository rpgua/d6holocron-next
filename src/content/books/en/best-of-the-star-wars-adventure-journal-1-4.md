*The Best of the Star Wars Adventure Journal, Issues 1–4* (1996, `WEG40129`) is a Second Edition collector's anthology edited by Peter M. Schweighofer and published by West End Games for *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game*. Compiled to bring out-of-print highlights from the first four 1994 issues of the *Star Wars Adventure Journal* back into circulation, the volume blends short fiction, campaign source material, and playable adventure content spanning the Rebellion and New Republic eras.

In addition to reprinting foundational Expanded Universe stories and game articles, the book adds editorial commentary, behind-the-scenes author essays, and a color art showcase restoring illustrations originally printed in black and white.

## Key Contents

- **Featured Short Fiction & Essays**: Reprints Timothy Zahn's *"First Contact"* and Kathy Tyers's *"Tinian on Trial"* with new author essays, alongside *"A Glimmer of Hope"* by Charlene Newcomb and *"The Final Exit"* by Patricia A. Jackson.
- **Playable Adventures**: Includes *"The Void Terror"* by Peter Schweighofer and *"The Spira Regatta"* by Paul Sudlow, ready to drop into ongoing D6 campaigns.
- **Source Articles & Setting Guides**: Features *"A Free-Trader's Guide to Sevarcos"* by Anthony Paul Russo, *"Rebel Privateers!"* by Timothy S. O'Brien, and *"Recon & Report: The Journey to Coruscant"* by Peter Schweighofer.
- **Editorial & Retrospective Features**: Opens with *"Admiral's Communiqué"* and includes Ilene Rosenberg's *"From Heir to Last Command"* examining the Thrawn Trilogy's RPG adaptations.
- **Artist Spotlight**: A dedicated full-color gallery showcasing the work of *Adventure Journal* illustrator Chris Gossett.
