*Rancor Pit Saga Conversion: Rebellion Era Campaign Guide* is a fan-compiled conversion supplement created by The Rancor Pit community to adapt Wizards of the Coast's *Rebellion Era Campaign Guide* (2009) from the *Star Wars Saga Edition* d20 ruleset to West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (Second Edition, Revised and Expanded). Intended as a mechanical companion to the original sourcebook, it translates d20 statistics, traits, and equipment into D6 dice codes, skill ratings, and scale rules so Gamemasters can use *Saga Edition* Galactic Civil War material directly at a D6 table.

The conversion spans the classic Rebellion Era—covering the period from *A New Hope* through *Return of the Jedi* alongside Legends Expanded Universe material—and provides ready-to-use D6 game statistics for Rebel cells, Imperial forces, and Outer Rim underworld operators.

## Key Contents

- **Character Options & Species Traits**: D6 adaptations of era-specific character backgrounds, specialized skills, and species-specific advantages for classic galactic species such as Cereans, Duros, and Rodians.
- **Force Traditions**: Converted Force powers and traditions active during the Galactic Civil War, including independent groups such as the Kilian Rangers.
- **Equipment & Starships**: Full D6 game statistics for era weaponry, combat assault vehicles, swoops, space transports (including the Mobquet Medium Transport, YKL-37R Nova Courier, and YV-545 Light Freighter), and starfighters such as the Kimogila, Krayt Gunship, Kihraxz, and Dunelizard.
- **Factions & Personalities**: Converted NPC stat blocks for Rebel Alliance cell leaders and operatives, Imperial military and COMPNOR personnel, stormtrooper specialists, and fringe figures tied to Black Sun and the Hutt cartels.
