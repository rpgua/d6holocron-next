*Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters (1st Edition)*, written by Mark Rein-Hagen and Stewart Wieck and published by West End Games in 1990, broadened *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* beyond Rebel Alliance military operations. Set during the Galactic Civil War, it provides the first complete framework for campaigns centered on independent free-traders, smugglers, and scoundrels struggling to keep their light freighters flying and out of debt.

Combining economic rules with an original sandbox setting, the book introduced the **Minos Cluster**—a quiet Outer Rim backwater linked to the rest of the galaxy by a single monthly space liner and patrolled by an aging *Victory*-class Star Destroyer, *The Chariot*.

## Key Contents

- **Cargo & Trade Economics:** First Edition rules for drop-point deliveries, speculative commodity trading, black-market smuggling, operating expenses, and Imperial Customs inspections.
- **Loan Sharks & Ship Debt:** Campaign mechanics for financing a tramp freighter, tracking interest payments, and dealing with ruthless creditors and local syndicates.
- **Ship Modifications & Repairs:** Guidelines for customizing light freighters with upgraded hyperdrives, shields, hidden smuggling compartments, and illicit weapon mounts, plus rules for jury-rigging and maintenance.
- **The Minos Cluster Gazetteer:** Regional overview of a secluded Outer Rim sector, including Imperial bureaucracy, the Minos-Mestra Corporation, local worlds (such as Adarlon, Mestra, Gesaril, and the Rebel hideout Haven), NPCs, and regional ships.
- **The Minos Campaign:** A multi-part adventure arc designed to launch an independent freighter crew in the Minos Cluster, supported by quick-reference trade charts and spacer templates.
